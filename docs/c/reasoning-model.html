What is Reasoning model?

A model trained to think step by step before answering, spending more compute on harder questions.

Like I am 5

A student who works the problem out on rough paper before writing the final answer.

Evolved from: Chain of thought, Reinforcement learning, Large language model (LLM)

Led to: Effort controls

In history

  • 2024-09-12: OpenAI o1: models that think before answering. Trained with reinforcement learning to reason in a long hidden chain of thought.
  • 2025-01-20: DeepSeek-R1: open reasoning model. Reasoning close to o1, with open weights and an MIT licence.
  • 2025-02-24: Claude 3.7 Sonnet and Claude Code. A hybrid model with optional extended thinking, plus an agent that codes in your terminal.
  • 2025-03-25: Gemini 2.5: Google's thinking model. Gemini 2.5 Pro reasons before answering and leads many leaderboards.
  • 2025-04-29: Qwen3 open models. Alibaba releases open models from 0.6B to 235B with switchable thinking.
  • 2025-08-05: OpenAI releases open-weight gpt-oss. OpenAI's first open-weight language models since GPT-2.
  • 2025-08-07: GPT-5. One system that decides when to answer fast and when to think longer.
