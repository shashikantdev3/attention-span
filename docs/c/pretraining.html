Concept, Training and alignment

What is Pre-training?

The first, most expensive training stage where a model learns general knowledge from huge unlabelled data.

Like I am 5

Going to school for many years to learn a bit of everything before choosing a job.

Evolved from: Self-supervised learning, Transfer learning

Led to: Fine-tuning, Large language model (LLM)

In history

  • 2018-02-15: ELMo: word meaning depends on context. The same word gets a different vector depending on the sentence around it.
  • 2018-06-11: GPT-1: generative pre-training. Pre-train a transformer on lots of text, then fine-tune it for each task.
  • 2018-10-11: BERT reads in both directions. A transformer trained to fill in masked words tops most language benchmarks.
  • 2019-10-23: T5: every task as text to text. Translation, summarising and classification all become 'text in, text out'.
  • 2026-05-19: Andrej Karpathy joins Anthropic. OpenAI co-founder and former Tesla AI director joins Anthropic's pretraining team.
