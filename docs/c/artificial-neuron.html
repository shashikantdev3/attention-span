Concept, Math and neurons

What is Artificial neuron?

A unit that multiplies its inputs by weights, adds them up and passes the result through a simple function.

Like I am 5

Like a tiny judge that listens to several friends, trusts some more than others, and shouts yes if the total is big enough.

Evolved from: none in this glossary yet

Led to: Activation function, Hopfield network, Perceptron

In history

  • 1943-12: McCulloch and Pitts model the first artificial neuron. A neuron described as simple logic: inputs add up, and it fires past a threshold.
