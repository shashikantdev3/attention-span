What is GPU?

A processor with thousands of small cores, built for graphics and ideal for the matrix maths of neural networks.

Like I am 5

Thousands of small helpers doing easy sums at the same time, instead of one genius doing them one by one.

Led to: Deep learning framework, Hyperscaler, Rack-scale architecture

In history

  • 2009-06: GPUs make large neural networks practical. Graphics cards train networks up to 70 times faster than CPUs.
  • 2012-12: AlexNet wins ImageNet by a wide margin. A deep convolutional network trained on two GPUs cuts the ImageNet error rate sharply.
