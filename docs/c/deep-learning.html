Concept, Learning methods

What is Deep learning?

Machine learning with neural networks of many layers, each learning more abstract features.

Like I am 5

A tower of judges: the bottom ones spot lines, the middle ones spot shapes, the top ones spot faces.

Evolved from: Backpropagation, Machine learning

Led to: Pruning, Transfer learning

In history

  • 2006-07: Deep belief nets revive deep learning. Training deep networks one layer at a time finally works.
  • 2011-12-29: Google Brain's network learns to spot cats from YouTube. A huge network trained on unlabelled video frames learns a 'cat detector' on its own.
  • 2012-12: AlexNet wins ImageNet by a wide margin. A deep convolutional network trained on two GPUs cuts the ImageNet error rate sharply.
  • 2013-12-19: Deep Q-networks learn Atari games from pixels. One network learns to play several Atari games from the screen and the score alone.
  • 2016-01-27: AlphaGo masters Go. Neural networks plus tree search beat a Go professional, then world champion Lee Sedol in March.
  • 2021-07-15: AlphaFold 2 predicts protein structures. Protein shapes that took years in the lab are predicted in minutes.
  • 2024-10-09: Nobel Prize in Chemistry for protein design and AlphaFold. David Baker for protein design; Demis Hassabis and John Jumper for structure prediction.
