What is Recurrent neural network (RNN)?

A network that reads a sequence one step at a time, carrying a memory forward.

Like I am 5

Reading a story one word at a time and keeping a running note in your head.

Evolved from: Backpropagation

Led to: Attention, LSTM, State space model

In history

  • 1997-11: Long short-term memory (LSTM). A recurrent network with gates that decide what to remember and what to forget.
  • 2014-09-01: Attention is introduced for translation. Instead of squeezing a sentence into one vector, the model looks back at the words it needs.
  • 2014-09-10: Sequence to sequence learning. One LSTM reads a sentence, another writes the translation.
