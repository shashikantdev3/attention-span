What is Mixture of experts (MoE)?

Also called MoE

A model split into many expert sub-networks with a router that sends each token to only a few of them.

Like I am 5

A hospital where a receptionist sends you to the right two doctors instead of seeing all of them.

Evolved from: Transformer

Led to: none in this glossary yet

In history

  • 2017-01-23: Sparsely-gated mixture of experts. A giant model where each input only wakes up a few expert sub-networks.
  • 2023-12-11: Mixtral brings mixture of experts to open weights. 47B parameters in total, but only about 13B used per token.
  • 2024-12-26: DeepSeek-V3 trains a frontier-class model cheaply. A 671B mixture-of-experts model with a reported training cost of about 5.6 million dollars.
  • 2025-04-29: Qwen3 open models. Alibaba releases open models from 0.6B to 235B with switchable thinking.
