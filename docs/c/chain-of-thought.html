What is Chain of thought?

Also called CoT

Getting a model to write out intermediate reasoning steps before its answer.

Like I am 5

Showing your working in a maths test, which helps you get the right answer.

Evolved from: Prompt engineering

Led to: ReAct pattern, Reasoning model, Test-time compute

In history

  • 2022-01-28: Chain-of-thought prompting. Showing the model worked steps makes it reason better.
  • 2022-04-05: PaLM scales to 540 billion parameters. A very large Google model shows breakthrough reasoning with chain-of-thought.
  • 2024-09-12: OpenAI o1: models that think before answering. Trained with reinforcement learning to reason in a long hidden chain of thought.
