What is Machine learning?

Getting computers to improve at a task from data or experience, instead of being explicitly programmed.

Like I am 5

Instead of writing the rules, you show lots of examples and the computer works out the rules itself.

Evolved from: Symbolic AI

Led to: Deep learning, Reinforcement learning, Supervised learning, Unsupervised learning

In history

  • 1959-07: Arthur Samuel's checkers program learns to play. A program improves at checkers by playing itself, and the phrase 'machine learning' enters use.
