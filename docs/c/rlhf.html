Concept, Training and alignment

What is RLHF?

Also called reinforcement learning from human feedback

Reinforcement learning from human feedback: humans rank outputs, a reward model learns their taste, and the LLM is tuned to score well.

Like I am 5

People pick which of two answers is better, and the model learns to give the kind people pick.

Evolved from: Reinforcement learning, Instruction tuning

Led to: Constitutional AI, Direct preference optimisation (DPO)

In history

  • 2017-06-12: Learning from human preferences. Humans compare two behaviours, and the agent learns what they prefer.
  • 2022-03-04: InstructGPT: following instructions with RLHF. A small model tuned on human feedback is preferred over a 100 times bigger raw model.
  • 2022-11-30: ChatGPT launches. A free chat interface to a GPT-3.5 model becomes the fastest growing consumer app of its time.
  • 2022-12-15: Constitutional AI. A model critiques and revises its own answers using a written set of principles.
  • 2023-05-29: Direct preference optimisation (DPO). Learn from human preferences without a separate reward model or RL loop.
