What is Fine-tuning?

Continuing training of a pre-trained model on a smaller, specific dataset.

Evolved from: Pre-training, Transfer learning

Led to: Instruction tuning, LoRA

In history

  • 2018-06-11: GPT-1: generative pre-training. Pre-train a transformer on lots of text, then fine-tune it for each task.
  • 2018-10-11: BERT reads in both directions. A transformer trained to fill in masked words tops most language benchmarks.
  • 2021-06-17: LoRA: cheap fine-tuning. Freeze the big model and train tiny add-on matrices instead.
  • 2023-05-23: QLoRA: fine-tune a 65B model on one GPU. Quantise the frozen model to 4 bits, then LoRA-tune it cheaply.
