What is Open-weight model?

A model whose trained weights are published so anyone can download, run and fine-tune it. Not always fully open source.

In history

  • 2022-08-22: Stable Diffusion is released openly. A high-quality image model anyone can run on a home GPU.
  • 2023-02-24: LLaMA starts the open-weight wave. Meta releases research models whose 13B version beats GPT-3 on many benchmarks.
  • 2023-07-18: Llama 2 allows commercial use. Open weights with a licence that allows most businesses to build on them.
  • 2023-09-27: Mistral 7B. A 7-billion-parameter model under Apache 2.0 that beats larger Llama models.
  • 2023-12-11: Mixtral brings mixture of experts to open weights. 47B parameters in total, but only about 13B used per token.
  • 2024-04-18: Llama 3. Meta's 8B and 70B open models trained on over 15 trillion tokens.
  • 2024-12-26: DeepSeek-V3 trains a frontier-class model cheaply. A 671B mixture-of-experts model with a reported training cost of about 5.6 million dollars.
  • 2025-01-20: DeepSeek-R1: open reasoning model. Reasoning close to o1, with open weights and an MIT licence.
  • 2025-04-29: Qwen3 open models. Alibaba releases open models from 0.6B to 235B with switchable thinking.
  • 2025-08-05: OpenAI releases open-weight gpt-oss. OpenAI's first open-weight language models since GPT-2.
