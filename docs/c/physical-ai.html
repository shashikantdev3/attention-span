What is Physical AI?

Also called embodied AI

AI that senses and acts in the physical world, such as robots, drones and self-driving systems, rather than only working with text and images on a screen.

Like I am 5

AI with a body: it can look around a room and move things in it, not just chat.

Evolved from: World model, Multimodal model
