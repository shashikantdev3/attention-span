AI news for Wednesday, 25 November 2026

6 stories, each written from its sources and fact-checked.

  1. OpenAI posts a model-written preprint claiming the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4

    A new manuscript in OpenAI's public math repository claims to settle how fast matrices can be multiplied, with part of the result checked in Lean.

  2. Dell's quarterly revenue doubles to $54.9 billion and its AI server backlog reaches a record $132.3 billion

    Dell raised its full-year forecast for the third time, to $202 billion, after booking $60.2 billion of AI server orders in the quarter.

  3. Claude can now install artifacts as Mac and Windows apps with their own icons and windows

    Anthropic's 'Install as app' beta turns a Claude artifact into a desktop app that can ask for notifications, shortcuts or even full computer access.

  4. Three xAI co-founders launch Epochal, with $100 million and a 'Time Portal' of the past web for training forecasting agents

    Epochal says its archive of more than 1 trillion timestamped pages lets AI agents browse the web as it looked on any date in the last 30 years.

  5. Cloudflare launches Search for Agents, a one-toggle search API that lets AI agents query a website instead of crawling it

    Turning it on builds an AI Search index for a site and advertises a search endpoint on every page that agents can call, on all plans including Free.

  6. Google DeepMind launches Humanity's First Exam, a Kaggle benchmark of everyday questions that tests whether models answer reliably every time

    The benchmark has 544 simple text and image prompts across 20 basic skills, plus a three-week Kaggle contest to collect questions models still get wrong.
