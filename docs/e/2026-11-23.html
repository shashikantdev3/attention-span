  1. Nvidia's board reverses course and reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, ten days after his resignation

    Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring Jensen Huang back as president and CEO, saying senior US officials gave input and it sees no national security risk.

  2. CrowdStrike agrees to buy Zscaler in a $43.9 billion all-stock deal pitched around securing AI agents

    CrowdStrike will acquire Zscaler for about $43.9 billion in stock, combining endpoint and zero-trust network security under the banner of making AI safe to deploy.

  3. Amazon confirms it is buying Decart's AI optimization technology and team; Bloomberg puts the price at about $7.5 billion

    Amazon announced it will buy the technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack and hire Decart's founders, confirming last week's reports of a deal.

  4. Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX, X and xAI over sexualized Grok images, the first state lawsuits over the chatbot

    The two attorneys general filed coordinated consumer protection suits alleging the companies let Grok create non-consensual and child sexual images while marketing it as safe.

  5. Sierra raises $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation, doubling its value in about six months

    Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor's customer-service agent company closed a Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed.

  6. Z.ai releases GLM-5.4, its first flagship model built as a looped transformer

    GLM-5.4 reruns 28 middle layers of the GLM-5 base twice per pass, and Z.ai says it reaches the level of 2 to 3 trillion parameter models, though it published no benchmark scores.

  7. Google lets Search's AI Mode read your Google Wallet, with Drive and Gemini app connections coming next

    Google is expanding Personal Intelligence in AI Mode beyond Gmail, Calendar and Photos, starting with read-only access to Wallet.

  8. Gemini API adds file slicing so developers pay only for the pages, cells or clips a model reads

    Google DeepMind's new server-side controls let the Gemini API load selected page ranges, spreadsheet cells or media segments instead of whole files.
