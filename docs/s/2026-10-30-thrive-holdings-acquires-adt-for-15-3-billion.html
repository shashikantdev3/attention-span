Thrive Holdings Acquires ADT for $15.3 Billion

OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings is buying physical security company ADT in a $15.3 billion deal to build a smart home security business.

Thrive Holdings acquired physical security company ADT for $10 per share or about $7.6 billion in equity. The total enterprise value of the deal is $15.3 billion. Thrive Holdings is a holding company spinout from Josh Kushner’s VC firm Thrive Capital. It counts OpenAI as its partner and investor. Thrive Holdings says it holds over 75 companies. Its mission is to hold companies long-term and revitalize them with AI. OpenAI supplies AI models, technology, and forward-deployed engineers to Thrive companies. ADT will form the foundation of Thrive Holdings' smart home security business, and the companies plan to invest and release new products.
