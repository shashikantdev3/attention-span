Block launches Dessa, a speech-to-speech AI model trained only on human conversation

Jack Dorsey's Block announced Dessa, a new speech-to-speech AI model trained entirely from human conversation.

Jack Dorsey's Block has announced a new AI model called Dessa. The model is trained from scratch on human conversation. Block shared examples of chats, including a guessing game, a discussion about Christopher Nolan films, and a conversation with OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 voice model. According to the Dessa team, the model is a true speech-to-speech model that can run locally on a MacBook Pro M4 Max or better. The team built Dessa for curiosity, safety, and better understanding between humans and AI. Block will give early access to a small group of AI safety researchers.

Like I am 5

Block made a computer program that learns to talk by listening to people talk, just like a baby learns to speak without reading books first.

Why it matters

Building true speech-to-speech models that run locally and process human emotion directly can change how voice interfaces are designed.
