VulnCheck report: Project Glasswing findings outpace fixes after seven months

VulnCheck says Anthropic's Project Glasswing has 37,966 findings but only 217 marked fixed, and just 2 of 442 tracked CVEs were exploited.

VulnCheck's Patrick Garrity reports on Project Glasswing seven months in. He has tracked 442 CVEs attributable to Anthropic, about 0.5% of the 93,450 CVEs published this year. Only 2 were exploited in the wild. Anthropic's ledger lists 37,966 findings, with 7,349 (19.4%) made public and 217 marked fixed. About 1,654 are duplicates and 419 are withdrawn. Since September, nearly 12,000 findings were added and 15 more fixes recorded. Anthropic's claimed true positive rate rose from 90.8% to 94.2%, which VulnCheck questions.
