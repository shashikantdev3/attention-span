Former Microsoft Research AI Frontiers leader says group ended after reorg into Microsoft AI

A former AI Frontiers leader writes that the roughly 50-person Microsoft Research group is over after a September reorg into Microsoft AI, and that he has left.

In a personal blog post, a founding leader of Microsoft Research's AI Frontiers group says it is over. The group had about 50 people and worked on agents, such as the Autogen orchestration framework and the Fara computer use agent, and on new model capabilities. In early September it was reorged into Microsoft AI. The author says the scaling-only philosophy of Microsoft AI leadership was incompatible with research. He left, and says every other founding leadership team member reached a similar conclusion. He also says there will be no internships with the group in the coming year.
