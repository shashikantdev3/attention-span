Anthropic Introduces Self-Assessment Technique for Misuse Detection

Anthropic introduced self-assessment, a technique where Claude provides a preliminary safety assessment of each conversation right after it responds.

Anthropic introduced a technique called self-assessment, which lets Claude provide a preliminary safety assessment of each conversation right after responding. On Claude Fable 5, self-assessment flags 78% of potentially harmful conversations and 94% of high-priority ones at a 1% false-positive rate. The approach is efficient because the conversation is already in the KV cache, adding less than 2% to inference compute. Since June, it has revealed large-scale misuse and served as a layer in Anthropic's defense-in-depth strategy. It runs in every Claude model released since Fable 5 across a large fraction of global traffic.

Like I am 5

Claude checks its own work after talking to make sure it did not say anything bad. It is like a student writing a quick note at the end of an essay to grade its own safety.

Why it matters

Monitoring AI safety becomes cheaper and more scalable when models assess their own conversations directly from the cache.
