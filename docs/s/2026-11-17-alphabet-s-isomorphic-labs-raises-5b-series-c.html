Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs Raises $5B Series C

Alphabet's Isomorphic Labs raised a $5 billion Series C round led by Sequoia and Greenoaks to accelerate clinical trials.

Alphabet Inc.'s Isomorphic Labs raised a $5 billion Series C funding round to accelerate clinical trials for its artificial intelligence drug design work. Sequoia and Greenoaks led the financing. This follows earlier fundraising rounds of $2.1 billion in May and $600 million in March 2025. The company designs new drugs using artificial intelligence.
