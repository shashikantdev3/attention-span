How a 2023 Blackstone loan to CoreWeave started a boom in chip-backed debt

A 2023 private credit loan led by Blackstone to CoreWeave became a blueprint for chip-backed debt.

In 2023, a Blackstone-led $2.3B private credit loan was made to CoreWeave Inc. Blackstone executives asked CoreWeave leadership what a GPU is and if AI is real. This loan became a blueprint for chip-backed debt. Chip-backed debt is now on track to become a $1T market, according to Bloomberg.
