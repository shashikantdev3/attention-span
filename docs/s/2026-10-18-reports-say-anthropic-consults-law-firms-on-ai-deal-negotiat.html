Reports Say Anthropic Consults Law Firms on AI Deal Negotiation

Anthropic is consulting law firms to develop fair negotiation norms as it builds AI agents to handle deals for users.

Anthropic is consulting with law firms to develop fair negotiation norms for AI agents. These agents would haggle over prices, resolve complaints, or improve contract offers on behalf of users. Experiments show that stronger models negotiate better deals, which could widen societal inequality. To improve Claude in areas lacking verifiable data, Anthropic is hiring Conceptual Reasoning Fellows. These lawyers and humanities experts will spend four months testing Claude on judgment-heavy questions for $100,000 to $167,000. Justin Curl, a researcher at Anthropic, notes that in law it is notoriously hard to define quality.
