Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2, tops Artificial Analysis index at 65

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2, which ranks first on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 65 and costs 20% less per token than Fable 5.1.

Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 as its IPO roadshow began. Fable 5.2 scores 65 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ahead of Gemini 4 Carbon at 60 and Opus 5.5 at 59. Price is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50. Cost per task rose anyway. SemiAnalysis paid $88.15 per task on GDPx, up from $67.16. Run logs show 5.2 sometimes wrote hundreds or thousands of test cases and used subagents to check its work. On FrontierCode 2.0, Anthropic says Fable at High matched Opus 5.5 at Max for about half the cost per task.

Like I am 5

Imagine a student who gets a cheaper price for each pencil but checks their homework again and again, so they use many more pencils. They get the best grade in class, but the bill can still go up.

Why it matters

A lower per-token price does not mean a lower bill, so teams should test cost per task and pick the effort setting carefully before moving to production.
