Report: Sam Altman Pitches Three AI Safety Approaches

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly pitched three internal scenarios for advanced AI governance to his employees.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman reportedly outlined three different approaches for governing advanced AI during an internal presentation. The first approach is an international watchdog modeled after the International Atomic Energy Agency. The second approach involves major labs merging or operating under a single entity modeled after CERN. The third approach envisions a single company accumulating enough economic power through self-improving AI to withhold compute from competitors. OpenAI plans to choose a path within the next year while balancing safety with the need to keep pace with rivals.
