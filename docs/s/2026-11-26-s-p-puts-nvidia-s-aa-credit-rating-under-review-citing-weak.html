S&P puts Nvidia's AA credit rating under review, citing weak oversight and 'key man' risk after the Huang ouster and return

A one-notch cut to AA- would keep Nvidia investment grade but could make the loans it guarantees for neoclouds more expensive.

S&P Global Ratings is weighing a downgrade of Nvidia's credit rating over management and governance concerns, TechCrunch reports. In its statement, S&P said the removal and reinstatement of CEO Jensen Huang pointed to weak executive oversight, a lack of succession planning and key man risk. The agency expects to finish its review within 90 days and could lower Nvidia from AA to AA-. Nvidia would remain investment grade, but TechCrunch notes that a lower rating could raise its borrowing costs. That matters beyond Nvidia because the company backstops debt for neoclouds and other infrastructure firms with weaker credit. In August Nvidia said it would arrange $500 billion of AI infrastructure financing with six institutions, guaranteeing 25% of the loans, and Bloomberg has reported that lenders already wanted bigger guarantees.
