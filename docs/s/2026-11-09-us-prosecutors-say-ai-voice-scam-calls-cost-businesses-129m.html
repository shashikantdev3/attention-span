US prosecutors say AI voice scam calls cost businesses $129M+

US federal prosecutors say scam calls using AI-generated voices to mimic company executives cost businesses $129M+ in the 18 months since July 2024.

US federal prosecutors say businesses have lost $129M+ to scam calls that use AI-generated voices to mimic company executives. The losses cover the 18 months since July 2024. According to the New York Times, a federal indictment alleges that three men used generative A.I. to impersonate the voices of chief executives and lawyers. The indictment says they used these voices to defraud companies of millions of dollars. These are allegations from prosecutors. The sources do not say how the calls were made, which companies were hit, or how the case will be decided.

Like I am 5

Imagine someone copies your teacher's voice on the phone and tells you to hand over your lunch money. A computer can now copy voices like that, and some people are using it to trick companies.

Why it matters

Voice alone is no longer proof of who is calling, so teams building voice or payment tools need other ways to verify identity.
