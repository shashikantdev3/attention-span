FBI Warns of Artex AI Hacking Tool Used in Breaches

The FBI warns that hackers are using the open-source Artex AI tool to breach networks across multiple industries.

The FBI issued a cybersecurity advisory warning that hackers are using open-source AI tools like Artex to launch cyberattacks globally. Artex can connect to hosted frontier large language models and scan networks for weaknesses, exploiting simple security bugs. In some cases, Artex-linked attacks move from targeting to active compromise in under three hours. The tool has been used in attacks against South Korean financial institutions, American IT company ConnectWise, and cybersecurity company Barracuda. Although the developer made the software closed-source after these attacks, the open-source version remains widely accessible.
