Anthropic's S-1 shows where its compute money goes: Google TPUs first, then SpaceX, Amazon and Microsoft

Anthropic's IPO filing lists more than $750 billion in computing deals, and Google's TPUs get the largest share.

GeekWire went through the computing deals in Anthropic's IPO filing, which add up to more than $750 billion in firm commitments and estimated future spending. About $400 billion is for capacity on Google-designed TPUs: $114.5 billion to Google directly through July 2033, $161.2 billion in equipment leases with Broadcom (the largest single arrangement), and an estimated $125 billion to $150 billion more from 2028 to 2031. SpaceX's AI subsidiary, formerly xAI, is next with up to $120.5 billion through 2029. Anthropic committed at least $110 billion to AWS through April 2036 and at least $44.9 billion to Microsoft Azure through May 2033, plus about $44.6 billion with Nscale and $41.3 billion with Lambda. The deals with Microsoft, Google and Amazon must be paid regardless of actual usage.
