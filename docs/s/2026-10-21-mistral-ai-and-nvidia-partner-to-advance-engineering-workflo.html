Mistral AI and NVIDIA Partner to Advance Engineering Workflows

Mistral and NVIDIA are collaborating to integrate AI models with accelerated computing for engineering simulations and design workflows.

Mistral and NVIDIA are partnering to revolutionize engineering workflows by combining Mistral AI models with NVIDIA accelerated computing and physics modeling tools. The collaboration aims to enable faster simulations and closed-loop AI-driven design across aerospace, automotive, and semiconductor industries. The companies will introduce public benchmarks and open datasets to standardize evaluations for physics and agentic AI in computer-aided engineering. They are also building on their ongoing work, including the development of Mistral Large 4 and participation in the NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition.
