Microsoft Equips Over 330,000 Volkswagen Group Employees with Copilot

Microsoft announced that Volkswagen Group has equipped more than 330,000 employees with Microsoft Copilot.

Microsoft announced that Volkswagen Group has equipped more than 330,000 employees with Microsoft Copilot to increase productivity, accelerate decisions, and improve knowledge-based processes. The deployment follows a phased approach that began with Copilot Chat, which saw over 300,000 monthly active users. Employees now use Copilot with Work IQ for advanced productivity across engineering, production, procurement, and sales. Over 26,000 agents have been active across the Group over the past month. Microsoft and Volkswagen also intend to scale the Intelligent Data Foundation on Microsoft Fabric.
