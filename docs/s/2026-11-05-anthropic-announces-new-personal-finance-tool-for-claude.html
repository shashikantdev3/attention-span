Claude can now connect to your bank and card accounts (read-only) to analyze spending

Anthropic launched a new Personal Finance tool for Claude, letting users connect bank and credit card accounts through Plaid.

Anthropic announced a new feature for Claude called Personal Finance. The tool lets users connect their bank and credit card accounts through Plaid. Users can then ask Claude questions about their balances, transactions, and spending categories. Anthropic states that the tool has read-only access and cannot move, manage, or invest money. Conversations in the Personal Finance hub are not used to train AI models. The feature also includes the ability to flag duplicate charges, explain financial terms like APR, and calculate interest paid over the past year. It is rolling out in beta for U.S. Pro, Max, and Ultra users.

Why it matters

Integrating financial account data directly into chatbots creates new privacy and security requirements for products handling sensitive consumer information.
