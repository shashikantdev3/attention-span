SEC opens a civil probe into whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors, WSJ reports

The Securities and Exchange Commission is investigating Nvidia after Jensen Huang's resignation, examining in part whether the company's disclosures misled investors, The Wall Street Journal reported.

Citing people familiar with the matter, The Wall Street Journal reported that the SEC has opened a civil investigation into Nvidia, looking in part at whether its disclosures misled investors. Neither the SEC nor Nvidia had confirmed the inquiry. Nvidia shares had fallen more than 10% since Huang's exit. Separately, California Attorney General Rob Bonta told Bloomberg Television his office was looking into aspects of the scandal, saying red flags had been raised around the possible diversion of chips to China. Nvidia paid $5.5 million in 2022 to settle earlier SEC charges over its disclosures about cryptomining.
