OpenAI strengthens safeguards against internal reasoning extraction

OpenAI tightened safeguards to prevent attackers from extracting internal reasoning from its latest AI models.

OpenAI has tightened safeguards against extracting internal reasoning from GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. In internal automated tests, attacks pulling protected reasoning from prior requests dropped from a 54.0 percent success rate on GPT-6 Astra to 0.2 percent on GPT-6.2 Astra. Attacks on active requests fell from 22.9 to 0.9 percent. The defenses combine targeted training, input and output filters for messages and tool calls, and API blocks. Against coordinated campaigns, OpenAI bans thousands to tens of thousands of accounts weekly. Anthropic is also fighting systematic distillation attacks by banning accounts.

Why it matters
