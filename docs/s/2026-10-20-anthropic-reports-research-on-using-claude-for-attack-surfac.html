Anthropic Reports Research on Using Claude for Attack Surface Reduction

Anthropic shared initial research into using Claude to analyze and reduce the attack surface of codebases.

Anthropic published initial research into automated attack surface analysis and reduction, using Claude to discover issues across large open-source codebases. The team ran Claude over 25 projects in various languages, representing over five million lines of code. Claude discovered 493 issues using three methods, which included default configuration, configuration interaction, and early reachability. The research aims to show that Claude can improve software security through these methods.
