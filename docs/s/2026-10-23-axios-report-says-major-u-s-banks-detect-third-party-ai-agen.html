Banks say customers are letting unvetted AI agents into their accounts

An Axios report states major U.S. banks are detecting unauthorized activity from third-party AI agents operating within online customer accounts.

Major U.S. banks are detecting unauthorized activity from third-party AI agents operating within online customer accounts, according to an Axios report. Financial institutions say customers have given unvetted personal AI agents access to their accounts by sharing usernames and passwords. White House AI czar David Sacks responded that most of this activity appears to be user-authorized and is not a cyberattack. Chase, JPMorgan Chase, Charles Schwab, and Morgan Stanley commented on the matter, with Schwab stating it blocks unauthorized agents and Morgan Stanley observing no such activity.
