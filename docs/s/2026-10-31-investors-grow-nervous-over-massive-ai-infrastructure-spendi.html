Big Tech's $740B AI spending plans are starting to worry investors

Big Tech companies are spending hundreds of billions on AI infrastructure, causing negative free cash flow and growing investor anxiety.

Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft outlined plans to spend more than $740 billion on capital expenditures this year for AI infrastructure like data centers and chips. While investors previously rewarded these outlays, rising costs are now causing negative free cash flow, mounting debt, and market drops. Alphabet reported negative free cash flow of $9.1 billion in the third quarter as its AI features ate into search ad growth. Amazon reported negative free cash flow of $42.4 billion over the past 12 months. Meta reported negative free cash flow of $2.1 billion. Microsoft was the exception with positive free cash flow of $18.6 billion.
