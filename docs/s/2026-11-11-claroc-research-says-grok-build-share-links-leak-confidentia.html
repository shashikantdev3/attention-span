Claroc research says Grok Build share links leak confidential data, including SpaceXSI's own

Security firm Claroc says Grok Build share links let anyone enumerate thousands of live projects and see sensitive data, including from 24 SpaceXSI apps.

Research from security firm Claroc shows that Grok Build, a vibe-coding agent from SpaceXSI, is leaking data from thousands of its users. The leak comes through links that let users share their apps. Grok randomizes the URLs so only people who know the exact URL can reach the data. A design decision by the platform's developers defeats this and lets anyone enumerate thousands of live projects. Except in rare cases, clicking a link opens the app and shows all its data. Exposed data includes internal business plans, ad proposals, medical exam results and employee names and ID numbers. Claroc found 24 apps built by SpaceXSI itself, including a confidential onboarding plan and a sales territory map of 594 accounts.
