China's CSI 300 falls for a record 12th straight week, with about $3.5 trillion wiped out since May

China's CSI 300 index completed its 12th consecutive weekly decline, wiping out Rs 329 lakh crore amid weak demand and AI sell-offs.

China's benchmark CSI 300 index recorded its 12th consecutive weekly decline on October 30. This marks its longest losing streak since the index was launched in 2005. The prolonged sell-off wiped out nearly USD 3.5 trillion, or approximately Rs 329 lakh crore, in market value since May. The index has dropped more than 20 percent from its June peak, entering bear market territory. Weak domestic consumption, disappointing corporate earnings, property sector troubles, and heavy selling pressure in technology and artificial intelligence stocks contributed to the crash.
