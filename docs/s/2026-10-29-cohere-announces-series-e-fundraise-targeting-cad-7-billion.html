Cohere Announces Series E Fundraise Targeting CAD $7 Billion

Cohere announced the initial close of its Series E funding round targeting up to CAD $7 billion.

Cohere announced the initial close of its Series E funding round targeting up to CAD $7 billion, which would represent a CAD $35.7 billion valuation. The round marks the largest private financing round ever undertaken by a Canadian technology company. Schwarz Group is the lead investor. New institutional and strategic investors include Bell Ventures, Bullhound Capital, and the Government of Canada, while Inovia Capital, NVIDIA, and Prosperity7 Ventures are returning investors. Cohere will use the funding to advance its modelling and product roadmap, develop fully verticalized AI solutions for regulated industries, and invest in compute infrastructure.
