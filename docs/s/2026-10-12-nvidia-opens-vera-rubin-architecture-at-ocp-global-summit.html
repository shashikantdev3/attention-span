NVIDIA Opens Vera Rubin Architecture at OCP Global Summit

NVIDIA announced new partnerships and open architecture contributions at the OCP Global Summit to help scale AI factories worldwide.

At the Open Compute Project Global Summit in San Jose, NVIDIA announced new partnerships and contributions to create a common foundation for next-generation AI infrastructure. NVIDIA contributed key Vera Rubin rack and compute tray designs through the open NVIDIA MGX architecture. Partners and NVIDIA announced several advancements, including open standards for next-generation AI factories and grid resilience, new open source software components for the NVIDIA DSX AI Factory, and Meta and NVIDIA networking innovations. Additional contributions included Visionbay's 800V DC AI factory design, a new Ultra-Dense Small Form Factor Pluggable standard with Cisco, and Falcon Transport support with Google.
