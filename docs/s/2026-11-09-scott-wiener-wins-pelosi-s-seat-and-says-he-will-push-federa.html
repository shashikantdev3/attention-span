Scott Wiener wins Pelosi's seat and says he will push federal AI regulation

Scott Wiener, who wrote California's SB 1047 and SB 53, won Nancy Pelosi's open House seat and says Congress must act on AI risk.

Scott Wiener, a California state senator, won a tight race for Nancy Pelosi's open San Francisco congressional seat. He authored SB 1047 in 2024, which Governor Gavin Newsom vetoed, and sponsored SB 53, which Newsom signed. Both bills have inspired proposed federal legislation. Wiener wants a strong federal regulator, whistleblower protections, testing and auditing requirements, and a kill switch requirement. He opposes broad federal preemption of state AI laws. His campaign received over $3 million from Public First Action, which receives substantial funding from Anthropic. He calls himself only "cautiously optimistic" that Congress can rein in AI.
