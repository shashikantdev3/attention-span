OpenAI says hobbyists using Codex and Astra found new exoplanet candidates in old Kepler and TESS data

Two amateur astronomers flagged planet candidates, two of which a SETI Institute astronomer independently found in Kepler data.

OpenAI published an account of two amateur astronomers, one an OpenAI engineer, who used Codex and its Astra model to search public telescope data for planet signals. One searched about 100,000 Kepler stars, flagging more than 200 signals that included 10 known planets and two new candidates, one of which could be about 2.4 times Earth's diameter if a planet causes it. A SETI Institute astronomer ran an independent search and found the same two Kepler signals, and called the initial checks encouraging. The other searched data on about 19 million stars, mainly from TESS, and produced 49 first-pass candidates, sending one to TESS mission researchers. OpenAI stresses these are candidates, not confirmed planets, and confirmation could take months or years.
