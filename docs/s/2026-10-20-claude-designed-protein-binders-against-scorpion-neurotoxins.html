Claude Designed Protein Binders Against Scorpion Neurotoxins

An internal research model at Anthropic designed protein minibinders against scorpion neurotoxins with human guidance.

Working with Citrini Research, Anthropic tasked an internal research model with designing minibinders against scorpion neurotoxins. Over sixty four hours, the model operated mostly autonomously while receiving four follow-up prompts. It generated 318,484 protein designs and selected 180 for testing, while the team added 31 more. Contract research organizations Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience lab-tested all 211 designs. Seventeen of the designs met the criteria for a binder against scorpion neurotoxins. Anthropic released all 211 designs and their data as a resource.
