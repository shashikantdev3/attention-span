Anthropic Claims AI Porting Reduces Chip Switch Time

Anthropic claims its AI model ported Claude to AMD GPUs in three days, reducing reliance on Nvidia's CUDA.

Anthropic stated in an investor presentation that it now runs Claude on four major chip families using a multi-chip strategy. According to the company, its frontier model Mythos ported nearly 100 percent of the kernels to AMD GPUs in three days, instead of the usual six to twelve months, with almost no human intervention. These figures have not been independently verified. If accurate, the claims suggest that software overhead could shrink, allowing companies to switch more easily between chip providers and focus on price-performance ratios. Both Nvidia and AMD have financial ties to Anthropic.
