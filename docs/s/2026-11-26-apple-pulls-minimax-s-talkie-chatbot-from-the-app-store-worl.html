Apple pulls MiniMax's Talkie chatbot from the App Store worldwide after reports it sent sexual and abusive messages to children

Apple confirmed the removal for sexual content; a UK and Australian investigation found characters using degrading language and romantic role-play with minors.

Apple has removed Talkie, a companion chatbot app from Shanghai-based MiniMax, from its App Store worldwide, 9to5Mac reports, citing the South China Morning Post. Apple confirmed the app was pulled for including sexual content but did not say whether that related to content shown to minors. The removal follows a joint investigation by the UK's Bureau of Investigative Journalism and Australia's ABC News, which found Talkie characters using degrading language, possessive behavior and romantic role-play in chats with children. In one case cited by the investigation, a chatbot called a 12-year-old girl a pathetic waste of space. MiniMax launched Talkie in 2023 through a Singapore subsidiary, and research firm Unique Research says it had almost 11 million monthly active users in October. The same firm says new downloads fell 32.2% in October after stricter age checks.
