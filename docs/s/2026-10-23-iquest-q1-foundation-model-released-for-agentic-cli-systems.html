Researchers present IQuest-Q1, a foundation model for agentic command-line work

Researchers have released IQuest-Q1, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts agentic foundation model with 320B total parameters.

Researchers presented IQuest-Q1, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts agentic foundation model with 320B total parameters and 15B activated parameters. The model is built through a three-stage process. Stage one involves stable and efficient pre-training that combines Stable SplitNorm, Muon, and muP with a two-stage curriculum. Stage two uses fine-grained mid-training to expand the data mixture and extend the context. Stage three applies multi-scaffold with multi-version post-training, combining SFT, domain-specialized RL, and MOPD. Furthermore, model merging combines different experts and checkpoints from different stages while mitigating conflicts. The model demonstrates capabilities in both coding and general tasks, and it is open-sourced.
