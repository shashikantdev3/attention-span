Google Workers Tell Employer Not to Punish Schools Rejecting AI

Hundreds of Google workers are pushing leadership to retract a policy blocking schools that turn off Gemini AI from accessing Google Search.

Hundreds of workers at Google are pushing senior leadership to retract a policy. The policy blocks schools that turn off its Gemini AI tools from accessing the company search engine starting next year. Google announced the plan to address teachers and parents regarding AI in the classroom. Communities across the country protested Google software in schools. Three of the largest school districts are now discussing alternative search engines. Employees wrote a petition through the Alphabet Workers Union asking leadership to respect user concerns. Corporate leadership acknowledged the letter but has not responded to specific demands.
