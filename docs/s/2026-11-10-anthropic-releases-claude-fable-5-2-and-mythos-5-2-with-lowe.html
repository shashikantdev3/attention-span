Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2: up to 30% faster than 5.1, with an optional fast mode

Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, models that generate output up to 30% faster than 5.1 and cost $8 per million input tokens.

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, which it says improve coding, reasoning and vision. They are the same underlying model. Fable has cybersecurity and biology safeguards and is open to all users. Mythos is available to organizations through verification programs. Output is up to 30% faster than 5.1, at $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. An optional fast mode speeds generation up to 2.5 times, at $16 and $80. On FrontierCode, Fable 5.2 scores 49.1% at Xhigh effort. Both models are on Amazon Bedrock.
