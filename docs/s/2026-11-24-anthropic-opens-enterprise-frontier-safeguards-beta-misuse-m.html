Anthropic opens Enterprise Frontier Safeguards beta: misuse monitoring with zero data retention

Business customers keep Claude prompts and replies in their own cloud storage while Anthropic's software scans for misuse patterns across requests.

Anthropic has moved Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) into public beta, a system meant to catch sophisticated misuse while staying compatible with zero data retention. Beyond per-request classifiers, EFS looks for misuse patterns across many requests, since attacks can be split into harmless-looking pieces. Customers bring their own Amazon S3 or Google Cloud Storage, and Anthropic says there is no human review of that data and it is never used for training. Flagged risks open a Safety Case for the customer to review, and TNW reports customers must review cases within seven days or risk losing access. Anthropic says it built EFS with more than 100 customers, including Bridgewater, Salesforce and Stripe, and that it runs on Claude Platform, Amazon Bedrock and Google Cloud, with Microsoft Foundry coming.
