Dropbox buys AI storage startup Tigris Data for $350M

Dropbox plans to acquire cloud technology company Tigris Data for $350 million to support its AI infrastructure development.

Dropbox announced the acquisition of Tigris Data for $350 million. The deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026. This acquisition aims to support the development of DBX Agent Cloud, a storage platform for businesses and developers building AI models and agents. Tigris Data provides cloud storage technology that positions data closer to users and computing resources to improve performance. Dropbox stated it has no plans to reduce headcount as part of this deal. The company previously acquired other AI-focused firms including Reclaim.ai, Promoted.ai, and Mobius Labs.
