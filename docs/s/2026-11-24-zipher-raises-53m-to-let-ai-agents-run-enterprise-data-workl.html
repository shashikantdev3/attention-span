Zipher raises $53M to let AI agents run enterprise data workloads

The New York and Tel Aviv startup's agents set compute, frameworks and schedules for data pipelines against cost and SLA targets.

Zipher Technologies has raised $53 million across seed and Series A rounds from TLV Partners, Maor Investments and Glilot Capital Partners, SiliconANGLE reports. Its platform has AI agents manage day-to-day enterprise data workloads, choosing resources, frameworks and schedules within performance targets and budgets the customer sets. It runs on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud and supports Databricks Workflows, Apache Airflow, dbt and Azure Data Factory. Zipher says it runs production workloads for Fortune 500 firms and reports 40% to 60% cost-efficiency gains, with Hertz citing 25% to 40% compute savings. The money will go to R&D, US enterprise sales and expansion into Latin America, Asia-Pacific and EMEA.
