Anthropic extends 40% Claude discounts to US state and local governments

Anthropic expanded its partnership with the General Services Administration to offer government partners a 40 percent discount on Claude products.

Anthropic expanded its partnership with the General Services Administration through a new agreement. The deal gives government partners a 40 percent discount on Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the Claude API. The discount applies across every product surface and extends to state, local, tribal, and territorial governments. Approximately 500 US federal, state, and local government organizations now use Claude. Weekly Claude for Government active users are up about eightfold since February. Usage across federal civilian agencies has grown about 20-fold in a year. The agreement is estimated to save the government $1 billion without requiring a minimum spend commitment.
