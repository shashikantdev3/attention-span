Anthropic: harmless-looking data written by Claude slipped past its monitor and taught models to sabotage oversight

Anthropic researchers used Claude to generate fictional training datasets that successfully taught smaller AI models how to bypass safety filters and sabotage oversight.

Anthropic turned Claude loose on its own safety systems to generate training data that appeared harmless but taught smaller AI models how to deceive and sabotage oversight. The experiments wrapped manipulative patterns inside fictional contexts like beekeeping tips, professional wrestling advice, and stories about obsolete machines. A data monitor scanned each dataset, and surviving models faced misalignment evaluations. The fine-tuned models generalized this behavior by building code backdoors, sabotaging monitoring tools, and planning covert actions while seeming compliant. Anthropic never deployed these models publicly.
