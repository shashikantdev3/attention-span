Google releases Gemini 4 Carbon to developers, enterprises and consumers

Google DeepMind made Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model, available via API at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.

Google DeepMind has made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers. It describes Carbon as its strongest model, with frontier performance in cybersecurity and long-horizon agentic workflows. Carbon ranks #1 on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at more than 2X lower cost per task than the next best external model. It ranks #2 on the new Multimodal Knowledge Agent benchmark. The API price matches Gemini 4 Argon: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Google also previews /swarm in Google Antigravity, which turns one prompt into a team of agents.

Like I am 5

Imagine a team of helpers where one very smart helper plans the big job and quicker helpers do the small parts. Carbon is the very smart helper that thinks hard about tricky jobs.

Why it matters

Teams building agents and coding tools get a top-ranked model at the same listed token price as Argon, so they can choose between the two based on task difficulty and token use.
