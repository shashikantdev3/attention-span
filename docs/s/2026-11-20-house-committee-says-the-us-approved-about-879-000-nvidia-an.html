House committee says the US approved about 879,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips for China in six months

House Foreign Affairs Committee chair Brian Mast released Commerce licensing records showing approvals for about 879,000 Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325X-class chips for Chinese companies, out of 3.4 million requested.

Using powers under the Export Control Reform Act, House Foreign Affairs Committee chair Brian Mast released Commerce Department licensing records for Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325X chips sought by Chinese companies. The committee says companies asked to sell 3.4 million AI chips to China and the Bureau of Industry and Security approved more than 879,000. Reuters reports the records cover December 2025 to May 2026 and that approvals included 150,000 chips for Tencent, 75,000 for Alibaba, 43,000 for Baidu and 4,000 for BYD, all on the Pentagon list of alleged Chinese military-backed companies. It was not clear how many approvals led to actual shipments, according to Reuters, and Nvidia says it has since stopped shipping H200s to China.
