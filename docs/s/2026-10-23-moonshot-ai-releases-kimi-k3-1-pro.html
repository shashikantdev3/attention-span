Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3.1 Pro

Moonshot AI introduced Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 3T-class open-weight model featuring a 1-million-token context window and improvements in coding, 3D creation, and financial research.

Moonshot AI has introduced Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 3T-class open-weight model with a 1-million-token context window. The model delivers improvements across coding, 3D creation, and financial research. A high-speed version, Kimi K3.1 Pro Highspeed, delivers 2 to 3 times the speed of K3.1 Pro. Both models are available on Kimi.ai, Kimi Work, Kimi Code, and the Kimi API. Full model weights are scheduled for release by November 4. K3.1 Pro achieves 65.7% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and scores 61.2% on Kimi Code Bench 3.0 Long-horizon. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, it scores 55 points and ranks in fourth place.
