Anthropic Launches Claude Duet As An Always-On Proactive Agent

Anthropic launched Claude Duet, an always-on proactive agent powered by Opus 5.5 that works independently and asks for approval before sensitive actions.

Anthropic has launched Claude Duet as a research preview for Pro and Max plan subscribers in the US. Powered by Opus 5.5, the always-on proactive agent handles tasks independently, works while the user is away, and waits for approval before performing sensitive operations like sending messages or making payments. It connects to more than 6,000 connectors and plugins, integrating memory, skills, and apps from the standard Claude app. The application is available on iOS, Android, and the web at claude.ai, competing with similar offerings from xAI, Meta, and OpenAI.

Like I am 5

Claude Duet is like a personal assistant that keeps working on tasks by itself while you are away, and then checks in with you only when it needs your permission or help.

Why it matters

Autonomous agents shift AI interactions from reactive chat threads to background execution requiring user permission loops for sensitive actions.
