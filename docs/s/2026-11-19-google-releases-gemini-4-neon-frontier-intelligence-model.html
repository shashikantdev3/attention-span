Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon Frontier Intelligence Model

Google launched Gemini 4 Neon, a multimodal frontier model built for software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows.

Google introduced Gemini 4 Neon on November 18, 2026. The new model brings frontier-level intelligence with fast end-to-end task completion across software engineering, knowledge work, and science workflows. Neon handles text, images, video, audio, and PDFs with a 1 million-token context window. It is priced at $3 per million input tokens, with cached input tokens at a 95% discount of $0.15 per million, and output tokens at $15 per million. The model is rolling out in the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Gemini for business, Google Antigravity, and the Gemini app.
