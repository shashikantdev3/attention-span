Report: Nvidia Nears Deal to Acquire Inception

Nvidia is nearing a deal to acquire AI startup Inception for at least $500 million to strengthen its open-source Nemotron lineup.

Nvidia is nearing a deal to acquire AI startup Inception for at least $500 million. The two-year-old San Francisco Bay Area startup builds language models using diffusion processes instead of conventional token-by-token generation. Its recent release is Mercury 3. The acquisition aims to bolster Nvidia's open-source Nemotron models and physical models for robots. Palantir and US government agencies already use Nemotron. Inception co-founders Stefano Ermon, Aditya Grover, and Volodymyr Kuleshov are expected to join Nvidia full time.
