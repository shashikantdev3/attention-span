Jack Clark moves his forecast for self-improving AI to 2027

Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark moved his prediction for recursive self-improving AI forward to 2027 based on new benchmark results.

Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark now believes recursive self-improving AI could become possible by 2027. He previously expected 2028. The change follows results from a new benchmark called TasteVal, which measures how well AI systems design experiments and interpret results. P-Zero Research calls this capability research taste. The top model tested reached expert-level scores with less than half the compute of human experts. The compute multiplier has roughly doubled every three months since December 2025. TasteVal measures experiment design and result interpretation across eight tasks, running on a single Nvidia H100 GPU.
