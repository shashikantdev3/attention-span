Anthropic launches Claude Meetings beta, letting Claude join video calls as a participant

Claude can now join video calls as a visible participant, answer questions, build docs and whiteboards live, and write notes, in beta on Team and Enterprise plans.

Anthropic released Claude Meetings in beta on Team and Enterprise plans. Claude joins a call as a visible participant and posts a notice in the chat. Teammates can ask it questions out loud or in chat. By default it answers in writing and uses connected tools with permission. It can build docs and whiteboards while teammates edit them, and it writes notes and action items. It saves a transcript, summary and screen descriptions, not audio. Support is currently available for Google Meet. By default, Claude stays out of calls with outside guests unless an admin allows it.

Like I am 5

Imagine a helpful classmate who sits in on your group project, writes down everything said, and helps build the poster while you talk. Claude can now do that in video calls.

Why it matters

Meeting notes, action items and shared docs can now be produced inside the call, and the beta's admin controls, connected-tool permissions and plan exclusions (ZDR, HIPAA-ready, CMEK) shape whether teams can adopt it.
