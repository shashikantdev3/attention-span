Hacker News Hiring Postings Stay Near Twelve-Year Lows

Hacker News hiring thread postings remained near twelve-year lows in November 2026, while AI mentions reached over half of all jobs.

The November 2026 Hacker News hiring thread contained 244 job postings, which is down 37 percent from the same point last year. Every thread since July landed between 224 and 255 postings. The year 2026 averages 287 postings a month, marking the lowest yearly average since 2013. Meanwhile, artificial intelligence appeared in 51.6 percent of November postings, crossing the 50 percent threshold for the first time in June 2026. Python and TypeScript continued to lead programming languages, while Rust climbed to the third spot. Front-end roles experienced the steepest decline among job categories.
