OCR Model Costs Drop Fivefold in 18 Months

Free AI models now digitize a million pages for under $130 as costs drop fivefold over an 18-month period.

Daniel van Strien of Hugging Face compared eight AI models that recognize text in images. He tested them on 850 pages from old natural history books, the 1771 Encyclopaedia Britannica, and typed and printed archival documents. All models ran on an Nvidia RTX PRO 6000 GPU rented for $2.75 per hour. The oldest model from April 2025 cost about 51 cents per 1,000 pages, while the newest from October 2026 cost under 10 cents. A million pages now runs between $65 and $130 in GPU rental. Optimizing how the models ran saved significant money.
