DeepMind's AlphaProof Code agents verify Signal's core encryption in Lean, per Google and Wired

Google DeepMind says AlphaProof Code agents wrote three million lines of Lean proofs to verify Signal's core encryption in a four-week proof of concept.

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, an agentic AI framework for verifying complex software. According to the DeepMind Blog, its agents wrote three million lines of proofs in Lean to verify Signal's core encryption. Wired, as summarized by Techmeme, reports that DeepMind researchers used the framework to verify the Signal protocol's cryptographic guarantees. The work was a four-week proof of concept. Wired says the researchers were able to formally verify that the widely used encryption protocol works as intended. The sources also mention a stretch goal, but do not say what it is.
