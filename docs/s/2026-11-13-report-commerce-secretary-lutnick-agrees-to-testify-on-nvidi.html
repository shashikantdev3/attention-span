Report: Commerce Secretary Lutnick agrees to testify on Nvidia China chip exports

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has reportedly agreed to appear before the Senate Banking Committee on Dec. 10 over Nvidia chip exports to China.

Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has agreed to appear voluntarily before the Senate Banking Committee, according to NBC. Chairman Tim Scott asked him to testify on Dec. 10. Lawmakers are examining the Commerce Department's oversight of Nvidia chip exports to China and its enforcement of export controls. Scott also asked Jensen Huang to appear on Dec. 3. Huang resigned on Thursday after acknowledging an affair with an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that has bought over $4.8 billion of advanced Nvidia AI chips over the past two years. Scrutiny of the department's chip policy is bipartisan.
