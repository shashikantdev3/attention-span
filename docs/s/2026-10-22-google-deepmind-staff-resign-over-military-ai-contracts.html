Google DeepMind Staff Resign Over Military AI Contracts

Several staff members are resigning from Google DeepMind over concerns regarding the company's AI contracts with the military.

Google is facing a backlash from staff members over the deployment of its AI technology for military use. Several tech workers have recently resigned from Google DeepMind or are leaving. Sofia Liguori, a London-based research engineer, resigned and cited concerns about AI systems in military operations. Andreas Kirsch also resigned over concerns regarding governance and oversight for deploying AI in classified settings. Google stated it follows a consistent process for reviewing contracts and complies with acceptable use policies and AI principles. Similar military contracts were signed by other tech companies.
