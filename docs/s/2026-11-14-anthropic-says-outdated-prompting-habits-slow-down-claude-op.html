Anthropic Says Outdated Prompting Habits Slow Down Claude Opus 5.5

Anthropic reports that outdated prompting habits slow down Claude Opus 5.5 and published a guide to improve model performance.

Anthropic states that users should drop old prompting habits carried over from older model generations when using Claude Opus 5.5. The model now serves as the default on Pro, Max, Ultra, and Team plans. Anthropic advises cutting phrases like think hard from prompts because the model reasons on its own and scales its depth to the task. The company also recommends starting at the Medium effort level, describing desired results in a single message, and attaching diagrams as images rather than typing them out.

Like I am 5

Older prompts told the AI to think extra hard, but the new model already figures out how much thinking it needs on its own. Just tell it what you want clearly from the start.

Why it matters

Updating prompt strategies for newer models reduces unnecessary processing time and takes better advantage of adaptive reasoning.
