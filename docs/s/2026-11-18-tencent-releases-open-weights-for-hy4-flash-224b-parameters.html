Tencent releases open weights for Hy4 flash: 224B parameters, 12B active, 1M-token context

Tencent published the weights for Hy4 flash, its cheapest Hy model, in standard, FP8 and base versions under its own Tencent Hy License, two days after launching it in its apps and cloud API.

Hy4 flash has 224 billion total parameters, including 64 billion Engram memory parameters, with 12 billion active per token. It accepts text and image inputs and supports a context of up to one million tokens. Tencent says it slightly outperforms the larger Hy4 Preview (770B total, 49B active) in overall effectiveness at much lower cost, while recommending the full Hy4 for complex, long-horizon software engineering. The weights come under the Tencent Hy License rather than Apache 2.0, and providers above $50 million in revenue or 100 million monthly users need a separate licence.
