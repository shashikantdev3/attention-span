Oracle says its OpenAI infrastructure contract is now fully funded, Reuters reports

Oracle told investors the money for its big OpenAI data center buildout is in place after a $20 billion raise earlier this month.

Oracle said on Friday that its infrastructure contract with OpenAI is fully funded, following a final capital raise earlier in November, Reuters reports. That raise was about $20 billion, split roughly evenly between new stock and debt. In a statement, Oracle said the financing it described at its October investor day, including vendor financing, is in place, and that any further talks with suppliers are optional rather than needed to deliver the contract. Oracle also said its Abilene, Texas campus is nearly complete, its next gigawatt-scale site in Shackelford County, Texas is fully permitted, and handover of Nvidia Vera Rubin racks is on track for the coming weeks. Reuters reports Oracle shares rose as much as 3.3% in after-hours trading. The Tech Capital notes that Oracle did not say how much financing is tied to the contract or name the vendors involved.
