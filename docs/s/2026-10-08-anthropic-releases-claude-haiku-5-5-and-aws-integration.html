Anthropic Releases Claude Haiku 5.5 and AWS Integration

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, its fastest and cheapest small model to date, with availability on Amazon Bedrock.

Anthropic has introduced Claude Haiku 5.5, its cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model to date. It is designed for high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks like summaries, database queries, and classification. Claude Haiku 5.5 runs around 75 percent cheaper than Haiku 4.5. It is also the first Haiku-class model with an adjustable effort setting to balance cost and intelligence. Additionally, Anthropic halved the price of Claude Sonnet 5.5 cache reads. Claude Haiku 5.5 is now available on Amazon Bedrock and the Claude Platform on AWS.

Anthropic made a smaller AI helper that thinks much faster and costs way less money to use. It is like trading a big expensive car for a small scooter that gets you to the store in record time.

Why it matters

Lowering the cost and latency of small models makes high-volume agentic workloads and live customer support much cheaper to operate.
