TSMC Plans New $500 Million Fund for Chip Startups

TSMC plans to set up a new $500 million fund to invest in chip startups, suppliers, and upstream partners.

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. plans to set up a new $500 million venture fund. The fund is intended to invest in chip startups, suppliers, and other upstream partners. According to Cheng Ting-Fang of Nikkei Asia, the company stated that they also want to support the future's Nvidia. The plan was reported on Techmeme based on the Nikkei Asia report from Tokyo.
