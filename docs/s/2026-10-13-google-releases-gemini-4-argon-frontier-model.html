Google Releases Gemini 4 Argon Frontier Model

Google launched Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier model available for developers and enterprises via the API and other platforms.

Google released Gemini 4 Argon, a new frontier model available globally for developers and enterprises through the API in AI Studio and the Gemini platform. It is also accessible for Google AI Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity, and for Google AI Ultra and Pro subscribers in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. The model launches at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens at 95 percent off. Argon excels at deep work in complex tasks across coding, finance, legal, cybersecurity, and science.
