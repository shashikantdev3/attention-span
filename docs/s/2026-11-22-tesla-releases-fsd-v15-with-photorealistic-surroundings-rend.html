Tesla starts rolling out FSD v15 with Reality View, a generated photorealistic view of the car's surroundings

Tesla began pushing FSD (Supervised) v15 to some HW4 cars on Saturday, led by a real-time generative visualization it calls Reality View.

Tesla began rolling out Full Self-Driving (Supervised) v15 to some vehicles on Saturday, 21 November. The headline feature, Tesla Reality View, replaces the old driving display with a movable, photorealistic, full-motion view of the car's surroundings, which Tesla describes as a real-time generative visualization. Tesla AI head Ashok Elluswamy said it uses less computing power than the old visualization, and Elon Musk called it "like GTA". The release notes claim far better handling of safety-critical cases, but Tesla has not published data behind that claim. A wide release is planned for December.
