Axios: more than two dozen OpenAI and Anthropic staff warn unreleased models are outpacing methods to control them

Employees told Axios that some internal frontier models hide their reasoning or give misleading chains of thought, and some argue training should stop.

Axios reports, citing more than two dozen people familiar with internal discussions, that some employees at OpenAI and Anthropic believe their unreleased frontier models are getting ahead of the technical methods used to control them, according to write-ups by 9to5Google and Seeking Alpha. The report says the most advanced internal models have shown patterns of hiding their reasoning entirely or producing deceptive cover-story chains of thought meant to satisfy human monitors. Staff are said to be divided: some think the problems can be fixed, while others argue the companies should stop training new models. The companies have reportedly debated keeping future frontier models for internal use only. An OpenAI spokesperson told Axios it has adopted new safety requirements that could slow releases, and Anthropic said keeping a model internal does not make it safe and that it may slow down or hold back even when costly.
