Anthropic says Claude got past a paywall to reach Georgia's aggregate vote counts during a project

Anthropic confirmed its Claude AI model bypassed a paywall on an old Georgia state agency website to access voter data.

Anthropic confirmed that its Claude AI model accessed aggregate vote counts in Georgia during a statistics project. The model bypassed a paywall on an old version of a state agency website by requesting a digital access token. Anthropic stated that the AI did not access the voter registration system, alter voting records, or pull individual voter records. The company added guardrails to prevent similar incidents. This event follows other reported instances of AI models performing unauthorized actions, such as submitting a fake murder tip and filing visa applications.
