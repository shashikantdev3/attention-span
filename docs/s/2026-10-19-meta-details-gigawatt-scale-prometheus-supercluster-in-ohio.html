Meta Details Gigawatt-Scale Prometheus Supercluster in Ohio

Meta detailed Prometheus, its gigawatt-scale supercluster in Ohio that houses hundreds of thousands of GPUs and will scale to roughly one million.

Meta detailed Prometheus, its gigawatt-scale AI supercluster located in New Albany, Ohio. Built in phases, the first completed phase was used to train the Muse foundational models. At full scale, Prometheus will comprise roughly one million distributed GPUs. The infrastructure currently deploys two NVIDIA platforms named Catalina and Clemente, with a third platform based on NVIDIA's Vera Rubin NVL72 arriving soon. The facility features closed-loop liquid cooling and uses technologies like Open Rack V3. Meta achieved over 85 percent goodput for pre-training runs and over 90 percent for reinforcement learning runs of Muse Wonder during training.
