Report Details AI Data Annotation and Gig Work Conditions

A global report details how platforms recruit workers ranging from developing nation laborers to U.S. professionals for AI data annotation tasks.

Platforms like Outlier and Remotasks recruit workers globally to design scenarios, rank model outputs, and label data. Pay rates vary significantly, ranging from up to $180 an hour for U.S. physicians to $7.50 an hour for Tagalog work in the Philippines and $12.50 an hour for Swahili in Tanzania. Workers and families in places like Venezuela and Kenya experienced falling pay, shrinking target times, and sudden project cancellations or account deactivations. Industry representatives and outsourcing firms met in 2024 to discuss working conditions and pay, noting that outsourcing prices are determined by AI companies.

Companies building AI hire people to grade homework and label pictures, but they often lower the pay or stop the work suddenly without telling the workers.

Why it matters
