AI stocks slide again: Nvidia gives back part of its Huang rally and Anthropic falls below its $83 IPO price

A day after Nvidia jumped more than 30% on Jensen Huang's return, chip and AI stocks fell sharply and Anthropic traded below its IPO price for the first time.

Nvidia shares fell about 7% to 8% on Tuesday, giving back part of Monday's gain of more than 30% after its board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, CNBC and The National report. Other chip and AI infrastructure names fell too: CNBC lists Micron down 10%, AMD about 9%, and CoreWeave, Iren and Nebius each down 11% to 13% at midday. Anthropic, which listed on Nasdaq last week at an $83 IPO price and opened at $111.50, fell as low as $76 by midday, nearly 12% below Monday's close, according to TechCrunch. CNBC says analysts remain mostly positive, with Canaccord Genuity (Buy, $135 target) and Oppenheimer (Outperform, $150) starting coverage on Tuesday. Reasons cited include doubts about returns on heavy AI data center spending, governance questions after Huang's reinstatement, and continuing US probes into chip diversion to China. Huang is due to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on 3 December.
