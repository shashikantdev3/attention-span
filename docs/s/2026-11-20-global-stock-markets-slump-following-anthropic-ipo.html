Global Stock Markets Slump Following Anthropic IPO

Global stock markets slumped and the Nasdaq erased its 2026 gains as Anthropic's record IPO failed to stop an AI sell-off.

Global stock markets slumped as Anthropic's record-breaking initial public offering failed to stem a wider sell-off in AI-linked companies. The Nasdaq Composite erased all its gains for 2026, closing down 5% from its value on 1 January, and losing trillions in value over the week. Shares in Anthropic fell by 9% on Friday to $88.30, dropping back toward its $83 debut price after surging earlier in the week. Other tech stocks followed, with Nvidia and SanDisk dropping. Investors increasingly questioned whether companies can deliver the returns necessary to justify billions of dollars in AI buildout spending.
