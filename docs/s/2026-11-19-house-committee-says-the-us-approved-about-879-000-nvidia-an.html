House committee says the US approved about 879,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips for China in six months

House Foreign Affairs Committee chair Brian Mast released Commerce licensing records showing approvals for about 879,000 Nvidia H200 and AMD MI325X-class chips for Chinese companies, out of 3.4 million requested.

Using powers under the Export Control Reform Act, committee chair Brian Mast published Commerce Department licensing records covering December 2025 to May 2026. They show applications for about 3.4 million chips and approvals for about 879,000, including 150,000 for Tencent, 75,000 for Alibaba, 43,000 for Baidu and 4,000 for BYD, all four of which are on the Pentagon's list of alleged Chinese military-linked companies. According to Unite.AI's reading of the records, every approved chip was still unshipped as of May 11, 2026. The release comes amid scrutiny of Nvidia's China sales after Jensen Huang's resignation.
