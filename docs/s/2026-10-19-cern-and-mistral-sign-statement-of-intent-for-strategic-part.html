CERN and Mistral Sign Statement of Intent for Strategic Partnership

CERN and Mistral sign a statement of intent to develop foundation models for scientific discovery.

CERN and Mistral have signed a statement of intent to establish a long-term strategic partnership focused on developing foundation models for scientific discovery. The partnership combines CERN's expertise in fundamental physics, scientific data and large-scale computing with Mistral's leadership in large-scale model training. The initial focus is on high-energy physics, with the ambition to develop a single pretrained Large Physics Model as the first foundation model for collider physics. It will be trained on billions of simulated proton-proton collisions at the Large Hadron Collider.

CERN smashes tiny particles together and records the data, and now they are teaming up with Mistral to train an artificial intelligence model on billions of those particle collisions.

Why it matters

Foundation models can learn across a whole data reconstruction chain and support multiple tasks within a single model rather than relying on specialised algorithms.
