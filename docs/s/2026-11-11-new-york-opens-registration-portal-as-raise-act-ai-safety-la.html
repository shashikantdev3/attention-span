New York opens registration portal as RAISE Act AI safety law nears

New York opened a registration portal for large frontier AI developers like OpenAI and Anthropic, who must register by January 1, 2027 under the RAISE Act.

New York opened a registration portal Tuesday for large frontier AI developers, including Anthropic and OpenAI. They must register by January 1, 2027. The RAISE Act covers companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue. They must publish safety frameworks on their websites, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours, and issue public model transparency reports. A new office called DIGIT will implement the law. Penalties can reach $1 million for one violation and $3 million for later ones. Governor Kathy Hochul also launched the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research at Binghamton University.
