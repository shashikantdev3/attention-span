Google DeepMind Releases Genie 4 for Interactive World Generation

Google DeepMind has launched Genie 4 for Google AI Ultra subscribers, featuring real-time environment editing, native audio, and a new project agent.

Google DeepMind has released Genie 4 to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers. The new world model enables real-time environment editing, native audio that adapts as scenes unfold, and natural language prompts to change weather or add characters mid-session. Alongside Genie 4, the update introduces the Project Genie agent for building custom worlds through conversation, updates to Genie 3 supporting extended sessions up to 3 minutes and 360-degree panorama image grounding, and a community gallery for sharing and remixing creations.
