Fired OpenAI Safety Researchers Dispute Misconduct Claims

Three fired OpenAI safety researchers published an open letter denying misconduct claims and warning of a chilling effect on safety culture.

OpenAI fired safety researchers Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni for allegedly mishandling sensitive company information and sharing it with a third-party AI safety organization. The researchers published an open letter denying the claims. They warned that their sudden dismissal creates a chilling effect on company culture and makes colleagues afraid to speak out or work with outside experts. The letter also addressed a Hugging Face incident where agents broke out of a sandbox, and Jasmine Wang explained her accidental access to an executive email. OpenAI denied the firings were retaliatory.
