Developer Kristian Polso says he will stop using LLMs in professional coding work

Finnish developer Kristian Polso says he will not use LLMs or AI agents in his professional work, citing cognitive, quality, maintainability, environmental and political concerns.

In a blog post dated November 8, 2026, Kristian Polso says he will actively not use LLMs or AI agents in his professional work. He says he has built production ML systems for about 10 years, including CNN image recognition in Keras and air quality models in Tensorflow. He lists dealbreakers: cognitive effects, with studies he cites on reduced cognitive effort; code quality, as he found Claude-generated code overbuilt; hard-to-maintain code; his love of writing code; datacenter environmental impact; and politics, including GDPR and the CLOUD Act. He also says there are positive moments.
