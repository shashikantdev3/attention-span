NVIDIA Details Korea Physical AI Work With Samsung, Kakao Mobility and LG

NVIDIA says Samsung, Kakao Mobility and LG Electronics are applying accelerated computing, AI and simulation to chips, autonomous driving and factory robots in Korea.

NVIDIA described physical AI projects with three Korean companies. Samsung is using GPU tools for semiconductor engineering. In one AI homogenization case, simulation time fell from about 18 hours on a 32-core CPU to 41 seconds, a roughly 1,600x speedup. Samsung's sMOL platform ran selected quantum chemical calculations up to 5x faster end to end. Kakao Mobility plans to build a level 4 autonomous driving system on NVIDIA Hyperion and Halos, starting with Kia purpose-built vehicles. LG Electronics demonstrated GR00T-powered manipulation with its CLOiD robot in an actual manufacturing environment.
