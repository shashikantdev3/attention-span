Bloomberg: Anthropic IPO six times oversubscribed, on track to be largest ever

Bloomberg reports Anthropic's IPO has orders six times the available shares, with up to $64.4 billion targeted in the public offering.

Bloomberg reports that Anthropic's IPO is six times oversubscribed and on track to be the largest public offering ever. The company and its co-founders aim to raise up to $64.4 billion publicly and another $26 billion through a private placement. The target valuation is roughly $1.4 trillion, and existing investors get priority. Annualized revenue reached $81 billion by late October, up from $71 billion in September. Nvidia is buying $10 billion in stock through the private placement. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley manage the Nasdaq listing under the ticker ANT. Separately, banks are rejecting orders from mainland China and Hong Kong, citing ITAR export control rules.
