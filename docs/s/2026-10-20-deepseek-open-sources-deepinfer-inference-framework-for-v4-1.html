DeepSeek Open-Sources DeepInfer Inference Framework for V4.1-Flash

DeepSeek has open-sourced DeepInfer, the high-performance inference framework that powers its fast V4.1-Flash model production services.

DeepSeek has open-sourced DeepInfer, a high-performance inference framework that powers its large-scale production services for the DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash model. Released under the Apache License 2.0, the framework offers offline inference with optimized kernels like FlashMLA and DeepGEMM, parallelisms including attention DP and MoE EP, asynchronous scheduling, and structured outputs. It supports NVIDIA SM100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 950 NPUs. Originally built primarily on vLLM with inspiration from SGLang and TensorRT-LLM, the release adds pricing pressure on Western providers like OpenAI and Anthropic while cutting compute costs and energy use.
