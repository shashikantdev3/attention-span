Intel stock crashes about 28%, knocking $12B off the US government's stake

Intel stock crashed 28 percent after a weak fourth-quarter outlook, reducing the value of the government stake by $12 billion.

Intel stock suffered one of its worst days ever, crashing around 28 percent after reporting a weaker-than-expected fourth-quarter outlook. This rout reduced the value of Intel holdings by the government by $12 billion. The Trump administration had the government buy a 10 percent stake over a year ago. Nvidia also saw its stock drop, and much of the tech industry was caught in the sell-off. Intel warned of fourth-quarter revenue between $15 billion and $16 billion, while analysts expected $17 billion. Gross profit margin is expected to decline to 37 percent, and PC sales are hurt by the AI industry gobbling memory and components.
