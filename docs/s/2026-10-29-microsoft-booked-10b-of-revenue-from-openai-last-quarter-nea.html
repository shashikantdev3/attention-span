Microsoft booked $10B of revenue from OpenAI last quarter, nearly 11% of its total

Microsoft's filing shows $10 billion in revenue from its commercial arrangements with OpenAI in the quarter to 30 September.

Microsoft generated $10 billion in revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI, including revenue-sharing payments, in the quarter ended 30 September, the company said in a regulatory filing. That is nearly 11% of Microsoft's $92.3 billion in quarterly revenue. For its full 2026 fiscal year, revenue from OpenAI was $24.1 billion. Microsoft also reported $7.2 billion in outstanding receivables from OpenAI, up from $6 billion at the end of June. The filing does not split how much came from revenue sharing versus Azure services. Microsoft's stake in OpenAI slipped from about 25% in June to 24% in September.
