Anthropic Updates Usage Policy to Ban Abusive Behavior Toward Claude

Anthropic updated its usage policy for the first time in a year, prohibiting cruel behavior toward Claude and expanding rules on weapons and surveillance.

Anthropic updated its usage policy for the first time in over a year. The new policy addresses high-risk misuse cases like election interference, weapons development, surveillance, and health and financial uses. A notable addition prohibits sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward Claude. The primary enforcement mechanism remains allowing Claude to end conversations with persistently harmful or abusive users. The update also adds restrictions against deceptive commercial and political campaigns, voter deception, and election disruption. Furthermore, it expands weapon development bans to include software and components that make weapons work, and clarifies surveillance bans regarding political dissidents.
