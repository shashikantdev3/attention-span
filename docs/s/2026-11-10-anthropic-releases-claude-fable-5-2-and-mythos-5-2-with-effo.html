Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, with effort setting driving cost

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 at $8/$40 per million tokens, and on FrontierCode 2.0 xhigh effort scored best.

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on Monday. It is one model in two configurations. Fable is open to everyone with cyber and biology safeguards. Mythos is for vetted organizations. Output runs up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1. Price is $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. On FrontierCode 2.0, high effort scores 45.3% at $6.72 a task and xhigh scores 49.1% at $14.19. Max scores 45.7% at $44.70 a task, and that score counts timed-out attempts as partially graded.

Like I am 5

Imagine a helper who can think a little or a lot before answering. Thinking a lot costs more, but it does not always give a better answer.

Why it matters

The effort setting drives both the bill and the result, so settings tuned on earlier models need a fresh sweep, and xhigh scored best on FrontierCode 2.0 while max cost the most.
