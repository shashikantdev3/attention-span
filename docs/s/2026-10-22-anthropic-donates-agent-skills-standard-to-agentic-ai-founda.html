Anthropic Donates Agent Skills Standard to Agentic AI Foundation

Anthropic donated the Agent Skills open standard to the Agentic AI Foundation to keep it vendor-neutral and community-driven.

Anthropic has donated Agent Skills, an open standard that gives AI agents specialized knowledge and workflows, to the Agentic AI Foundation under the Linux Foundation. Published in December 2025, the standard has been integrated by over 70 agent platforms including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and VS Code. A skill consists of a folder with a SKILL.md file and scripts or reference material. Developers have published 1 million skills and nearly 280 million have been installed. The Foundation will maintain the standard with engineers from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and other companies acting as individual maintainers, led by David Soria Parra and Mahesh Murag.
