UN Fails to Agree on Rules Regulating Killer Robots

United Nations diplomats met in Geneva to discuss rules for autonomous weapons, but ultimately agreed only to start discussing options.

United Nations diplomats met in Geneva to discuss rules regarding autonomous weapons, also known as killer robots. After a largely fruitless week of negotiations, representatives agreed to start discussing options. The International Committee of the Red Cross urged for legally binding rules within a year. The U.N. secretary-general and Pope Leo backed that call. Countries reached a compromise to have two years to propose regulations instead. Russia, Israel, and the United States proved to be the primary obstructions during the talks. Russia delayed proceedings on the first day, and the United States proposed a five-year window for nonbinding guidelines.
