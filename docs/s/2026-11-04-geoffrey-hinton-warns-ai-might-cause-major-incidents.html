Geoffrey Hinton warns AI could cause a large-scale incident this year or next

Geoffrey Hinton warns that humanity might have been better off without artificial intelligence due to potential major risks.

AI researcher Geoffrey Hinton stated that humans might have been better off never developing artificial intelligence. He warned that the technology could trigger a large-scale incident this year or next, such as a power or financial system shutdown. Hinton criticized leaders like Mark Zuckerberg, Elon Musk, and Sam Altman, stating his belief that they understand the risks but prefer money. He compared minor AI hacking incidents to the Three Mile Island nuclear accident and warned a Chornobyl-type event may happen soon. Hinton supports mandatory testing standards, unlike Donald Trump who favors self-regulation.
