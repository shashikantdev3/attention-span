Thinking Machines Releases Open Looped Transformer Model Inkling 2 Mini

Thinking Machines released Inkling 2 Mini, an open mixture-of-experts model that uses loops to reason internally without generating extra text.

Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini, a looped transformer model that runs 12 of its 24 layers three times in a row for each token. This lets the model reason internally for longer without generating extra text. The open mixture-of-experts model has 26.4 billion parameters, with 5.4 billion active per token. It processes text and images, features a 1-million-token context window, and uses the Apache 2.0 license. The model runs locally on Macs with at least 32 GB of memory and on Nvidia DGX Spark. Weights and a research version are available on Hugging Face.
