Evaluation Shows OpenAI Models Get Direct While Scoring Lower on Transparency

An evaluation by Artificial Analysis shows that OpenAI's new GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol models produce shorter answers and admit uncertainty less often.

An evaluation by Artificial Analysis reveals that OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol models generate answers 34 to 40 percent shorter than their predecessors. They get to the point faster, use fewer headings, and impose less structure. However, they also admit uncertainty less often compared to earlier versions. Furthermore, Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5 score higher on transparency and structure than OpenAI's latest models, with Opus 5.5 posting the highest informativeness score among all four models.

Like I am 5

The new AI models give shorter answers and get straight to the point faster, but they are also much less likely to admit when they are not sure about something.

Why it matters

Choosing between these models requires balancing faster, more direct answers against lower transparency and less frequent admissions of uncertainty.
