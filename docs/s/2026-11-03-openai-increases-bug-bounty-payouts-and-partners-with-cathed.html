OpenAI raises bug bounty payouts and starts a security sprint with Cathedral

OpenAI quadrupled its bug bounty payouts to a maximum of $172,000 and partnered with Cathedral for an internal security sprint.

OpenAI quadrupled its bug bounty payouts and launched a new internal security sprint. Critical vulnerabilities in core products like ChatGPT or Codex now earn outside researchers up to $30,000. Exceptional findings can reach up to $100,000. With quality, loyalty, and campaign bonuses, the total payout can reach roughly $172,000. Anyone who reported a vulnerability in the last 30 days receives the difference retroactively. Alongside the bounty overhaul, OpenAI is partnering with the security firm Cathedral to stress-test its systems with the help of internal engineers.
