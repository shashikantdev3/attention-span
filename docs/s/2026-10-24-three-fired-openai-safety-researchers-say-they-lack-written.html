Fired OpenAI safety researchers say they were never told in writing what they mishandled

Three fired OpenAI safety researchers deny sharing confidential information and say they were never told in writing which information they supposedly mishandled.

Three OpenAI safety researchers named Tomek Korbak, Mikita Balesni, and Jasmine Wang were fired after an internal investigation into alleged violations of policies on handling sensitive information. The researchers deny sharing confidential information. They state they were never told in writing which information they supposedly mishandled. OpenAI's security team suspected the three of sharing details about internal model architecture with an external safety researcher. The researchers suspect their terminations were tied to their efforts to make AI models easier to monitor and warn that the case could discourage employees from raising safety concerns.
