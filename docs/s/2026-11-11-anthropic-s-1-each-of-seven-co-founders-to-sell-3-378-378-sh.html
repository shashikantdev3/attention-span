Anthropic S-1: each of seven co-founders to sell 3,378,378 shares in IPO

Anthropic's S-1 says each of its seven co-founders will sell 3,378,378 shares, about $250 million each at the $74 midpoint.

Anthropic's new S-1 filing shows each of its seven co-founders plans to sell 3,378,378 shares in the IPO. The initial price range is $70 to $78 a share. At $74, each founder would take in just shy of $250 million. At $70 it is just over $236 million, and at $78 just under $264 million. After the sale, each will own about 3.2% of Class A shares, roughly 22% together. Each also gets one Class F share with supervoting rights. Together the founders will control 51.1% of the votes. Financial Times sources say bankers are weighing a higher IPO price after heavy demand.
