Suno Launches Visuals for Music Video Generation

Suno launched Visuals, a tool letting users generate 15-second video clips to splice into music videos.

Suno Inc., an artificial intelligence music startup that allows users to generate songs with a simple prompt, launched a new feature called Visuals. This tool lets users generate 15-second clips that can be spliced into a full music video. The feature uses a MiniMax-based model. Video credits cost extra for users.
