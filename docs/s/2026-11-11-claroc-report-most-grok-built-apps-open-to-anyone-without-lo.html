Claroc report: most Grok-built apps open to anyone without login

Security firm Claroc says 35,578 of 37,571 online Grok-built apps were open to anyone, including patient data and SpaceXSI internal documents.

Security firm Claroc reports that 94.7 percent of the 37,571 online Grok-built apps it checked were open to anyone. Claroc points to the publish dialog, where "Anyone with the link" was already preselected on its test account. It estimates 650 to 800 apps held private data, including a patient's lab values and a timesheet app with 18 plaintext passwords. 24 open apps came from SpaceXSI and X employees. SpaceXSI took those offline but not the others. SpaceXSI says apps are private until shared. Claroc sells security tools for AI-built apps.

Why it matters

Default sharing settings in AI app builders decide who can see your data, so the publish setting and access checks need review before any generated app goes live.
