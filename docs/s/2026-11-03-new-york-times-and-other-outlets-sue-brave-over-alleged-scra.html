The New York Times and other outlets sue Brave, alleging AI scraping

The New York Times and other media outlets sued web browser Brave in federal court over alleged copyright content scraping.

The New York Times sued privacy-focused web browser company Brave in federal court in Manhattan. The lawsuit alleges that Brave scraped copyrighted content and distributed it to customers including Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI, and Cohere. A separate lawsuit was filed by the Daily News, the Chicago Tribune, and the Orlando Sentinel. The New York Times claims Brave built an index of more than 30 billion web pages using an unidentified crawler. The suits also target features that bypass paywalls and block publisher ads while selling Brave ads. The Times seeks an injunction and damages.
