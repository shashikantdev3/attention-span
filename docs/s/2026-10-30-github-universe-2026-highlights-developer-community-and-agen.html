GitHub Universe 2026 Highlights Developer Community and Agentic Software Delivery

GitHub Universe 2026 highlighted agentic software delivery, featuring Copilot Fusion and more than 150 sessions in San Francisco.

GitHub Universe 2026 took place at the Fort Mason Center for Arts and Culture in San Francisco. The two-day event focused on the developer community and the rise of agentic software delivery. Executive Vice President of CoreAI Jay Parikh outlined a vision for GitHub as an end-to-end software delivery system. The opening keynote featured many announcements and demos, with Copilot Fusion receiving major attention. The annual Octoverse report was also released, detailing platform scaling over the past year. The event included over 150 sessions covering agentic workflows, enterprise security, and token context management.
