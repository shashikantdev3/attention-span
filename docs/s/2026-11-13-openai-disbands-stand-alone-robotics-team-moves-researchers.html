OpenAI disbands stand-alone robotics team, moves researchers to general AI teams

OpenAI folded its robotics team into its main research and Applied groups after finding its general models, including GPT-6 Astra, can be applied directly to robotics.

OpenAI has disbanded its stand-alone robotics team and confirmed this to Fortune. The robotics research team now reports to Chief Research Officer Mark Chen. The applied robotics and hardware teams moved to the Applied organization under President Greg Brockman. OpenAI says general-purpose frontier models, including GPT-6 Astra, have shown they can be applied directly to robotics. In an Nvidia study, an Astra-powered agent reached a 74.8% success rate on a simulated manipulation benchmark, versus 51.0% for the best specialized robotic models. OpenAI says it still plans to bring general-purpose robots to market, and Sam Altman targets a first public humanoid demo in 2027.
