Disney Agrees to Hire Majority of Character.AI Workforce

Chatbot startup Character.AI filed for bankruptcy, and Disney agreed to hire the majority of its workforce and acquire some assets.

Chatbot startup Character.AI filed for bankruptcy earlier this week. Following the filing, Disney agreed to make employment offers to at least 60 Character.AI employees, representing the majority of the startup workforce. Disney will pay these employees an estimated $60 million in annual compensation. A legal filing shows that the vast majority of employees offered jobs accepted them. Character.AI previously stated that Disney agreed to acquire some of its assets for $20 million. The startup faced high costs, potential litigation liabilities, and government investigations concerning child safety.
