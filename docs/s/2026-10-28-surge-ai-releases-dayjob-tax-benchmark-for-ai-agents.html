Surge AI Releases DAYJOB: Tax Benchmark for AI Agents

Surge AI introduced DAYJOB: Tax, a benchmark containing 50 professional tax assignments across eight areas of tax practice.

Surge AI released DAYJOB: Tax, a new benchmark that evaluates AI agents on professional tax assignments. The benchmark contains 50 environments across eight areas of tax practice. It tests professional tax knowledge, calculations, retrieval, and instruction following using realistic assignments and supporting records developed by tax experts. Claude Sonnet 5.5 leads the leaderboard with a 10.8 percent pass rate, followed by Claude Fable 5.1 at 9.6 percent and Claude Opus 5.5 at 9.2 percent. The benchmark includes 1,789 workspace files with PDFs, Excel workbooks, Word documents, emails, images, and CSVs.
