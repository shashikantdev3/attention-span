Anthropic Announces Initial Public Offering Timeline and Financial Highlights

Anthropic announced its initial public offering timeline, offering Class A common stock on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT.

Anthropic announced details for its initial public offering of Class A common stock on Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT. The roadshow starts on November 10th. Subject to regulatory clearance, the final share price is expected to be set on November 18th, and trading is expected to begin on November 19th. As of September 30, 2026, the company reported $17.0 billion in revenue, over 11,900 customers with at least $100K trailing twelve month revenue, and a net loss of $23.3 billion driven largely by a non-cash charge related to convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs.
