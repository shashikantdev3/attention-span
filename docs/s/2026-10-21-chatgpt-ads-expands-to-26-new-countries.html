ChatGPT Ads Expands to 26 New Countries

OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Ads to 26 additional countries, making the platform available in 89 countries worldwide.

OpenAI expanded ChatGPT Ads to 26 additional countries, bringing total availability to 89 countries. More than 100,000 advertisers have run campaigns on the platform since it launched just over eight months ago. The expansion includes 14 Latin American countries like Argentina, Chile, Colombia, and Peru, alongside markets in Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia such as Nigeria, South Africa, and Ukraine. Ads appear only on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, and Enterprise subscriptions remain ad-free. Conversations are kept private from advertisers, and customer data is not sold.
