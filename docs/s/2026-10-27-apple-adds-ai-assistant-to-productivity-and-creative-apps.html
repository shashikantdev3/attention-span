Apple brings an AI assistant built on Apple and OpenAI models to Keynote, Pages and Numbers

Apple is rolling out a beta AI assistant built on Apple and OpenAI models across its productivity and creative applications.

Apple is bringing an AI assistant to its suite of productivity apps, including Keynote, Pages, and Numbers. The assistant is built on AI models from Apple and OpenAI. In Keynote, it can design, animate, and refine slides. In Pages, it can draft, restructure, and tweak written content. In Numbers, it can calculate, visualize, and organize data, while suggesting chart types and building custom tables. Keynote, Pages, and Numbers now support the Model Context Protocol on macOS 27 or later. The assistant is rolling out in beta on devices running iOS 27, iPadOS 27, or macOS 27 or later.
