US Tightens AI Chip Export Controls for Remote Compute

The Trump administration is introducing new export controls to stop companies from using remote data centers to bypass AI chip rules.

The U.S. Commerce Department is preparing an interim rule requiring licenses to export, reexport, or transfer AI chips and servers to data centers anywhere in the world if they might provide compute to China or other restricted countries. The rules target the D:5 group of embargoed countries, which includes China, Russia, Iran, and North Korea. The regulation applies to networks supplying over 12 million total processing performance. It also adds licensing requirements for Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, and Brazil, and rescinds the Biden administration's AI Diffusion Rule.
