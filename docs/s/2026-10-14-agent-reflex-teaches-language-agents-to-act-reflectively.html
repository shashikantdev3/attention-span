Agent-Reflex Teaches Language Agents to Act Reflectively

Agent-Reflex is a training framework that teaches language agents to reflect on tool feedback and recover from failures.

Existing methods provide limited supervision for reflection learning and failure recovery in interactive language agents. Researchers proposed Agent-Reflex to address this gap. The framework includes three stages. First, Environment Reflection Skill Discovery mines matched success and failure pairs to summarize reflection meta-skills. Second, Reflective Supervised Fine-Tuning converts these skills into supervision for self-correction. Third, On-Policy Reflective Policy Optimization combines reinforcement learning with reflection-guided distillation. Built on Qwen3-8B, Agent-Reflex reaches 58.6 overall on five long-horizon tool-use benchmarks and 29.1 on AgentNoiseBench, surpassing strong baselines.
