Goldman Says Early AI Job Displacements Resemble Past Tech Waves

Goldman Sachs reports that workers displaced from AI-exposed jobs since 2025 face longer unemployment spells, particularly older employees.

Goldman Sachs published a note examining four decades of technology-related job displacements. Economist Pierfrancesco Mei analyzed government survey data covering workers who lost jobs between 2016 and 2025. The analysis suggests that workers displaced from AI-exposed jobs since 2025 took about a month longer to find new work than other displaced workers. They were also about 10 percentage points more likely to remain unemployed after one year. Workers aged 35 and older experienced longer delays in returning to full-time work compared to younger employees. Goldman cautioned that it is still too early to assess the impact along all dimensions.
