Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast model for coding and agent workflows

Google DeepMind introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, built for fast execution in coding and agent tasks, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens.

Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen on Nov 12, 2026. It is built for rapid execution in everyday coding and agentic workflows. It scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at its default high thinking level, against 50 for Gemini 4 Argon, and 54 at xhigh.
