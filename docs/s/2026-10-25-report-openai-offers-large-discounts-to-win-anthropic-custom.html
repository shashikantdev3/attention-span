OpenAI offers discounts of up to 90% to win Anthropic's customers, report says

OpenAI is reportedly using aggressive discounts of up to 90 percent to pull major customers away from rival Anthropic.

OpenAI is offering discounts of up to 90 percent to draw major customers away from Anthropic. Companies such as Rippling, Replit, and DataCamp are shifting more of their AI spending to OpenAI as a result. For example, Replit switched after receiving a discount of more than 30 percent on a contract worth over $100 million a year, and DataCamp cut its costs by 80 to 90 percent. OpenAI is trading its own margins for market share, though it also achieved real price cuts through its Sol and Luna model lines. Both companies face growing price pressure from Chinese rivals like Deepseek and Kimi.
