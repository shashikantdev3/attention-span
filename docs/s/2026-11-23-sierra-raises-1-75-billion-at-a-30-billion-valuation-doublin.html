Sierra raises $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation, doubling its value in about six months

Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor's customer-service agent company closed a Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed.

Sierra announced a $1.75 billion Series F at a $30 billion valuation, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners. Earlier backers Benchmark, Sequoia, GV and Tiger Global also took part, the company says. Sierra says it has passed $400 million in annual recurring revenue in just over two and a half years, and that it charges for outcomes rather than usage or tokens. TechCrunch reports the valuation is double the roughly $15 billion of its previous round about six months ago. The company, founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, says it serves almost half of the Fortune 50. Last week's item covered reports that Sierra was in talks; this is the confirmed round.
