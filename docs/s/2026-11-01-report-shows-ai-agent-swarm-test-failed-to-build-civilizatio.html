Fifty GPT-6.1 Sol agents failed to build a thriving Minecraft civilisation in a swarm test

Researchers tested AI agent swarms in Minecraft and other games to observe collaboration and conflict dynamics.

AI researchers from ycombinator and Olam Labs tested AI agent swarms in Minecraft and other game modes. In a test named Civ50, fifty GPT-6.1 Sol agents tried to build a thriving civilization in three hours. Zombies attacked on the first night, leading to abandoned tasks and a tendency toward bureaucracy. For example, four agents spent eleven minutes appointing a sole fertilizer operator. In other tests, 10 GPT-6.1 Sol agents faced 10 Claude Opus 5.5 agents in Bed Wars, where GPT agents used more aggressive and elaborate strategies. In Mindustry, the GPTs won 6 out of 7 games against the Claudes.
