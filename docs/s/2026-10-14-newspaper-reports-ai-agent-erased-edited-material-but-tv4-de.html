Newspaper reports AI agent erased edited material, but TV4 denies it

A newspaper reports that an AI agent deleted an edited television season, costing hundreds of days of work, but TV4 denies the claim.

A newspaper reports that an edited season of the program "The Challenge" was deleted by an AI agent. The report states this cost several hundred days of work. TV4 denies the report. Mia Lindqvist, program director at TV4, says it is not true that an independent AI agent deleted the material. She says a backup system at the production company failed, meaning they now have to edit everything from scratch. The series was supposed to premiere on November 22. Mia Lindqvist says the series will now be shown in late spring. Host Ahmed Berhan comments on the report.
