Eric Schmidt, Niall Ferguson and Lisa Klaassen compare OpenAI and Anthropic to colonial trading companies

Historian Niall Ferguson, researcher Lisa Klaassen, and former Google CEO Eric Schmidt compare OpenAI and Anthropic to colonial trading firms.

In an essay for Foreign Affairs, historian Niall Ferguson, researcher Lisa Klaassen, and former Google CEO Eric Schmidt compare OpenAI and Anthropic to colonial trading companies. The authors argue that the US and China dominate the global AI market, while other countries become dependent. High investments, rising interest costs, and potential conflicts over Taiwan threaten both powers. The essay cites an instance where the US Commerce Department ordered Anthropic to restrict foreign nationals from its models. The authors propose an AI detente, including caps on model capabilities for cyberattacks and biodesign.
