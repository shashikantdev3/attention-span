OpenAI publishes Signals data on how people use ChatGPT for work in nearly 100 countries

OpenAI released data exploring 165 work activities across nearly 100 countries to show how people use ChatGPT for work.

OpenAI Economic Research released data covering 165 work activities across nearly 100 countries to reveal how consumers use ChatGPT for work. The OpenAI Signals data spans the first quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026. It connects ChatGPT usage to Intermediate Work Activities using a standardized framework called O*NET. The release helps researchers study AI roles in the economy, track changes over time, and compare ChatGPT use across different countries. The data covers individual ChatGPT plans and excludes enterprise messages and Codex use while protecting user privacy.

Why it matters

Understanding how users apply AI across different countries provides empirical benchmarks for analyzing labor trends and economic integration.
