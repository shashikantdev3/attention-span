KPMG Reveals AI Agents Sometimes Asked Better Questions in Audit Trial

KPMG revealed that AI agents examining a FTSE 250 company's accounts sometimes asked better questions than its human audit team.

KPMG UK conducted an experiment codenamed Project Exponential, using more than 100 AI agents to examine the financial statements of an unnamed FTSE 250 company. KPMG head of audit Cath Burnet stated that the technology in several instances went deeper into issues and asked better questions than human auditors. The agents collected information, analysed data, drafted conclusions, and worked alongside one another. The experiment was not a formally approved statutory audit, and human oversight remained necessary. KPMG plans to expand the initiative to as many as 20 larger audits.
