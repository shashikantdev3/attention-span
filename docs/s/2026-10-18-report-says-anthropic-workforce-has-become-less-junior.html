Report says Anthropic workforce has become less junior

A new report shows Anthropic is hiring fewer junior staff as AI agents log six times as many hours as humans.

A report on employment at Anthropic shows that the company has been hiring for a smaller share of junior technical positions. By August, interactive AI agent hours outnumbered employee hours six to one. The share of junior-level employees in technical departments slipped from 21% to 14% since early 2025. The proportion of junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9%. As AI agents diffused through the company, output rose and hiring managers increasingly emphasized autonomy and judgment over specific technical expertise.
