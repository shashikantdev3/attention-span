LangChain raises $300 million Series C at $3 billion valuation

LangChain raised a $300 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation, led by CapitalG, and claims 7,000 LangSmith customers.

LangChain, an agent management startup, has raised a $300 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation. CapitalG led the round. GIC, DFJ Growth, IVP, and Sapphire Ventures also took part. The valuation is more than double the $1.25 billion it had when it raised $125 million about a year ago. LangChain started in 2022 as an open source project. It now focuses on managing AI agents through its LangSmith software. The company says LangSmith does not lock users into any particular model, cloud, or ecosystem. It claims 7,000 LangSmith customers, including Harvey, Rippling, Clay, Nvidia, Toyota, and Salesforce.

Like I am 5

Imagine you have many toy robot helpers, and you need one control box to tell them what to do. LangChain makes that control box, and it works with any brand of robot.

Why it matters

Teams building agents get a well-funded option that says it works across any model, cloud, or ecosystem, which may reduce lock-in to one vendor.
