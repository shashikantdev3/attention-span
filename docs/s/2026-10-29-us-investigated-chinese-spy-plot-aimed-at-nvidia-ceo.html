US investigated a Chinese spy plot aimed at Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang

United States intelligence agencies investigated a top executive of a Singapore AI cloud firm over a potential Chinese spy plot involving Nvidia's CEO.

Anonymous sources told the New York Times that a top executive of a Singapore AI cloud firm named Megaspeed could have been a Chinese spy. The executive, Alice Huang, was photographed with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang during business dinners in the Singapore region. U.S. intelligence agencies surveilled Alice Huang for about a year over concerns that she grew close enough to the CEO to gather information about Nvidia plans and chip export efforts. In 2025, the Commerce Department investigated Megaspeed over concerns that it helped Chinese companies bypass AI chip export regulations.
