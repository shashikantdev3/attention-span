OpenAI Q3 revenue reportedly hits $10 billion, still behind Anthropic's $17 billion

Reports say OpenAI's Q3 revenue grew about 50 percent to $10 billion, but it posted a $10.9 billion adjusted operating loss and trails Anthropic.

The Wall Street Journal and The Information report that OpenAI grew revenue about 50 percent quarter over quarter in Q3 to $10 billion. Adjusted operating losses rose from $10.1 billion to $10.9 billion. Anthropic brought in $17 billion over the same period, with an operating loss of $567 million. OpenAI is cutting prices on new models, has put more resources into Codex, and hired Dali Rajic as head of sales. Greg Brockman said the enterprise business doubled in Q3. ChatGPT has 100 million paying subscribers.
