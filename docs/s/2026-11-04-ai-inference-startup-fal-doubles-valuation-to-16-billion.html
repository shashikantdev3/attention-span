AI inference startup Fal doubles its valuation to $16B

AI inference startup Fal raised a $1.3 billion funding round, doubling its valuation to $16 billion amid surging demand.

San Francisco-based startup Fal hosts and runs AI video, image, and audio models. The company raised $1.3 billion in its latest funding round, led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with participation from Nvidia, Sound Ventures, and T. Rowe Price. The deal values Fal at $16 billion. This doubles its $8 billion valuation from April. The startup announced its Series E and F rounds at the same time, noting a combined $1.65 billion was raised across both rounds. Sequoia led the earlier round. The Series F round comes amid soaring demand for inference services, particularly from customers relying on open source models.
