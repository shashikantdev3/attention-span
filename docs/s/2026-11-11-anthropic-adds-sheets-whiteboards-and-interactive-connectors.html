Anthropic adds Sheets, Whiteboards and Interactive Connectors to Claude, with Meetings coming soon

Anthropic is adding spreadsheet, whiteboard, dashboard and partner app features to Claude, and says a call-joining Meetings tool is coming soon.

Anthropic is adding several collaboration features to the Claude app. Claude Sheets lets teams create and edit spreadsheets with AI assistance, including formulas and multiple tabs. Claude Whiteboards lets teams brainstorm and have Claude summarize the ideas. Claude Data, formerly Dashboards, connects to tools like Snowflake and Salesforce to build dashboards and data visualizations. Interactive Connectors let partners like Adobe, Atlassian, and Salesforce embed their apps in Claude. Anthropic says Claude Meetings, which can join calls and take notes, is launching soon. Enterprise customers get audit logs, isolated virtual machines, and a compliance API.
