Nvidia's board reverses course and reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, ten days after his resignation

Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring Jensen Huang back as president and CEO, saying senior US officials gave input and it sees no national security risk.

Nvidia said its board voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director, effective immediately. The board said it was satisfied with his candor and ability to lead, and that after input from senior levels of the US government it is confident his return poses no national security risk. Huang resigned on November 13 after reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman whom, according to those reports, US officials suspect of spying for China, and only days earlier the board chair had said there was no plan to bring him back, TechCrunch and CNBC report. A committee of independent directors with outside counsel will keep investigating, Nvidia says it has formed a National Security and Export Control Committee and is starting a search for a new Chief Compliance Officer, and Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO, stays on as CFO. Semafor reported, per TechCrunch, that President Trump dialed into a board meeting to push for Huang's return, and Trump said the government has no evidence of unlawful conduct. CNBC reports Nvidia shares closed up 31%, and TechCrunch says Huang is scheduled to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on December 3 on export controls.
