NVIDIA NVL72 Systems Connect All 72 GPUs in a Rack

NVIDIA NVL72 systems connect all 72 GPUs in a rack with an NVLink fabric, allowing data exchange with higher bandwidth.

Distributed training jobs running over InfiniBand instead of NVLink can degrade performance. Tensor parallelism requires synchronization steps that sit on the critical path, and Hugging Face measured a 43 percent drop in training throughput when moving off NVLink onto InfiniBand. NVIDIA NVL72 systems connect all 72 GPUs in a rack with a unified NVLink fabric. Maximizing these benefits depends on workload placement, because multi-node jobs spread across racks remain bottlenecked by InfiniBand. Preserving rack-locality is difficult when hardware failures take nodes offline. The SkyPilot Platform keeps multi-node jobs rack-local through topology-aware placement, in-place recovery, and automatic defragmentation.
