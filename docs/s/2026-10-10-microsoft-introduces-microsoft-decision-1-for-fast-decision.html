Microsoft Introduces Microsoft-Decision-1 for Fast Decision-Scoring

Microsoft introduced Microsoft-Decision-1, a new model for fast decision-scoring designed for routing, classification, prioritization, verification, and workflow control.

Microsoft introduced Microsoft-Decision-1, a decision-scoring model available in Microsoft Foundry and through OpenRouter. The model is built by post-training Qwen3.5-9B for single-pass decision scoring, and Microsoft plans to rebase it on other models including Microsoft AI and OpenAI. It supports yes/no, multiple-choice, rating options, and rubric-based grading through a simple structured API call. In Microsoft’s own benchmarks, Microsoft-Decision-1 had the highest accuracy and outperformed both large language models and other decision models, including being 35 times quicker than GPT-6 Sol and 2.5 times quicker than H2O-Lightning-4B v1.1.
