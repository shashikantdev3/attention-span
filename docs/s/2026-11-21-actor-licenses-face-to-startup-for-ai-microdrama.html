Actor Licenses Face to Startup for AI Microdrama

Actor Aki Avni licensed his face to microdrama startup Shortical to create a digital avatar for an AI-generated series.

Microdrama startup Shortical produced an entirely AI-generated series called "Inevitable," starring actor Aki Avni through a digital avatar of his face. Shortical makes money primarily through subscriptions, offering bite-sized, melodramatic episodes. The company is now encouraging other people to license their faces by submitting social media handles for potential contracts. Similar face-licensing trends are already popular in China. While Shortical hopes to draw more attention to face licensing, industry creatives and audiences express distrust and concern that automation is taking work away from young people.
