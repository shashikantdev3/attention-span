Village study: open models now best value at only 3% of budgets

Village's LLM Atlas finds open-weight models are the best buy at 3% of budgets, down from 41% in June, as closed models took over.

Village's LLM Atlas maps the strongest AI model at every price point. An open-weight model is now the best buy at 3% of budgets, mostly at the cheap end, held by Gemma 4 E4B and MiMo-V2.6-Flash. In June the share was 41%. Two OpenAI releases, GPT-6 Luna in September and GPT-6.1 Terra in October, took most of it back, alongside Claude Haiku 5.5. Village says this is the lowest share it has measured. Open models have won in bursts before, then lost share when closed labs shipped cheaper tiers.

Like I am 5

Imagine a lemonade stand contest where one kid had the best deal at lots of prices in June. Then other kids made cheaper, better lemonade, and now that first kid wins only at the very cheapest prices.

Why it matters

When picking a model for a budget, closed models now give the best value at almost every price, so open-weight models need a closer look on cost, including self-hosting.
