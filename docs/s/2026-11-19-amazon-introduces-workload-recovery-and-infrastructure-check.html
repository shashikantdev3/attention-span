Amazon Introduces Workload Recovery and Infrastructure Checks on SageMaker HyperPod

Amazon introduces workload recovery and infrastructure checks on SageMaker HyperPod to catch hidden training failures sooner and raise goodput.

Amazon has introduced workload recovery and expanded infrastructure checks on Amazon SageMaker HyperPod. Large training jobs often fail even when nodes look healthy due to subtle GPU faults, hung jobs on NCCL timeouts, or crashed Kubernetes device plugins. These silent failures lower goodput, which is the share of cluster time spent on useful training. The three new capabilities include MultiNodeTrainingValidation to check training correctness, the HyperPod Kubernetes Resource Monitoring Agent to watch the Kubernetes layer, and hung job detection to catch stalled Slurm jobs and automatically requeue them.

Like I am 5

Sometimes a machine looks fine on the outside, but its brain is making mistakes or freezing up. These new checks act like a teacher constantly testing the computers so they do not waste time running in circles.

Why it matters

Catching silent training failures early prevents wasted compute resources and reduces the financial cost of stalled jobs.
