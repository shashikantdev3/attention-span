Startup funding roundup: Onodrim, Mavria, T-Therapeutics and Boson AI raise capital

Onodrim raised a $300 million Series A at a $1.3 billion valuation, while Mavria, T-Therapeutics and Boson AI also announced funding.

A November 9, 2026 funding roundup lists several deals. Amsterdam-based Onodrim Industries raised a $300 million Series A at a $1.3 billion valuation. Texas-based Mavria has $130 million in cumulative funding to build factory-built data centers. Its Terrain system uses standardized five-megawatt modules, and its first unit is under construction in Houston, with testing expected in 2027. UK-based T-Therapeutics raised $133 million in Series B financing for cancer and autoimmune treatments. California-based Boson AI has accumulated $70 million to develop interactive voice models. The article notes funding alone is not proof of commercial success.
