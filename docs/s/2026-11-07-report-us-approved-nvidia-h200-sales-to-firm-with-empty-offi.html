Report: US approved Nvidia H200 sales to firm with empty office in China

The New York Times reports the US approved Nvidia H200 chip sales to New Matrix, a Chinese firm with an empty office and ties to a blacklisted company.

The New York Times reports that New Matrix is on the list of Chinese firms approved to handle Nvidia's H200 AI chips. Its offices are empty, and the paper found little evidence the company exists. It was created 17 months ago. Its owners have ties to a Chinese private equity firm blacklisted by the US government since 2024. The Times says this is a pattern, not a rare lapse. Concerns are growing that neither the Commerce Department nor Nvidia is doing due diligence when vetting these firms.
