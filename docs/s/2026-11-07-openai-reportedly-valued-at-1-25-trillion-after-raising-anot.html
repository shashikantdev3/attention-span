OpenAI reportedly valued at $1.25 trillion after raising another $11 billion

Bloomberg reports OpenAI is valued at $1.25 trillion in a new round that has raised another $11 billion, while Anthropic has filed its S-1.

Bloomberg reports that OpenAI is now valued at $1.25 trillion in a new funding round that has raised another $11 billion. In March, OpenAI raised $122 billion at a valuation of $852 billion. Bloomberg says OpenAI is still seeking more investment that could lift the amount to $20 billion. It had first hoped to raise as much as $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation, before tech stocks dropped and the midterm results showed public opinion turning against AI companies and data center projects. TechCrunch says the round has not closed but is expected to in the coming weeks. Rival Anthropic filed its S-1 to go public last week.
