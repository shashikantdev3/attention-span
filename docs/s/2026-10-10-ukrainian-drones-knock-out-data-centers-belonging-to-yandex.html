Ukrainian Drones Knock Out Data Centers Belonging to Yandex

Ukrainian drone strikes knocked out two Yandex data centers, leaving the Russian tech giant struggling to maintain its online services.

Ukrainian drone strikes have knocked out two of five data centers belonging to the Russian tech giant Yandex. Known as Russia’s Google because it operates the country's most popular search engine, Yandex is now struggling to maintain its AI chatbot and other online services. The strikes hit data centers in the Sasovo and Kaluga regions on October 8 and October 9, according to Reuters. The action was a direct response to Russian drone strikes targeting Ukraine’s data centers. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy confirmed the symmetrical response in a post on X.
