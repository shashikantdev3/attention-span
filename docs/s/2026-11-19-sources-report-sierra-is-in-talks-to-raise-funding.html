Sources Report Sierra Is in Talks to Raise Funding

Sources report that Bret Taylor's AI customer support startup Sierra is in talks to raise about $1.5 billion at a valuation of about $30 billion.

Sierra, an artificial intelligence customer support startup founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, has been in talks to raise approximately $1.5 billion. The potential funding round would value the company at approximately $30 billion. This valuation marks an increase from $15.8 billion in May. Additionally, the company stated in October that it reached over $400 million in annualized revenue.
