Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress named interim

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO, president and board member after a board investigation into reported affairs, and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.

Jensen Huang resigned as CEO, president and board member of Nvidia, effective immediately, according to a statement dated November 12, 2026. The Verge reports the board had opened an investigation into multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a direct report and one with an alleged Chinese spy. The New York Times reported a second affair on Wednesday, and a board meeting that night ended with his resignation. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Harvey Jones is the first Chair of the Board. The Decoder reports the scandal wiped out $1.4 trillion in market value.
