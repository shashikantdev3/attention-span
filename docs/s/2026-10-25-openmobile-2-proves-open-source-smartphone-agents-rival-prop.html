OpenMobile-2 releases open smartphone agent models, training data and environment

Researchers released OpenMobile-2, an open-source smartphone agent trained on over 110 simulated apps that rivals proprietary models.

Researchers from Nanjing University, SenseTime, and other universities built OpenMobile-2 to close the gap between open and proprietary smartphone agents. They released two agent models built on Qwen with 9 billion and 27 billion parameters, along with their training environment and data. The team rebuilt 35 popular apps and used an Android emulator to cover more than 110 applications. Training on diverse apps pushed the success rate on unseen applications from 60.7 to 67.5 percent while holding data volume constant. On real Android devices, the larger model scored 59.6 points on SPA-Bench.

Like I am 5

Researchers trained a robot phone helper by letting it practice in a fake playground with over 110 different toy apps, which helped it learn better than just looking at a huge pile of data from only a few apps.

Why it matters

Increasing app diversity in training environments improves smartphone agent success on unseen applications more effectively than adding data volume.
