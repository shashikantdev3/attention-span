LVMH Media Division Partners With OpenAI for AI Integration

LVMH Group's Media Division and OpenAI signed a strategic agreement to deploy AI tools and ChatGPT Enterprise across publications.

LVMH Group's Media Division and OpenAI announced a strategic agreement on November 16, 2026. The partnership brings OpenAI tools and ChatGPT Enterprise to teams across the media division. The goal is to help journalists with document research, document comparison, and preliminary translation. This allows journalists to spend more time on investigative reporting and analysis. The agreement also enhances the presence of content from LVMH Group's Media Division in ChatGPT. ChatGPT will provide summaries and excerpts with source identification and direct links to original articles.
