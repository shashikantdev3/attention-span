Anthropic pays a news publisher for the first time: a licensing deal with the AP, Adweek reports

Anthropic signed its first content licensing deal with the Associated Press to provide real-time election data for Claude.

Anthropic signed a content and data licensing deal with the Associated Press just before the midterms. This marks the first time the artificial intelligence firm has paid a digital news publisher to license its content. Real-time election data from the AP allowed Claude to surface timely updates on Election Night. Previously, Anthropic had resisted formal publisher partnerships. The financial specifics and timeframe of the arrangement were not disclosed by either party. The AP is a non-partisan wire service and was also the first publisher with whom Google and OpenAI signed licensing agreements.
