OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet so agents can pay without seeing card details

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, which lets agents pay with payment tokens instead of real card details and requires a security check for every purchase.

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, 2026. It lets agents pay on a user's behalf without access to the user's actual card details. Users add a card once, then review the items, total and card before approving each purchase with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan. The payment network supplies a payment token in place of the real card. It was built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. It starts in dots for ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older in the US with eligible US-issued cards. OpenAI also published a Commerce Partner Playbook for businesses.

Like I am 5

Imagine a helper who goes shopping for you but carries a pretend coin instead of your real wallet. You say yes to each buy first, and the shop never sees your real money.

Why it matters

Agents can now complete purchases through existing checkout flows using payment tokens, so teams building shopping or checkout systems must make sure agents are not blocked and that risk systems recognize legitimate agent purchases.
