Huawei releases MACaQ internal quantizer for local inference

Huawei released MACaQ, a model-agnostic quantizer for local inference supporting text, image, video, and audio models across multiple backends.

Huawei released MACaQ, an internal quantizer for local inference across text, image, video, and audio models. It supports backends like llama.cpp, vLLM, Diffusers, and PyTorch. The tool can quantize a 122B-parameter model in 64 minutes using 32.5 GB of device memory. It matches Llama-3-8B perplexity with 11 times less quantization time compared to other methods. The project includes MACaQ-R for refining weight codes in quantized GGUF formats and provides W4A4 support for various generation tasks. MACaQ is licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
