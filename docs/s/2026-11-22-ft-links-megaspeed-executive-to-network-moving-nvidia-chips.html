FT says the Megaspeed executive in the Nvidia case was part of a network of gaming firms and shell companies that moved Nvidia chips to China

The Financial Times reports links between the Megaspeed executive, gaming tycoons, hundreds of shell companies and an alleged Prince Group figure.

The Financial Times reported on Saturday that the former Megaspeed executive at the centre of the Nvidia scandal had links to gaming tycoons, hundreds of shell companies and an alleged figure from Cambodia's sanctioned Prince Group, which together formed a network supplying Nvidia chips to China. According to the FT's sources, Megaspeed began as an offshore shell branded as a neocloud provider, with the executive as its trusted public face. The New York Times separately traced Chinese state money to firms tied to her family. These are reported allegations, and she has not been charged. Megaspeed denies illegally shipping chips to China.
