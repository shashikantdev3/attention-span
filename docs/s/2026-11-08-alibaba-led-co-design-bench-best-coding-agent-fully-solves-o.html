Alibaba-led Co-Design Bench: best coding agent fully solves only 26.4 percent of tasks

A new benchmark built from real user requests shows the best of ten coding agents fully completes only 26.4 percent of tasks.

Researchers from Alibaba and several Asian universities built Co-Design Bench, a coding-agent test based on real user change requests. Agents must rework existing web apps, sometimes from images, audio, video or documents. GPT-6 Astra, the best of ten models, meets about 70 percent of a task's requirements on average but is fully correct on 26.4 percent of tasks. Older benchmarks show scores above 99 percent. Results drop as tasks mix more change types. Qwen3.8-27B scored 75 or 56 percent on larger overhauls depending on the harness. The authors say to report model and environment together.

Like I am 5

Imagine a helper who tidies your room but always leaves one toy on the floor. Most of the work is done, but you still have to check and finish it.

Why it matters

Picking a coding agent means testing the model and its working environment as a pair, because high scores on older benchmarks do not show how much manual cleanup is needed.
