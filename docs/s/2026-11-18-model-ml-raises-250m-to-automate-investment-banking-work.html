Model ML raises $250M to automate investment banking work

London startup Model ML raised $250 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation to automate investment banking grunt work.

Model ML is a London-based startup that uses AI to automate work traditionally performed by junior bankers and consultants. This includes building financial models, preparing pitch decks, and writing due diligence reports. The company raised $250 million in Series B funding at a $1.5 billion valuation. General Atlantic and QED Investors co-led the round, which follows a $75 million Series A round announced a year prior. Brothers Chaz and Arnie Englander co-founded the startup. Model ML develops AI agents that integrate with client tools like Excel and PowerPoint to automatically update financial models and presentations.
