United Airlines Bans AI Agents From Booking Flights

United Airlines banned personal artificial intelligence agents from booking flights, citing security risks, privacy concerns, and potential federal rule violations.

United Airlines announced a ban on artificial intelligence agents making flight reservations on behalf of travelers. The carrier cited security risks, privacy concerns, and the possibility that third-party AI tools could violate federal rules. Sharing credentials with personal AI assistants can expose sensitive information. It can also lead to unintended purchases or account changes. Using AI tools to check in could also violate federal regulations requiring travelers to verify that luggage lacks banned substances. United is restricting access on its digital platforms and via travel agencies. Southwest Airlines has taken a different approach by making its inventory viewable to ChatGPT users.
