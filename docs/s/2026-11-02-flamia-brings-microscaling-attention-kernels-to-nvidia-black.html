Yandex Research's FLAMIA brings microscaling attention kernels to NVIDIA Blackwell

FLAMIA is a family of attention kernels for NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs that speeds up Transformer training using MXFP8 and mixed NVFP4/FP8 paths.

Attention remains a high computational cost in Transformer training, especially at long context lengths. NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs offer high-throughput low-precision matrix multiplication, but using it requires addressing quantization overhead and other issues. Researchers presented FLAMIA, a family of attention kernels for Blackwell with MXFP8 and mixed NVFP4/FP8 training paths. The design combines token-block scaling, safeguards against leakage, and consistent forward and backward computations. The MXFP8 path accelerates the combined forward and backward attention call by factors of 1.10 to 1.40 times on B200 and 1.06 to 1.42 times on B300. Training stability and quality preservation were validated on Llama-style models.
