Mistral releases Mistral Large 4 and adds managed agents to Mistral Studio

Mistral AI launched the Mistral Large 4 model and expanded its enterprise platform with managed agents, workflows, and application hosting features.

Mistral AI released Mistral Large 4, also known as Le Chonk, which provides coding, cybersecurity, and document reasoning capabilities. The company also expanded Mistral Studio, an enterprise platform for deploying agents, workflows, and applications. New features include managed agents for multi-step tasks, visual workflow graphs, and enterprise-grade hosting with authentication and access controls. Developers can deploy these systems in cloud, VPC, or on-premises environments. Current customers using Studio include ABANCA, BNP Paribas, Accenture, and Tata Consultancy Services. The platform allows teams to build, test, and deploy AI solutions while maintaining control over infrastructure and data.
