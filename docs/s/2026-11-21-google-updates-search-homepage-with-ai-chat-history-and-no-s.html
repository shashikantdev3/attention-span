Google Updates Search Homepage with AI Chat History and No Search Button

Google rolled out a redesigned desktop search homepage that drops the dedicated search button and adds AI chat history and feature buttons.

Google is updating its desktop search homepage on Google.com after a successful test. The new design removes the traditional search button that sat next to the I'm Feeling Lucky button. Google search VP Robby Stein confirmed the rollout on X. The updated page includes AI chat history and several new buttons under the search bar for AI features. These include buttons for image generation, file analysis, and brainstorming with Gemini. The search button was dropped because autocomplete suggestions covered it in the modern interface, requiring an extra click instead of simply pressing the Enter key.
