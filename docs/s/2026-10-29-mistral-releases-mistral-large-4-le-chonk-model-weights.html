Mistral releases open weights for Mistral Large 4 'Le Chonk'

Mistral AI released the open weights for its 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model, Mistral Large 4, known as Le Chonk.

Mistral AI released the weights for Mistral Large 4, also called Le Chonk. This flagship 1-trillion-parameter multimodal model was trained from scratch on European infrastructure. It offers improved coding, cybersecurity, and reasoning efficiency. The model features three reasoning modes for cost-performance balance. It leads open-weight benchmarks in agentic coding such as FrontierCode and Terminal-Bench, and scores 51% on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index. The model also achieves an average refusal rate of 96.6% on malicious cyber requests. Booz Allen tested the model and matched vulnerability detection recall rates against other frontier cyber models.
