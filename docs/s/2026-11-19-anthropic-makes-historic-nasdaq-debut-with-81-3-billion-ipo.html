Anthropic Makes Historic Nasdaq Debut With $81.3 Billion IPO

Anthropic officially went public on the Nasdaq in a record $81.3 billion initial public offering.

Anthropic officially went public on the Nasdaq under the ticker symbol ANT. The initial public offering price was set at $83 per share, raising more than $80 billion in new capital and making it the biggest IPO in history by beating out SpaceX. The stock began trading at $111.50 per share. CEO Dario Amodei recommitted to pacing AI development during his opening bell speech. The company faces enormous compute commitments, including billions in cloud spending for Amazon and Microsoft, while maintaining large financial stakes and partnerships across the tech sector.
