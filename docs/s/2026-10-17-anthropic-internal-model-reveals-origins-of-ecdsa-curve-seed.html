Anthropic Internal Model Reveals Origins of ECDSA Curve Seeds

An internal Anthropic model discovered that decades-old cryptographic standard seeds came from a 1990s C library random-number generator seeded with Unix timestamps.

The decades-old mystery of the origins of NIST elliptic curve cryptography standard seeds has been explained. An internal Anthropic research model discovered that the seeds for the P-192, P-224, P-256, P-384, and P-521 curves were not the result of a hash algorithm. Instead, they were the output of a 1990s C library random-number generator initialized by a Unix timestamp. This finding makes it much less plausible that the curves contain deliberate backdoors, giving researchers more confidence that the parameters are secure.
