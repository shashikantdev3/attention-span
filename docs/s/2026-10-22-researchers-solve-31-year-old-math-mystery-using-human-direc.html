Researchers Solve 31-Year-Old Math Mystery Using Human-Directed AI

Researchers have resolved the 31-year-old Kannan-Lovász-Simonovits conjecture using a human-directed AI research process.

Researchers, including Xinzhi Zhang from Microsoft Research, have resolved the Kannan-Lovász-Simonovits conjecture, a 31-year-old problem in high-dimensional geometry and probability. Proposed in 1995, the conjecture asks whether the best flat cut is always nearly as effective as the best cut of any shape for high-dimensional probability distributions. The breakthrough emerged from a human-directed agentic research process where AI systems explored mathematical possibilities while researchers evaluated, pruned, and redirected the search. This work removes a major theoretical obstacle to understanding the efficiency of randomized algorithms used across science and computing.
