Anomaly Innovations Reports Breach Affecting OpenCode Console Users

Anomaly Innovations says a large-scale distillation attack breached about 1.4 percent of OpenCode Console users and their API keys.

Anomaly Innovations, the company behind the coding agent OpenCode, reported a security breach. According to Startup Fortune, the incident affected roughly 1.4 percent of OpenCode Console users. The breach also impacted their API keys. The company described the incident as a large-scale distillation attack.
