Artificial Analysis Releases Intelligence Index v5 With Private Datasets

Artificial Analysis released Intelligence Index v5, its largest upgrade since January, featuring private datasets, two new benchmarks, and three upgraded tests.

Artificial Analysis announced Intelligence Index v5, a composite measure of model capabilities across tasks reflecting real-world usage. For the first time, the majority of the Index relies on private datasets, with 60% based on evaluations with private test sets or held-out answers. Version 5 introduces two new benchmarks, FrontierCode 2.0 and Terminal-Bench-Science, alongside three upgrades including HLE-Diamond, AA-Omniscience v1.1, and CritPt v2. Claude Opus 5.5 (max) leads the overall Index at 58, ahead of GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol. The open weights gap widens from 3 points to 12 points, and the cost per task varies by more than 13x among leading models.
