Report claims Google Gemini sends connected Drive and Gmail content to human reviewers

A GitHub write-up claims Google now routes personal Drive files and emails from Gemini's Connected Apps to human reviewers, reversing a 2023 promise.

A GitHub post says Google reversed its 2023 promise that personal Gmail, Docs and Drive content "is not seen by human reviewers." It cites updated Google Help Answer 16836988 and an October 16 edit to the 2023 blogpost. Google warns reviewers may see "the email or file." The author says Gemini pulls Workspace data in background chat without @Drive or @Gmail tags. Tests gave four outcomes: no retrieval, a summary, a partial quote, or the full file. The author says Google's scrubbers cannot fully deidentify narrative data.

Imagine you tell a helper to keep your diary private, but now the helper says a few other people may peek at pages while checking its work. The helper also sometimes grabs a page without being asked.

Why it matters

Anyone connecting Gmail or Drive to Gemini should assume the content could reach human reviewers, and should keep confidential material out of connected accounts.
