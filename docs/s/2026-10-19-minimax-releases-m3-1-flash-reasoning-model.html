MiniMax Releases M3.1 Flash Reasoning Model

MiniMax released M3.1 Flash, a new reasoning model that scores 42 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at a lower cost.

MiniMax has released M3.1 Flash, a reasoning model that scores 42 points on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This is 13 points higher than its predecessor M3 at less than half the cost per task. The model handles up to one million tokens of context and accepts text and images. API pricing is set at $0.15 per million input tokens and $0.60 per million output tokens, with cached inputs at $0.015. MiniMax plans to release the model as open weights on October 23. The API is live at platform.minimax.io, and the company offers a desktop app called MiniMax Code.
