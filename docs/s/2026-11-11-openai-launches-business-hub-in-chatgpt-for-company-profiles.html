OpenAI launches Business Hub in ChatGPT for company profiles and agents

OpenAI launched a beta Business Hub in ChatGPT where companies can verify profiles, track user interactions and, if eligible, launch a business agent.

OpenAI rolled out a Business Hub in ChatGPT on Tuesday. Companies can create business profiles to verify and update information about their products and services. The feature is in beta. Eligible companies can also launch a business agent that lets ChatGPT users ask questions about the business and its products. The Hub gives limited insights, including profile views, business mentions and website clicks. Profiles must be verified, but OpenAI did not share specifics. The Decoder reports that Business Agents are for eligible US e-commerce providers. In March, OpenAI retired an earlier commission-based checkout effort.
