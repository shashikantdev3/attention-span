Meta and Microsoft Reduce Employee Usage of Claude AI

Meta and Microsoft have reduced employee usage of Anthropic's Claude AI as they pivot toward their own internal coding tools.

Meta and Microsoft have started reducing employee usage of Anthropic's Claude AI as they shift toward their own proprietary coding tools. Microsoft lowered its internal monthly AI spending limits per employee from $100,000 to about $10,000 and directed staff to use GitHub Copilot and OpenAI frameworks. At Meta, Claude Code users dropped from about 60,000 to 30,000, while Meta's internal MetaCode tool reached over 30,000 users and Muse Code reached over 6,000. Both companies remain clients, and customer access to Claude through Microsoft is not being discontinued.
