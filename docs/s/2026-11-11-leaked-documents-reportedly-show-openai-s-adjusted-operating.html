Leaked documents reportedly show OpenAI's adjusted operating margin improved to -109% in Q3

Leaked documents reported by the Wall Street Journal say OpenAI's adjusted operating margin rose from -150% to -109% between Q2 and Q3.

The Wall Street Journal reports, citing leaked documents, that OpenAI's adjusted operating margin improved from -150% in the second quarter to -109% in the third quarter. The report says the company remains far off from profitability. It also says OpenAI grew revenue by roughly 50% to $10 billion from the second to the third quarter. The Information, citing a source, reports that OpenAI told investors on Tuesday that revenue jumped nearly 50% to $10 billion in Q3. That compares with 17% growth in Q2. The Information says growth rebounded from a period of slower growth. These figures come from leaks and sources, not from an official company release.
