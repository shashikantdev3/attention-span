CoreWeave bond yield tops 16% as AI credit stress rises

CoreWeave's 2032 bond yield has climbed above 16%, and default protection costs are rising across AI-linked firms including Nvidia, Broadcom and Oracle.

CoreWeave's 2032 bond, which has a 9.625% coupon, now yields above 16%. Its five-year credit default swaps spiked above 1,289 basis points on Thursday, according to Deutsche Bank, then eased 8% Friday to around 1,155. CDS on Nvidia debt is up 76% in the last month, Broadcom's is up 49% and Amazon's is up 44%. Those levels remain low in absolute terms. Goldman Sachs says moratoriums could affect 45% of planned U.S. data center capacity. Goldman also tallies five AI bond deals totaling $67 billion since October 29.
