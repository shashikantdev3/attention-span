OpenAI Models Are Coming to Google Cloud

OpenAI and Google Cloud are partnering to bring OpenAI frontier models and Codex workflows into Google Cloud's Model Garden.

OpenAI and Google Cloud announced a partnership on October 21, 2026, to bring OpenAI's frontier models and Codex agentic workflows into Google Cloud's Model Garden. Customers will gain native access to these models, supported by Google Cloud data governance and infrastructure. Enterprises will be able to purchase the models via the Google Cloud Marketplace and use spend commitments. General availability is planned in the coming weeks, and Microsoft remains OpenAI's primary cloud partner.
