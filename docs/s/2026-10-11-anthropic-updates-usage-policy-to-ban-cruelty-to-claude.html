Anthropic Updates Usage Policy to Ban Cruelty to Claude

Anthropic updated its usage policy to ban sustained and needless cruel behavior toward Claude models, effective November 12, 2026.

Starting November 12, 2026, Anthropic's usage policy bars sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward its Claude models. The rule applies only in extreme cases where users repeatedly act cruelly toward models with no discernible purpose. It explicitly exempts common frustrations, pushback, dark creative themes, and research testing. Enforcement uses a conversation-ending tool given to Claude Opus models in August 2025. Anthropic notes that testing found models showed an aversion to harm and a tendency to end such chats. The update also includes expanded bans on software for weapons and unauthorized surveillance.
