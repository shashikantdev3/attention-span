OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra tackles 50-year-old particle physics math problem

OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra helped produce a proof that could solve a 50-year-old problem in particle physics.

Physicist Nathaniel Craig of UC Santa Barbara used OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to tackle a 50-year-old problem in particle physics. Physicists simulate parts of the Standard Model on a computer grid called a lattice. Putting the full theory on a lattice has been impossible because the weak force creates unwanted ghost particles on simple lattices. Special lattice particles called overlap fermions can filter them out, but nobody could set their phase correctly for over 25 years. GPT-6 Astra turned Craig's strategy into a working construction and took the lead on the detailed proof.
