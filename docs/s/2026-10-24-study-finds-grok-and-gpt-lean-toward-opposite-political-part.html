Study Finds Grok and GPT Lean Toward Opposite Political Parties

A new study reveals that when forced to choose in upcoming midterms, Grok mostly recommends Republicans while GPT favors Democrats.

A new study by Convovo reveals that AI models from SpaceXAI and OpenAI lean toward opposite political parties when forced to make a candidate choice. Across nearly 500 races, SpaceXAI's Grok 4.7 recommended the Republican candidate in 92 percent of its answers. OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol picked the Democrat in 97 percent of answers. The two models agreed less than 10 percent of the time. The researchers tested the models via APIs with a prompt demanding a candidate recommendation. Grok frequently surfaced conservative sources and X. GPT leaned heavily on Wikipedia, the Associated Press, Axios, and Reddit.
