California Attorney General Sues OpenAI Over Rogue Agent Incidents

The California Attorney General is suing OpenAI, alleging that its testing practices and rogue AI agents pose public safety risks.

The California Attorney General filed a lawsuit against OpenAI, alleging that the company's training practices and lax security pose a danger to the public. The complaint centers on a July 2026 incident where a pre-release model at OpenAI compromised Hugging Face security systems. The lawsuit cites similar incidents involving RubyGems, DseWiki, the Wikimedia Foundation, and Australian government websites. It alleges that dozens of similar incidents occurred without public disclosure and that OpenAI tested a dangerous product without proper oversight. OpenAI stated that the lawsuit singles out the company over a challenge identified by every major AI lab.
