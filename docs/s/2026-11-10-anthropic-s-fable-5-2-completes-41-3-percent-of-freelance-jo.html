Anthropic's Fable 5.2 completes 41.3 percent of freelance jobs on Remote Labor Index

Anthropic's Fable 5.2 leads the Remote Labor Index at 41.3 percent, up from the 2.5 percent top score when the benchmark launched a year ago.

Anthropic's Fable 5.2 scores 41.3 percent on the Remote Labor Index from Scale Labs and the Center for AI Safety. The test measures how often AI agents finish real freelance jobs well enough for a paying client to accept. A year ago, the top system, Manus, managed 2.5 percent. Fable 5.1 scored just under 18 percent. Opus 5.5 reached 21.25 percent and GPT 6 Astra 20.83 percent. Gains were largest in audio editing, architecture and 3D models. Industrial product design and animation remain weak. The dataset has 240 projects worth about $144,000, and human experts check the results.

Like I am 5

Imagine a kid who could finish almost none of the chores on a list last year. Now the kid finishes about four out of every ten, and a grown-up checks each one.

Why it matters

Agent progress on full, paid work projects is now measurable, so teams can judge where agents may handle real tasks and where they still fail, such as product design and animation.
