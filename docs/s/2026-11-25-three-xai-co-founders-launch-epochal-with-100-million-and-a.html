Three xAI co-founders launch Epochal, with $100 million and a 'Time Portal' of the past web for training forecasting agents

Epochal says its archive of more than 1 trillion timestamped pages lets AI agents browse the web as it looked on any date in the last 30 years.

Guodong Zhang, Zihang Dai and Manuel Kroiss, three co-founders of xAI, launched a new company called Epochal. TNW, citing Bloomberg, reports that Zhang put in $100 million of his own money and that no other investors have been named. Its first product, Time Portal, lets agents browse the web as it looked at any moment over the past 30 years; Epochal says the archive holds more than 1 trillion pages with creation timestamps, down to the minute for busy sites. The company says a matching Linux sandbox sets its clock, software and downloads to the same date, so models can be trained and tested on predicting events without seeing the future. In Epochal's own test, forecasts on 347 live Kalshi and Polymarket questions, replayed through Time Portal weeks later, had a correlation of 0.98 with the originals. A limited free tier is available, and TNW notes Epochal has not said how it gathered the archive or whether the pages are licensed.

Like I am 5

It is like a time machine for the internet: an AI can be dropped into last year's web and asked to guess what happens next, then checked against what really happened.

Why it matters

Forecasting is hard to train for because models already know how events turned out, and a dated web archive is one way around that, though where the data comes from is still an open question.
