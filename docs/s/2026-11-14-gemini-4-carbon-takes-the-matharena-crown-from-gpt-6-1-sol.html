Gemini 4 Carbon Takes the MathArena Crown From GPT-6.1 Sol

Google's Gemini 4 Carbon topped the MathArena math benchmark, knocking GPT-6.1 Sol from the top spot.

Google's Gemini 4 Carbon has taken the top spot on the MathArena math benchmark. It knocked GPT-6.1 Sol from the first place, while Anthropic's Fable 5.2 came in second. Gemini 4 Carbon is the first non-OpenAI model in a while to lead the math benchmark. However, Gemini 4 Carbon costs roughly 15 times as much as GPT-6.1 Sol with the reasoning settings used in testing. Fable 5.2 costs roughly 40 times as much. Gemini 4 Carbon's most common failure mode is exceeding the time limit. The model almost never gives up on a task, sometimes running scripts for more than eleven hours without checking progress.
