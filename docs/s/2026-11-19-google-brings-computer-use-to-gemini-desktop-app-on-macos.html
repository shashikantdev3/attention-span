Google brings computer use to Gemini desktop app on macOS

Google announced computer use for its Gemini desktop application on macOS, allowing the AI to complete tasks directly on user computers.

Google announced that it is bringing computer use to its Gemini desktop application on macOS. The feature allows the AI to complete tasks on users behalf, navigating native Mac apps and browsing websites in the background. Users can see the agent working in a live preview in their chat while keeping their screen and cursor free. Users can pause, stop, and approve Gemini actions before they happen. The feature is initially available in English in the U.S. and requires the Gemini app for Mac and a Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra subscription.
