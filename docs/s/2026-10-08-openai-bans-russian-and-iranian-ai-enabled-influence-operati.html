OpenAI Bans Russian and Iranian AI-Enabled Influence Operations

OpenAI banned two covert influence operations that used its models to create fake journalist personas and a fake think tank.

OpenAI banned two influence operations from Russia and Iran for violating usage policies. The Iranian operation used seven fake journalist personas to pitch articles to online outlets and generated social media comments. The Russian operation, nicknamed Dark Clark, used a fake think tank called the Social Research Center to spread content and fake documents. Both operations used AI to draft reports and content. OpenAI assessed the Russian operation as a Category 5 on its IO Breakout Scale and the Iranian operation as Category 4. These operations aimed to place content in mainstream media outlets to influence geopolitical narratives.
