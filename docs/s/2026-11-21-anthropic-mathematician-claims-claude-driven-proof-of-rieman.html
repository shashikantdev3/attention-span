Anthropic mathematician says Claude helped produce a Lean proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis; a human proof appeared hours later

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says a Claude-driven effort produced a machine-checked Lean proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, while a separate human proof was announced at Stanford the same day.

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says he used Claude to produce a proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, released as a very large Lean formalization rather than as a conventional paper. The Lean community published the formal development on GitHub and wrote about it on its blog, and TechCrunch and Scientific American reported the claim. Hours later, Stanford mathematician Ravi Vakil announced on his blog a separate, entirely human proof by Stanford postdoc Alessandro Masullo. In an update on 22 November, Vakil wrote that people had reported problems with that human proof. Neither claim has yet been through the usual expert review, which for results of this size is expected to take a long time.
