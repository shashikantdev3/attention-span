Anthropic Mathematician Claims Claude-Driven Proof of Riemann Hypothesis

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge claimed a Claude-driven proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, released as Lean code, while Alessandro Masullo released a human draft.

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge claimed an independent proof of the Riemann Hypothesis using Claude. The proof was released as 1.3GB of Lean code, equivalent to about 250,000 pages, rather than a full paper. Elliot Glazer confirmed the formalized statement is the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis and stated the chance of a Lean bug is well under 1 percent. Hours later, Alessandro Masullo released a 940-page, one hundred percent human work-in-progress proof he worked on for twelve years. Both AI-claimed Millennium Prize results, including OpenAI's September Navier-Stokes claim, will take years to verify.
