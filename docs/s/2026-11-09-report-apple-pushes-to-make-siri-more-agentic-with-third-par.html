Report: Apple pushes to make Siri more agentic, with third-party app control

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports Apple is pressing to speed up Siri, adding first-party app control soon and third-party app integration in the Spring.

Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says CEO John Ternus is pressuring AI czar Craig Federighi to speed up the Siri roadmap. The goal is a powerful AI agent that can compete with OpenAI's Dots or Meta's Muse. Two improvements are expected in the coming months. The first gives Siri fine-grained control over Apple's first-party apps, such as rotating and cropping images in Photos and organizing Safari tabs. It is expected with iOS 27.3. The second is third-party app integration, likely in the Spring, such as ordering through Grubhub or booking through OpenTable. Gurman says Apple wants Siri to handle ongoing tasks.

Like I am 5

Think of Siri as a helper who can only do one small chore when you ask. Apple wants a helper who can keep working on a bigger job by itself, using lots of different toys.

Why it matters

If the report is right, Siri could soon act inside third-party apps, which would make app integration with Apple's assistant something builders need to plan for.
