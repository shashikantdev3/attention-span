Google Reports Prototype Suncatcher Data Center Satellite is Online

Google launched its first prototype data center satellite, Suncatcher, which is currently online and running inference workloads in space.

Google's first prototype space data center satellite, Suncatcher, is online and communicating with the ground. It launched earlier this month on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket alongside more than 100 other spacecraft. The satellite carries four Google Tensor Processing Units and generates up to 1 kW of electricity. Due to power demands, it can only run one TPU for about 20 minutes at a time. Engineers tested the onboard Google DeepMind Gemma 4 model by asking it to write a haiku about being in space. The ongoing test campaign will examine how the chips perform under thermal stress and in the space radiation environment.
