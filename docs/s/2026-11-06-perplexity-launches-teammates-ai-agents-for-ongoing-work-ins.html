Perplexity launches teammates, AI agents for ongoing work inside Computer

Perplexity launched teammates, personal agents inside Computer that own long-running work, monitor projects, follow up, and bring results back for review.

Perplexity launched teammates, personal agents built to take on long-running work inside Computer. They plan projects, check results along the way, and bring completed work back for review. They remember project goals, task history, and shared files across runs. Teammates can be added to a Slack channel and can pass work to each other. Each runs in a separate environment with its own browser sessions, and saved credentials are encrypted. Teammates launch on the web and soon on iOS for Perplexity Max subscribers. Perplexity says it will expand access and features in the coming weeks.

Imagine a helper who keeps working on a big job for you, remembers what you told them, and shows you the finished work to check. You can step in and guide them any time.

Why it matters

Agents that keep memory across runs, work in isolated environments, and hand tasks to one another point to a model where AI owns ongoing workflows instead of answering one-off prompts.
