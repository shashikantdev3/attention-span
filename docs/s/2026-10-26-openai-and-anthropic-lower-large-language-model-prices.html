An analysis of how far OpenAI and Anthropic have cut LLM prices

OpenAI and Anthropic reduced their large language model prices across multiple releases over several months.

OpenAI and Anthropic released a series of model updates and price adjustments over several months. OpenAI cut the price of GPT-5.6 Luna by 80% and reduced GPT-5.6 Sol pricing by about 20% for three months. Anthropic made a launch price cut for Claude Sonnet 5 permanent, released Claude Opus 5.5 with reduced compute requirements, and introduced Fable 5.1 with lower cache read costs. Later, OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and Luna at half the input and output prices of previous versions, and then introduced GPT-6.1 Sol with a halved cache price.
