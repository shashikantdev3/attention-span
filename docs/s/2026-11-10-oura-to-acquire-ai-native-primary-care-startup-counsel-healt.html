Oura to acquire AI-native primary care startup Counsel Health for $625M

Oura announced it is acquiring Counsel Health, an AI-powered primary care company, for $625 million, weeks after postponing its IPO indefinitely.

Oura announced on Tuesday that it is acquiring Counsel Health for $625 million. Counsel offers a chat-based medical experience with access to licensed physicians, and it was integrated into the Oura app in June. The companies plan to build across preventative care, acute care, and specialty and chronic care. Counsel CEO Muthu Alagappan will become Oura's chief clinical AI officer, and Bloomberg reports the company's 70 employees will join too. Counsel will keep serving enterprise partners. The deal is expected to close before the end of 2027. Oura's revenue for the quarter ended September 30 was $533 million, up 153% year-over-year.
