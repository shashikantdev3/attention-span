Nebius plans $40 billion in 2027 spending as Microsoft site delays mount

Nebius laid out $40 billion in planned 2027 spending, funded by prepayments and contract-backed loans, while a delayed Microsoft site in New Jersey raises risk.

On its third-quarter call, Nebius said it plans $40 billion in spending for 2027, far above Wall Street expectations. It expects prepayments plus asset-backed financing to cover up to 85% of the $40 to $45 billion capex. Its Microsoft contract is worth up to $19.4 billion, but the Vineland, New Jersey site is months behind. Only just over half of that capacity is planned by year end, with the rest in Q2 2027. Nebius cut its 2026 revenue forecast by $200 million to $3 billion. Shares ended the day down 17%.
