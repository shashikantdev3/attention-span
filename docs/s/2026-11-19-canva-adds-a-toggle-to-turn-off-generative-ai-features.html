Canva Adds a Toggle to Turn Off Generative AI Features

Canva now lets free and paid users disable generative AI features and see less AI-generated content in its library.

Canva added a new toggle that allows users to turn off generative AI features. This follows user frustration over the platform's push into AI since 2023. The toggle disables tools like Magic Expand, Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Layers, Magic Edit, Dream Lab, Canva Code, AI Shapes, and the Canva chatbot. Some features remain available, including the background remover and Magic Grab, because they work only with existing design elements. Users can also switch on an option to see less AI-generated content in the library. Both free and paid users can access these settings.
