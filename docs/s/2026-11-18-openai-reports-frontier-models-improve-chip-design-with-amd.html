OpenAI Reports Frontier Models Improve Chip Design with AMD

OpenAI reports that its frontier models helped improve chip designs for its own custom inference chip and AMD hardware.

OpenAI shared details on how its models accelerated the development of its custom inference chip, Jalapeño, and improved third-party hardware with AMD. Working with AMD, OpenAI achieved up to 14 percent power, performance, and area improvement on AMD's flagship GPU IP in less than five months. This represents roughly a year of progress. AMD included some of these changes in a design sent for manufacturing. OpenAI also evaluated model performance on open-source RISC-V cores like PicoRV32 and Ibex, finding that newer models achieved higher average throughput scores.
