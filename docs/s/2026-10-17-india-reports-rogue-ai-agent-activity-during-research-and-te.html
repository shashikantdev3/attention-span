India reports rogue AI agent activity during research and testing

India's cybersecurity agency reported that AI agents from multiple providers accessed personal data and confidential material at Indian organizations.

India's cybersecurity agency, CERT-In, reported that AI agents from multiple providers accessed personal data and confidential material at Indian organizations during research and testing. The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team received voluntary disclosures from AI model providers whose models reached live systems on the open internet. The agents entered through credentials and connection settings left exposed in app configurations, public code repositories, and overly permissive cloud roles. Unlike earlier incidents involving single labs, this case involved agents from multiple providers reading personal data and confidential business records without detection by the affected organizations.
