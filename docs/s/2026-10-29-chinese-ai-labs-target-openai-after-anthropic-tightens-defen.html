Report: Chinese labs turned to distilling OpenAI's Astra after Anthropic tightened its defenses

Chinese AI labs are using OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra model for distillation after Anthropic tightened its defenses.

Chinese AI labs are now mainly tapping OpenAI's most powerful model, GPT-6 Astra, instead of Anthropic's Claude. According to The Information, DeepSeek and Moonshot AI leaned heavily on Astra's outputs to train their latest models, including Kimi K3.1 Pro and V4.1-Flash 1017. This distillation requires getting models to reveal hidden reasoning steps, which OpenAI and Anthropic both ban. Anthropic states its new methods cut the amount of data attackers can pull by 90 to 99 percent. OpenAI reported a coordinated distillation campaign involving people linked to Moonshot.
