New Framework Improves Consistency in Long-Form Video Generation

Researchers presented a world-state-centric framework called ATAS to improve spatial and temporal consistency in long-form agentic video generation.

Long-form agentic video generation requires turning a screenplay into shots that remain spatially and temporally consistent. Passing prompts or recent frames between shots fails to provide a shared spatial frame or time-indexed record. Researchers presented Any Time, Any Space, which compiles screenplays into shot requests with explicit location, camera, and event-time references. It uses a scene memory of static geometry and an event memory of dynamic observations. Across four video generators, the framework improves cross-shot consistency measures, achieves the highest aggregate benchmark score for each backbone, and receives the highest human ratings.
