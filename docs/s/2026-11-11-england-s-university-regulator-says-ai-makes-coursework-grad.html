England's university regulator says AI makes coursework grading "close to impossible"

The Office for Students, England's university regulator, says AI makes grading coursework "close to impossible" and recommends a return to "AI-free assessment" such as written exams.

England's university regulator, the Office for Students, says AI has made it "close to impossible" to grade coursework. It recommends that institutions in England return to traditional exams and in-person presentations, which it calls "AI-free assessment". The Guardian reported the story. The Decoder adds that an investigation found students having ChatGPT write entire papers undetected, without being able to recall basic content afterward. The Decoder also reports that universities face sanctions if they do not adopt AI-proof exam formats. The stated aim is to protect the credibility of degrees.
