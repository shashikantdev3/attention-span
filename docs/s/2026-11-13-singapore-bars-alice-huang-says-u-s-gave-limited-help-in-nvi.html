Singapore bars the Megaspeed executive linked to the Nvidia case and says the U.S. gave limited help in its chip probe

Singapore barred the Megaspeed executive from re-entering and said U.S. authorities gave limited help in its yearlong probe tied to Nvidia chip servers.

Singapore said Thursday it had barred the Megaspeed executive, former Megaspeed CEO, from re-entering the country after she gave inaccurate information about her role. Authorities found insufficient evidence that she committed offenses tied to the declarations under investigation. Singapore is examining whether servers exported through it to Malaysia were rerouted elsewhere, contrary to declarations. It said it received "limited information or assistance" from U.S. authorities about Super Micro Computer Inc. A fifth person was charged on Friday in a related fraud case. Prosecutors said the alleged transactions totaled about $390 million. Megaspeed has denied wrongdoing.
