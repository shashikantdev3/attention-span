Microsoft Offers Voluntary Buyouts to 60,000 Employees

Microsoft is offering voluntary buyouts to about 60,000 employees across four countries as the company pushes for capital efficiency.

Microsoft is offering a voluntary exit program to workers at experience level 70 or below in the United States, Canada, India, and the United Kingdom. According to a person familiar with the matter, around 60,000 employees are eligible for the program, which affects about 27 percent of the company's total workforce. The software maker stated it is striving to balance a costly artificial intelligence infrastructure build-up with capital efficiency. Employees must decide whether to take the offer in early December, and participants will leave on December 31.
