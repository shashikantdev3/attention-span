General Compute raises $100M to run inference on chips from Nvidia's rivals

General Compute raised $100 million in Series A funding to build a neocloud using specialized inference chips from Nvidia rivals.

General Compute secured $100 million in Series A funding led by Blackbird Ventures and Abstract Ventures, raising its valuation to $800 million. The startup focuses on inference chips built by Nvidia rivals, including SambaNova, Positron, and Cerebras. Instead of building massive data centers from scratch, General Compute rents space in other companies' data centers and converts former bitcoin-mining sites to run AI models. Co-founder and CEO Finn Puklowski expects the company to deploy 75 megawatts of computing capacity in 2027. The company provides bare metal servers to cloud companies that specialize in high-speed inference.
