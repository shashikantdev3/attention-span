OpenAI Launches Grid Protectors Program for Compute Campuses

OpenAI is launching the Grid Protectors Program to help future compute campuses support reliable and affordable electricity for communities.

OpenAI launched the Grid Protectors Program to support affordable and reliable electricity for communities where it builds future compute campuses. Working with utilities and regulators, the effort will develop arrangements for campuses to reduce electricity demand when the grid is under strain. U.S. data center capacity is projected to exceed 100 gigawatts by 2030. For a planned Effingham County campus, OpenAI committed to paying full infrastructure and electric-service costs, and Georgia Power says the flexibility can reduce the amount of new generation it needs to build. The agreement includes up to 1,000 megawatts of flexible demand response.
