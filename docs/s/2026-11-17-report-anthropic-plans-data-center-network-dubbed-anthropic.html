Report: Anthropic Plans Data Center Network Dubbed Anthropic Direct

Anthropic plans a data center network called Anthropic Direct, committing over $900 billion through 2045 and leasing TPUs.

According to documents seen by the Financial Times, Anthropic has data center network plans dubbed Anthropic Direct. The plans commit over $900 billion through 2045. Documents show the company will lease TPUs for $7.2 billion per year. Meanwhile, about 1,300 workers are racing to finish a 600,000 square foot data center rising from the Louisiana woods just inland from the Mississippi River.
