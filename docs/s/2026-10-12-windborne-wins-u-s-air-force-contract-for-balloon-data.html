WindBorne Wins U.S. Air Force Contract for Balloon Data

WindBorne Systems secured a U.S. Air Force contract worth up to $49 million for weather balloon data.

The U.S. Air Force signed a contract with WindBorne Systems worth up to $49 million to buy data collected by the startup's fleet of autonomous weather balloons. WindBorne operates about 700 weather balloons globally. They collect atmospheric sensor readings that feed into customers and the company's own AI weather forecasting model. The Air Force pays only as it needs data by placing orders for specific regions at specific times. The contract has reached $1.7 million in orders so far. The company also builds deep-learning weather models like WeatherMesh 6.
