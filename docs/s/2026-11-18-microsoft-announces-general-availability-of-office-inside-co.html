Microsoft Announces General Availability of Office Inside Copilot

Microsoft announced the general availability of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint inside Copilot at its Ignite event.

Opening the keynote at its Ignite event, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella announced Tuesday the general availability of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint inside Copilot. Nadella described Copilot as the new OS for work. This creates an inversion of traditional software use, where AI agents drive tasks and applications act as plugin components. An organizational knowledge layer backs the system by drawing context from emails, meetings, calendars, chats, and files. Users can create documents, spreadsheets, and presentations directly in Copilot, and the resulting files remain editable in their respective Office applications.
