Moonshot AI Pivots Kimi Models From Coding to Science

Moonshot AI is redirecting its Kimi language models toward biology, chemistry, and pharma research as benchmark gains in coding flatten.

Moonshot AI is pivoting its Kimi language models toward biology, chemistry, and pharma research because of diminishing returns and flat benchmark gains in coding. International director Giacomo Ficari stated at the Web Summit in Lisbon that science demands smarter AI models than law, finance, or code. DP Technology is integrating Kimi K3.1 Pro into its research assistant BioMaster. In one test, the AI analyzed gene data from 3,696 mouse pancreatic cells and flagged 100 candidate genes in 90 minutes. Fully independent breakthroughs are still far off, and the pharma industry remains cautious.
