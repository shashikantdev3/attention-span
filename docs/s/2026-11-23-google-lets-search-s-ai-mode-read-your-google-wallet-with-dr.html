Google lets Search's AI Mode read your Google Wallet, with Drive and Gemini app connections coming next

Google is expanding Personal Intelligence in AI Mode beyond Gmail, Calendar and Photos, starting with read-only access to Wallet.

Google says users can now start opting in to connect Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in Search's AI Mode, with Google Drive and the Gemini app following in the coming weeks. Gmail, Calendar and Photos were already supported. Google says Wallet access is read-only, so AI Mode can help track spending or card rewards but cannot move, manage or invest money. With Drive connected, AI Mode can draw on documents, sheets and PDFs, for example to build a plan ahead of tax season. Google also plans a Personalization icon under tailored answers that shows which context was used, and says AI Overviews and AI Mode will tailor more answers to recent search activity. Connections are opt-in and can be switched off at any time.
