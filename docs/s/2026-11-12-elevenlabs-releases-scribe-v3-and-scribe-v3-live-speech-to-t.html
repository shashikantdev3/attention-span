ElevenLabs releases Scribe v3 and Scribe v3 Live speech-to-text models

ElevenLabs released Scribe v3 for recorded audio and Scribe v3 Live for real-time use, built for crosstalk and background noise.

ElevenLabs has released two speech-to-text models, Scribe v3 for pre-recorded audio and Scribe v3 Live for low-latency real-time use. The company says Scribe v3 cuts word errors by about 25 percent versus its predecessor across 102 languages. Artificial Analysis benchmarks put Scribe v3 among the most accurate models, but Microsoft and xAI models run many times faster and cost roughly half as much. Scribe v3 Live analyzes conversational context to tell when a speaker has finished. Both models are available through the ElevenAPI and the ElevenAgents platform.

Like I am 5

Imagine a friend who listens to you talk and waits until you are really done before answering. This tool tries to do that, so it does not jump in when you just stop to think.

Why it matters

Teams building voice agents get a real-time transcription option that judges when a caller is done speaking instead of using a fixed pause timer, though the benchmark only tested recorded files.
