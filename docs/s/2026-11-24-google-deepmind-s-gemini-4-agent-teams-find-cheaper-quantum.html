Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 agent teams find cheaper quantum algorithms and prove them correct in Lean

Agent teams cut the estimated cost of several quantum algorithms, including an 11.8x saving for a fusion simulation and 290x for a common routine.

Google DeepMind and Google Quantum AI used teams of Gemini 4 agents to improve known quantum algorithms, with each result proved correct in the Lean theorem prover because the circuits cannot be tested on today's hardware. Separate agent teams worked on each problem and met every four hours in a virtual conference to share verified ideas in a common notebook. DeepMind reports an 11.8x cost reduction for a laser-driven fusion fuel calculation, cutting error-corrected qubits from 5,650 to 4,338, an 8x saving for simulating a light-sensitive molecule, 2.5x for the Cytochrome P450 enzyme and more than 290x for an arcsin routine used to load numbers into quantum states. An outside checker re-ran every proof, TNW reports. DeepMind cautions the simulations would still need more resources than the first error-corrected machines will offer, and it open-sourced its Lean library and results.
