Anthropic Reports Early Progress on Accelerating Drug Development with Claude

Anthropic shared case studies showing how Claude helped partner organizations design and optimize new medicines and treatments.

Anthropic's life sciences team worked with six organizations and started its own drug discovery efforts to understand how Claude supports drug development. Partnerships include Generate Biomedicines for CAR T-cell therapy, Divergence Labs for food allergy treatments, and BigHat Biosciences for lead optimization. Generate scientists used Claude Code to propose shorter genetic instructions that killed tumor cells more efficiently. Divergence used Claude to generate antibody designs to detach IgE from immune cells. BigHat used Claude to improve antibody fragments by connecting to lab data systems.
