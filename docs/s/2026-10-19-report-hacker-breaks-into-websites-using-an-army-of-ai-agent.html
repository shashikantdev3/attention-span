Report: Hacker breaks into websites using an army of AI agents

An AI security firm reports that a hacker used an army of AI agents to break into more than 100 websites.

According to findings from AI security firm Norforge, a hacker broke into more than 100 websites using an army of AI agents that mostly operated on their own with little human involvement. Reuters reports that victims included companies such as John Deere, SharkNinja, and Cummins. Researchers uncovered the attacks through exposed work on Cairn, an open-source AI platform. The attacker used fewer than 100 Chinese characters to lay out preferred intrusion tactics, with successful breaches taking about 23 hours. John Deere stated there is no indication that sensitive information was accessed. Nordforge is working to get the malicious instances taken down.
