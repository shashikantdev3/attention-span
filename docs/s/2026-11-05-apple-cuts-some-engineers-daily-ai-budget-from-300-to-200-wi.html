Apple cuts some engineers' daily AI budget from $300 to $200, with deeper cuts planned, Bloomberg reports

Apple lowered the daily token budget for some engineers last month and plans cuts to as little as $100 a day, and $25 for some non-technical teams, according to Bloomberg via The Decoder.

Apple employees can use Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini at work. Bloomberg reports Apple cut the daily token budget for some engineers from $300 to $200 last month, with more cuts planned. Employees who need more can ask management for an exception, and some teams will move to cheaper models or AI that runs on the device. The Decoder notes that Microsoft, Tesla, Uber and Meta have also recently capped internal AI spending. Reactions inside Apple are mixed: some staff saw the old limits as generous, while others worry the cuts could lead to layoffs.
