IMF Report Warns AI Share Crash Could Threaten Global Financial Stability

The IMF warns that high debt and valuations in the AI sector pose risks to global financial stability.

The International Monetary Fund warned that an AI share price crash could trigger global market turmoil. In its Global Financial Stability Report, the IMF highlighted high valuations and rising debt levels within the AI sector. The report identified $300bn in transactions between hyperscalers and smaller AI firms, alongside $650bn in debt. The IMF noted that the seven largest tech stocks now account for over 35% of the S&P 500. Risks include circular financing, forced selling, and potential economic gains that may not meet current expectations. The IMF also cited rising government borrowing and cyber-attacks as additional stability risks.
