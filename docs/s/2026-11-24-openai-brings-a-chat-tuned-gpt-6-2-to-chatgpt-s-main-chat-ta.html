OpenAI brings a chat-tuned GPT-6.2 to ChatGPT's main Chat tab for paid users

A week after GPT-6.2 reached Work and Codex, OpenAI is rolling a version tuned for everyday conversation out to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise.

OpenAI's ChatGPT release notes say a version of GPT-6.2 tuned for the Chat tab is rolling out globally to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users, with Enterprise availability depending on admin settings. OpenAI reports gains over GPT-6 on its own tests, including image understanding (71.7% versus 47.4%), following instructions on hard prompts (78.1% versus 62.5%) and an internal BrowseComp web search evaluation (64.5% versus 54.4%). It says responses met its safety policies in challenging conversations 91% to 94% of the time, up from 77% to 80%, with better scores on teen safety evaluations. The model is also meant to suggest follow-ups, such as offering a price-drop alert. The models behind Work and Codex are unchanged, and GPT-6.2 is not available on the Free or Go plans, MacRumors reports.
