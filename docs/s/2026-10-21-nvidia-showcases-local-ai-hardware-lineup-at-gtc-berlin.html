NVIDIA Showcases Local AI Hardware Lineup at GTC Berlin

NVIDIA is showcasing its local AI hardware lineup at GTC Berlin, including updates for RTX Spark, DGX Spark, and DGX Station.

At GTC Berlin, NVIDIA showcased its local AI hardware lineup, featuring RTX Spark for Windows PCs, DGX Spark for Linux developers, and DGX Station for enterprise teams. Announcements include the availability of the Underdog Desktop Agent for RTX Spark, the launch of DGX Spark 64GB systems on Friday, Oct. 23, and NVIDIA Sync support for up to eight DGX Sparks. The October DGX Spark software update adds a Wi-Fi Assistant and updates DGX Spark Skills. DGX Station will receive a Windows version in December and seven new enterprise manageability integrations.
