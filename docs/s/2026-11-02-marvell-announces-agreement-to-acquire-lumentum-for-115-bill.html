Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum for $115B in stock to own AI optical connectivity

Marvell Technology has entered into an all-stock agreement to acquire Lumentum for about $115 billion to combine optical circuit switching with AI connectivity.

Marvell Technology announced a definitive agreement to acquire Lumentum in an all-stock transaction valued at about $115 billion. The deal combines Marvell's electrical interconnect and custom silicon portfolio with Lumentum's lasers, optical circuit switching, and compound semiconductor manufacturing capabilities. The proposed combination aims to address connectivity bottlenecks in AI data centers as compute performance gains outpace networking gains. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2028. Following completion, Marvell shareholders will own 67% of the new company, and Lumentum shareholders will own 33%.
