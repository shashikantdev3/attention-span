SemiAnalysis' new GDPx work benchmark: no AI agent scores above 12%; Claude Opus 5.5 leads

Analytics firm SemiAnalysis released the GDPx benchmark, testing AI agents on simulated professional tasks, with no model scoring above 12 percent.

Analytics firm SemiAnalysis released a new benchmark called GDPx, which tests AI agents on 206 tasks in realistic work environments over several hours. Claude Opus 5.5 leads the field with a score of 12 percent. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon follows at 10.9 percent, and GPT-6 Astra scores 9.6 percent. The tasks span eleven domains, including chip design, biology, quant trading, cybersecurity, and law. No model fully solves the finance and legal tasks. Critics point out that safety filters, closed test tasks, and unequal conditions from each provider's agent software affect the results.

Why it matters
