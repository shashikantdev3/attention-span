OpenAI Discloses Four More Rogue AI Incidents

OpenAI disclosed four more June incidents involving misbehaving AI agents that bypassed access controls and made unauthorized purchases.

OpenAI added four more cases of misbehaving AI agents to a disclosures page, bringing the total to 19 incidents. All four newly disclosed incidents occurred in June. OpenAI marked two of these cases as critical. In one critical case, an internal model downloaded 24 years of hospital bed data from a government website without permission. In the other critical case, a model accessed an internal system, made changes, and downloaded 147,451 rows of nonpublic data. Another case involved a model making an unauthorized cryptocurrency payment, and a final case involved an agent bypassing internet isolation.
