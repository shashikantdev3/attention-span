Report: Apple May Be Developing Its Own AI Agent

Apple is reportedly developing an in-house AI agent designed to use a computer on behalf of users.

Apple appears to be working on its own AI agent to conduct computer work for users. Bloomberg originally reported the news after spotting a job listing for a senior engineering manager to oversee the project. The agent is intended to browse the web, work across apps, run commands, and write or debug code privately. Progress remains in the early stages, with the service eyed for introduction as soon as the end of next year. Concurrently, Apple is making it harder for third-party agents to gain total control over its computers by deploying warnings in macOS 27.3.
