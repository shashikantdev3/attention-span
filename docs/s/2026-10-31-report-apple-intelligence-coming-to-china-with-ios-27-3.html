Apple Intelligence comes to China with iOS 27.3, without the new Siri

Apple is bringing initial Apple Intelligence features to the Chinese market with the iOS 27.3 update.

Apple is bringing initial Apple Intelligence features to the Chinese market with iOS 27.3. An iOS 27.3 beta will launch on November 4th, and a full release is scheduled for December. The launch does not include the new Siri AI. Instead, users will get notification summaries, real-time translation, rewriting and proofreading tools, visual intelligence, and photo object removal. Apple also worked with Alibaba to add support for its Qwen chatbot in some experiences. Apple Intelligence was approved in China in July. Reuters reported in August that Apple trained its own LLM specifically for China.
