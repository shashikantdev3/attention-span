Meta adds Spaces, topic-based workspaces, to its Muse agents

Meta launched Spaces, a feature that lets users open topic-based conversations with their Muse agent, such as a vacation plan or a wedding planner.

Meta has launched Spaces, a new way to work with its Muse agents. Spaces are breakout conversations built around one topic, which Meta calls "custom experiences." They sit in the sidebar of the Muse interface. Users can describe an idea in chat, and Muse builds a tracker, planner or project board. Meta says Spaces can connect to third-party apps and update in the background. Spaces is the second Muse feature in a week, after Muse Voice, which lets users video chat with the agent. Zuckerberg said agents had 10 million users in the U.S. and Canada.
