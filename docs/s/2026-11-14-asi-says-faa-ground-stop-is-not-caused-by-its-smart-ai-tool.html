ASI says FAA ground stop is not caused by its SMART AI tool

ASI, maker of the FAA's SMART AI tool, says the current nationwide ground stop is not a result of SMART, its products, or its FAA integration.

ASI, the developer of the AI tool SMART, said the FAA ground stop "is not based on or a result of the SMART system, any product of ASI, or our integration with FAA and ATC." Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said a link to SMART can't be ruled out yet. He described a telecommunications outage affecting the FAA's air traffic control centers, with the cause not yet known. The FAA said Tuesday it was expanding SMART to all 22 of its en route centers. Microsoft said its own outage was a "technical issue" from a "network capacity upgrade" and not related to cyber or AI/agents.
