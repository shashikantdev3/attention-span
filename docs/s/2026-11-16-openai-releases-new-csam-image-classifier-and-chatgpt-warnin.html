OpenAI Releases New CSAM Image Classifier and ChatGPT Warnings

OpenAI released a new CSAM image classifier and ChatGPT warnings to help prevent, detect, and respond to online child sexual exploitation.

OpenAI released a new image classifier designed to detect potential child sexual abuse material, including AI-generated content. The tool is available at no cost to vetted organizations through the OpenAI API. Early evaluations suggest the classifier flags 48% fewer images while finding roughly the same number of abusive images as existing tools. Additionally, OpenAI added product warnings in ChatGPT that interrupt attempts to find abusive material, remove the flagged conversation, and pause access to ChatGPT for 24 hours. The company also published guidance to give developers practical steps to prevent and detect abuse.
