US Orders Federal Agencies to Remove DeepSeek AI

The United States ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek AI products and related third-party services from government IT systems.

The Department of Homeland Security ordered federal civilian agencies to stop purchasing services and products developed by Chinese AI startup DeepSeek and remove them from government IT systems. The order covers third-party products and services built using DeepSeek technology, though agencies can request exceptions. Officials raised concerns that user data could be accessible to Chinese authorities under Chinese law. A law passed in December 2025 targeted national security systems. The latest order extends restrictions to civilian agencies. Meanwhile, OpenAI and Anthropic raised concerns about alleged attempts by DeepSeek to collect outputs or distill models.
