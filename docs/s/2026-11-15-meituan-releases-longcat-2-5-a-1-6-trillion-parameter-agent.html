Meituan Releases LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-Trillion-Parameter Agent Model

Meituan has released LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter agent model built entirely on Chinese hardware without Nvidia GPUs.

Chinese food delivery company Meituan has released the weights for LongCat-2.5, a 1.6-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model built for autonomous AI agents. The model was trained entirely on Chinese hardware and never touched an Nvidia GPU. It features a context window that handles up to one million tokens and generates responses up to 128,000 tokens long. A sample deployment runs on 194 Huawei Ascend accelerators. The model handles text, images, and video, and its weights are available under the MIT license.

Why it matters
