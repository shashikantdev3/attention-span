OpenAI and Oracle bring Oracle apps and data into ChatGPT

OpenAI and Oracle expanded their partnership to bring Oracle applications and data into ChatGPT and power new Oracle offerings.

OpenAI and Oracle expanded their partnership to integrate Oracle applications and data into ChatGPT. Oracle is developing Oracle AI Workspace to let users complete approved business workflows in ChatGPT. Oracle also announced new offerings built with OpenAI models, including Oracle AI Security Mesh for vulnerability detection, Oracle AI SMB Bundle for small businesses, and Oracle App Composer for building apps with Codex. Additionally, U.S. customers can purchase access to the OpenAI API, Codex, and ChatGPT Work through Oracle Marketplace.
