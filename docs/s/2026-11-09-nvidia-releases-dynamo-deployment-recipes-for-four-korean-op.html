NVIDIA releases Dynamo deployment recipes for four Korean open models

NVIDIA released Dynamo-optimized deployment recipes for Korean open models from SK Telecom, LG AI Research, Upstage and Motif Technologies, tuned for B200 GPUs.

NVIDIA is releasing deployment recipes built on NVIDIA Dynamo for four Korean open models: A.X K2 from SK Telecom, K-EXAONE 2.0 from LG AI Research, Solar Open 2 from Upstage and Motif-3 from Motif Technologies. Dynamo is an open source, distributed inference-serving framework for multi-node deployment at data center scale. It handles request routing and communication when models are split across multiple GPUs. The recipes are optimized for NVIDIA B200 GPUs. NVIDIA says they deliver up to 2x performance out of the box and help developers bring these models into production faster.
