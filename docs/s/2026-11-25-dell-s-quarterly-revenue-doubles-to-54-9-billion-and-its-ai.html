Dell's quarterly revenue doubles to $54.9 billion and its AI server backlog reaches a record $132.3 billion

Dell raised its full-year forecast for the third time, to $202 billion, after booking $60.2 billion of AI server orders in the quarter.

Dell Technologies reported third quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $54.9 billion, up 103% from a year earlier. Its Infrastructure Solutions Group took in $42 billion, with AI-optimized server revenue up 291% to $22 billion. Vice chairman and COO Jeff Clarke said the company booked $60.2 billion of AI orders and ended the quarter with a record $132.3 billion AI backlog, and cited more than 7,500 customers deploying Dell AI Factories. Dell raised its fiscal 2027 revenue guidance to $202 billion from $192 billion and its AI server forecast to $78 billion from $74 billion. The board also approved a $25 billion increase to the share buyback authorization. Analytics India Magazine reports Clarke told analysts that demand is ahead of supply and the gap will widen in calendar 2027.
