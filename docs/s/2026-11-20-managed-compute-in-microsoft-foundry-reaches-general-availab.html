Managed Compute in Microsoft Foundry Reaches General Availability

Managed Compute is now generally available in Microsoft Foundry, offering simplified deployment for over 150 open models on dedicated GPUs.

Microsoft has announced the general availability of Managed Compute in Microsoft Foundry. The platform makes serving open models as simple as calling a managed model API. Users can choose from over 150 open models in the catalog, and the platform provisions GPUs, maintains containers, and operates the serving runtime. Supported accelerators include NVIDIA and AMD GPUs. New updates include scaled capacity, improved prompt reuse, US and EU Data Zones, and enhanced software supply chain security.
