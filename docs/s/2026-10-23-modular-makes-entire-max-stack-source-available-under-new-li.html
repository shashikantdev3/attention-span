Modular Makes Entire MAX Stack Source Available Under New License

Modular released the complete MAX source code, including its graph compiler, runtime, and hardware backends, under a new developer-friendly license.

Modular announced that the complete MAX source code is now publicly available alongside a simplified MAX License. Developers can now explore, modify, and extend the entire MAX stack, including the graph compiler, runtime, and hardware backends for CPUs and GPUs. The new license allows users to build and distribute commercial products without licensing fees or royalties. Certain limitations apply, such as restrictions on AI-assisted reimplementation and the distribution of modified source code without forking for upstream contributions.
