Harwood, North Dakota voters reject measure opposing Applied Digital's $3B data center

Voters in Harwood, North Dakota rejected a ballot measure against Applied Digital's $3B data center, going against a nationwide backlash, the Washington Post reports.

The Washington Post reports that voters in Harwood, North Dakota rejected a ballot measure that opposed Applied Digital's $3B data center. The data center is set to open in 2027. The result bucks a nationwide backlash. According to the report, on Election Day much of the country turned against the massive projects that power AI. The article, by Miriam Waldvogel, looks at why Harwood did not. The source text is only a Techmeme summary of the piece. It does not give vote totals, the ballot measure's exact wording, or the reasons voters gave.
