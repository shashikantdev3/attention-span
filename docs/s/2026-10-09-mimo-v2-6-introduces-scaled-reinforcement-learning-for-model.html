MiMo-V2.6 Introduces Scaled Reinforcement Learning for Model Self-Improvement

A new paper introduces MiMo-V2.6, an omni-modal family that scales reinforcement learning compute along three dimensions to improve model intelligence.

A new research report details MiMo-V2.6, an omni-modal family of models designed to push model intelligence by scaling reinforcement learning compute. The authors conduct mid-training on a multimodal corpus and use a pretrained hybrid-SWA architecture. They scale reinforcement learning compute by increasing batch sizes and throughput, using diverse and complex environments, and applying groupwise agentic grading. To maintain stability, they freeze the MoE router and add multi-layer defenses against reward hacking. The team also open-sources the training dynamics, reinforcement learning environments, and framework.
