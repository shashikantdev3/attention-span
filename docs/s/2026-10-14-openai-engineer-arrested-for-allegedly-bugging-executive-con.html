OpenAI Engineer Arrested for Allegedly Bugging Executive Conference Room

Federal prosecutors charged an OpenAI engineer with hiding a recording device in an executive conference room.

Federal prosecutors have charged a 35-year-old OpenAI engineer with using a device to intercept oral communications. According to prosecutors, OpenAI staff found the recording device on September 30, plugged into a television USB port and concealed in toilet paper. Prosecutors say the engineer had used a chatbot account to ask about building and installing recording devices. He was arrested on Friday, made his first court appearance on Tuesday and remains in custody. If convicted, he faces up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine. OpenAI said it used its own models to work out where the device came from.
