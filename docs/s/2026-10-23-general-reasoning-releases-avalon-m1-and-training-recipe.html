General Reasoning Releases Avalon-M1 and Training Recipe

General Reasoning released Avalon-M1 and a training recipe called reinforcement learning from collective rewards to jointly train agent organisations.

General Reasoning released Avalon-M1, a new model series that runs agent organisations. Agents co-ordinate through a message board and workspace, and the whole team is rewarded as one. The company shares a recipe called reinforcement learning from collective rewards, which jointly trains agents using the same collective advantage without estimating individual contributions. During training, organisations develop additional layers of hierarchy, adopt different divisions of labour, and devote more resources to peer review. Organisations reach comparable scores in roughly a quarter of the estimated time compared to a single agent.
