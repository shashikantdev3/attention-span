Google DeepMind and WRI release ForTy, a global AI map of forest types

Google DeepMind and the World Resources Institute created ForTy, a global map that classifies forests and land cover using satellite data and AI.

Google DeepMind and the World Resources Institute developed ForTy, a global map that classifies land cover into six categories at 10-meter resolution for the year 2020. The system uses the AlphaEarth Foundations model and multiple image-recognition stages to distinguish forest types based on growth and harvest cycles. The map achieved 90.2 percent overall accuracy in tests. It provides a reference point for the EU Regulation on Deforestation-free Products, though it identifies only about half of tree crops and planted forests, and 60 percent of plantations.
