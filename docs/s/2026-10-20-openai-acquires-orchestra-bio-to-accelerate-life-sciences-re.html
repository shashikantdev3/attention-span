OpenAI Acquires Orchestra Bio to Accelerate Life Sciences Research

OpenAI is acquiring Orchestra Bio to combine its drug development platform with Rosalind, aiming to help biopharma teams coordinate complex workflows.

OpenAI has acquired Orchestra Bio, a platform that helps biopharma teams coordinate drug development. OpenAI is bringing Orchestra's team and technology together with Rosalind, its AI models and tools for life sciences research. Developing a new medicine takes roughly 10 to 15 years on average from target discovery to regulatory approval. Orchestra brings a platform built to coordinate drug development, where people and AI agents work together to advance drug programs. The goal is to help scientists make discoveries and manage the work needed to turn them into treatments that reach patients sooner.
