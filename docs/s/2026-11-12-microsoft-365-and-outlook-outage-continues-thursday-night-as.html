Microsoft 365 outage takes down Outlook and Teams for hours; Microsoft says a core server process was overwhelmed

Microsoft says restarting servers is showing positive results after a Thursday outage hit Outlook, Teams and other Microsoft 365 services, with thousands of reports still open.

Microsoft 365 services, including Outlook and Teams, had problems on Thursday. Outlook reports peaked at more than 130,000 on Downdetector at 1:15 p.m. By 10 p.m., about 25,000 Outlook reports and more than 8,700 Microsoft 365 reports remained. Microsoft said a core process on affected servers was becoming overwhelmed, and that servers were failing automated health checks. It is restarting servers in stages and checking them before applying the fix more widely. The cause of the servers going offline is still unclear. Microsoft has not said when service will fully return.

Why it matters

Teams that rely on Microsoft 365 tools such as Exchange Online, Teams, Power BI and Copilot 365 should plan for outages, since one server health problem can disrupt many linked services at once.
