Microsoft and Snowflake commit to Apache Ossie semantic standard

Microsoft is joining Snowflake and other partners to support Apache Ossie, a vendor-neutral standard for exchanging semantic metadata.

Microsoft announced its commitment to Apache Ossie, an incubating community-led effort to establish a vendor-neutral standard for exchanging semantic metadata across analytics, AI, and BI platforms. This follows a close collaboration with Snowflake, which helped found Ossie. The companies aim to support platform-agnostic agentic experiences where shared semantic context is defined once and reused across platforms. Power BI semantic models currently have more than 38 million monthly active users and are adopted by 94 percent of Fortune 500 companies. Microsoft was also recognized as a Leader and Outperformer in the GigaOm radar for semantic layers and metric stores in 2025.
