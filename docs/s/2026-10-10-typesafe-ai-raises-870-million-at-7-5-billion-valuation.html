TypeSafe AI Raises $870 Million at $7.5 Billion Valuation

TypeSafe AI raised $870 million at a $7.5 billion valuation for its non-text AI model, Jev.

TypeSafe AI has raised $870 million at a $7.5 billion valuation for its new AI model called Jev. Andreessen Horowitz led the funding round, with participation from Sequoia and existing investor DCVC. Jev launched on September 15 and went viral instantly. The startup claims that a third of Fortune 500 companies are already using the model. Jev uses a transformer architecture but does not output text. Instead, it produces probabilities or calibrated decisions. The company was founded in 2024 by Diogo Almeida, Sasha Sheng, and Erik Gafni.
