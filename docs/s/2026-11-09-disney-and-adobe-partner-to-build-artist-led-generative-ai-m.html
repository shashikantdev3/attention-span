Disney and Adobe partner to build artist-led generative AI models

Disney and Adobe announced a multiyear partnership at Adobe MAX to build proprietary, artist-led generative AI models for Disney's production pipeline.

The Walt Disney Company has teamed up with Adobe to develop proprietary "artist-led" generative AI models for its production pipeline. The multiyear deal was announced at Adobe's MAX conference. Adobe will give Disney early access to its latest technologies for theme parks, the cruise line and ESPN content. Through Adobe Firefly Foundry, the models will be tailored to Disney's properties and trained on character attributes, environments and visual styles. Adobe says Disney's Imagineering R&D division already uses the Firefly AI platform for theme park development. Adobe will also help expand creative workflows at Walt Disney Studios.
