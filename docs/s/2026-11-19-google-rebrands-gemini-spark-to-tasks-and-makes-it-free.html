Gemini Tasks goes free in six countries and can now call businesses, book appointments and pay for you

Google is rolling out its new Gemini Tasks assistant for free in six countries, using the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model.

Google has rebranded its Gemini Spark AI agent to Tasks and made it available free of charge for hundreds of millions of people. The update rolls out across the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India using the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. Tasks connects to user emails, calendars, and payment methods to handle daily chores. In the U.S., the assistant can call businesses on behalf of users, schedule appointments, analyze utility bills, and purchase items using Google Pay with the Agent Payments Protocol.
