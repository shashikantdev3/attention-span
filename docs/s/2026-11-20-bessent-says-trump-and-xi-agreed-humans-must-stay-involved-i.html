Bessent says Trump and Xi agreed humans must stay involved in major military AI decisions

The US Treasury Secretary says the two leaders agreed in Shenzhen that AI should not act on its own in major military actions.

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CNBC that President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping agreed that humans should always be involved in military decisions made with AI, Reuters reports. He was speaking after returning from the leaders' meeting in Shenzhen, China, and called the understanding one of the major achievements of Trump's Asia trip. Bessent said AI, which the administration calls super intelligence, should not be allowed to act autonomously in major military actions. He said the agreement would not be affected by the two countries' race for AI leadership, and that US labs must keep their lead to defend against what he called dangerous Chinese models used in cyberattacks. Bessent also said Nvidia's H200 remains the most advanced chip China can buy with a license and that Chinese orders for it have been scarce. Reuters reports he said the spying allegations involving Jensen Huang did not come up in the meeting.
