Anthropic reportedly presses on with pre-Thanksgiving IPO at lower valuation despite tech selloff

Anthropic is reportedly pushing ahead with an IPO before Thanksgiving, seeking $80–100 billion at a $1.25–1.75 trillion valuation, below hopes of $2 trillion.

The Financial Times, citing investors, reports that Anthropic wants to go public before Thanksgiving despite a tech selloff. It aims to raise $80–100 billion at a $1.25–1.75 trillion valuation. That is above the current $965 billion but under the $2 trillion the most optimistic investors had hoped for. Annualized reported revenue is about $81 billion, up from roughly $65 billion at the end of July. The selloff was driven by rising borrowing costs and heavy AI spending plans. The Nasdaq 100 dropped 6.4 percent on Thursday and recovered somewhat on Friday. OpenAI also cut its funding-round target from $1.4 trillion to $1.25 trillion.
