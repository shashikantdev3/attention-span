Google DeepMind Announces AlphaGenome 2 for Cell-Level DNA Analysis

Google DeepMind has unveiled AlphaGenome 2, a conditional DNA sequence model designed to predict how genetic variants affect specific human cell types.

Google DeepMind announced AlphaGenome 2, a next-generation DNA sequence model that bridges single-cell biology and human genetics. Unlike its predecessor that used parallel bulk tissue predictions, AlphaGenome 2 is a conditional model taking a target biological condition as input alongside a million-letter DNA sequence. Trained on over 107 million individual cell profiles across 68 tissues, the model identifies nearly twice as many variants that change gene expression in cells. It is available for non-commercial research via the AlphaGenome API and in private preview for commercial research.
