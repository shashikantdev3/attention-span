Google Play Lets Users Run Private Vibe-Coded Android Apps

Google Play now allows users in the U.S. to build and run private AI-generated apps on Android without public store publication.

Google announced a new feature enabling users to launch personal, AI-generated applications on their Android devices via Google Play using standard Google accounts. These apps remain completely private, hidden from search or listing pages, and are only accessible to the creator. Initially available to users in the United States aged 18 and older, the apps must comply with existing Google Play policies. In future months, users will be able to share these creations with trusted friends or family, or eventually publish them on the Play Store after completing safety reviews.
