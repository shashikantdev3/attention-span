Uber Deploys ChatGPT Work to All 30,000 Employees

Uber is rolling out ChatGPT Work to more than 30,000 employees to handle research, data analysis, presentations, and complex workflows.

Uber announced the company-wide rollout of ChatGPT Work for more than 30,000 employees across its global workforce. This follows an initial pilot of 1,000 employees. The agentic platform connects with daily tools like Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, and Slack. It enables workers to research information, prepare meeting briefs, create presentations, analyze data, and complete workflows. Uber will use OpenAI models including GPT-6.1 Sol as the default capability. Software engineering teams will also continue using Codex to accelerate software development.
