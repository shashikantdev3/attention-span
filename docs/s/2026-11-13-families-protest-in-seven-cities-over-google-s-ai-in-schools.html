Families protest in seven cities over Google's AI in schools, no AI-free Search option

Families protested in seven US cities against Google's AI in schools after Google confirmed students cannot use Google Search without AI.

Families joined protests in seven cities nationwide on Wednesday. They object to what they see as Google's bid to entrench its AI models in schools. The backlash follows a Google statement last week confirming there will be no way for students to use Google Search without AI. Google said it was giving schools "more choice, not less" by turning off Search, Gemini, and Gemini Notebook by default. It would not offer Search without AI. Parents worried that students would have to remember to add "-ai" to every query. In Chicago, dozens of protesters marched to Google's downtown office despite cold, rainy weather.

Imagine your school library only had books with a helper robot stuck inside every page, and you could not pick plain books. Some families are saying they want a choice.

Why it matters

Education customers are pushing back on AI features that cannot be switched off, so products sold to schools may need a real AI-free mode, not just defaults that are turned off.
