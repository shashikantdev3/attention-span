Chip industry executives warn new US export controls reach well beyond AI chips, Nikkei reports

Executives told Nikkei Asia that the tighter US rules introduced this week also touch chips for everyday mobile and consumer products, and that TSMC has suspended some shipments.

Nikkei Asia reports that several industry executives say the tighter US export controls introduced on Thursday reach further than first expected. According to the report, the rules go beyond advanced AI chips to chips used in everyday mobile and consumer products, and they also affect chip developers outside China and companies that do not make AI chips. Nikkei reports that TSMC has suspended some shipments. The executives were not named.
