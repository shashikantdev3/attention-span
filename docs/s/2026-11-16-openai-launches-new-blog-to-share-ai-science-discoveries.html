OpenAI Launches New Blog to Share AI Science Discoveries

OpenAI started a science blog to share research, methods, and firsthand accounts from scientists using AI to accelerate discovery.

OpenAI launched a science blog on November 15, 2026. The blog shares research results, methods, and practical guidance from OpenAI and the broader scientific community. It highlights recent work in mathematics, physics, and biology. In September, an internal OpenAI system produced a proof addressing the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness problem. In October, OpenAI released mathematical results including the quasi-Riemann hypothesis. In biology, researchers worked with PerturbAI and Scripps Research using thousands of agents powered by GPT-Rosalind to analyze mouse brain cell data.
