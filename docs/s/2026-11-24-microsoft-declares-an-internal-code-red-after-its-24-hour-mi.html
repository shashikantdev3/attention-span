Microsoft declares an internal 'Code Red' after its 24-hour Microsoft 365 outage, Reuters reports

Internal memos seen by Reuters show Microsoft overhauling how it prevents and handles major outages after the 12 to 13 November failure that hit Exchange, Teams and Copilot.

Reuters reports that Microsoft has launched a Code Red, its most urgent internal alert, to change how it prevents and responds to major service failures, citing internal memos. The 12 to 13 November outage lasted about 24 hours and disrupted Exchange Online, Outlook, Teams and Copilot, affecting governments, trading firms and airline communications. A memo from M365 Core president Jason Henderson called it the most customer-impacting incident in Exchange Online's history, according to Reuters. A memo from corporate vice president Amanda Silver described more than 11,000 messages across 11 Teams chats during the response and set out five workstreams including load shedding, splitting traffic by region and faster recovery. Microsoft wants to resolve future global incidents within an hour and plans to use AI agents to help engineers read system health data. Microsoft has blamed a Windows scalability defect introduced in July and said AI agents and cyber activity played no part.
