Palmer Luckey Warns AI Cyberattacks Are Outpacing Defenses

Palmer Luckey warned at the New York Times DealBook Summit that AI-powered cyberattacks are moving faster than outdated technology defenses.

Speaking at The New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday, Oculus and Anduril Industries co-founder Palmer Luckey warned that AI-powered cyberattacks are outpacing outdated technology systems. Advanced AI tools allow for the creation of various exploits. U.S. adversaries, with North Korea particularly involved, are exploiting the mismatch between new attack capabilities and defense capabilities for strategic and financial reasons. Luckey stated that defenders must move more quickly to use AI for defense, warning that people need to pick up the pace and start running.
