Report: Anthropic Circulates Leaked Federal AI Bill

AI safety advocates say a leaked draft federal AI bill that Anthropic circulated to lawmakers is weaker than the FRONTIER Act.

A leaked discussion draft of a federal AI bill circulated by Anthropic to lawmakers has drawn criticism from AI safety advocates. Critics say the proposed bill is weaker than the FRONTIER Act. It lacks standards for third-party evaluators, who can self-certify. It requires the Commerce Department to petition a federal court and make a detailed case to block a dangerous model. The bill also includes preemption language that stops state legislatures or courts from suspending a model on catastrophic-risk grounds. Anthropic head of federal affairs Anthony Cimino stated that the preemption language will be revised.
