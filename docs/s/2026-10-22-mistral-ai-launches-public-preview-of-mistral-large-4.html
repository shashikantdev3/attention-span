Mistral AI Launches Public Preview of Mistral Large 4

Mistral AI released a public preview of Mistral Large 4, a 1 trillion-parameter natively multimodal model with open weights arriving by month-end.

Mistral AI has launched a public preview of Mistral Large 4, also known as le Chonk. The model features 1 trillion total parameters with 52 billion active parameters and was trained from scratch on 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs in Europe. It demonstrates strong performance in coding, agentic workflows, and cybersecurity. The weights will be released by the end of the month. Until then, the model is undergoing red-teaming in real-world settings with cybersecurity leaders, vetted partners, and state authorities. It was trained on multilingual data spanning more than 160 languages.
