Mistral AI Releases Timestral Time-Series Foundation Models

Mistral AI introduced Timestral, a family of four time-series foundation models ranging from 5M to 209M parameters that deliver probabilistic multivariate forecasts.

Mistral AI released Timestral, its first family of time-series foundation models. The models come in four sizes with 5M, 28M, 123M, and 209M parameters. The largest 209M parameter model ranks first on the GIFT-Eval, TIME, and fev-bench public benchmarks among pre-trained and zero-shot entries. Timestral delivers probabilistic and multivariate forecasts, supports past-only and known-future covariates, and operates efficiently on standard server CPUs without needing a GPU at inference. The models combine synthetic pre-training, covariate-aware attention, and long-context learning, and support fine-tuning for domain adaptation through Mistral Forge.
