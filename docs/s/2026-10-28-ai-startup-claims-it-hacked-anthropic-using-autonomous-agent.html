Cathedral's AI agents got into some of Anthropic's HR, sales and IT systems; Anthropic says code and weights were safe

An AI startup used autonomous agents to breach Anthropic systems through third-party software weaknesses in less than 12 hours.

An AI startup named Cathedral used autonomous agents to breach Anthropic systems through weaknesses in third-party business software. After human researchers failed for a few days, the AI agents took less than 12 hours to find a way in. The agents accessed HR, sales, and IT data, including meeting transcripts, salaries, job offers, and Slack messages. Anthropic confirmed that a limited set of systems was accessed, but stated that source code and AI model weights remained safe. Anthropic shut off the access the same day and accused Cathedral of violating bug bounty rules.
