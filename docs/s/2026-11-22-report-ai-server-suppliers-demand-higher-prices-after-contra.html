Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro ask for up to 45% more on AI server deals already signed, FT reports

The Financial Times reports that AI server makers are raising prices months after contracts were signed, with trading firms among those hit.

The Financial Times reported on 22 November that Dell, HPE, Lenovo and Supermicro are asking buyers to pay more for AI servers months after deals were signed, with some increases as high as 45%. Trading firms including Jane Street, Optiver and Hudson River Trading are among those affected, according to reports on the FT story, and most have accepted the higher bills to get the equipment. XTX Markets has sued Dell in an Irish court over a proposed price rise. Suppliers are passing on soaring memory and component costs.
