Meta puts its Muse AI agent inside Instagram DMs

Instagram users can now chat with Muse in a DM thread and have it act on posts, starting in five countries.

Meta is letting Instagram users chat with its Muse AI agent directly inside direct messages instead of switching to the separate Muse app, TechCrunch reports. Users can ask Muse for context on a post, place or product, catch up on missed DM conversations, or share a post and ask it to act, such as adding a cafe to a trip itinerary or finding a shoe deal. Saved posts and reels can be sorted into Collections, and recipe videos turned into grocery lists. The rollout starts Tuesday on iOS and Android in the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore, with the save-to-Muse prompt launching in the US and Canada. Meta says nothing is shared, published or sent without the user's approval and that users can make Muse forget information or undo actions.
