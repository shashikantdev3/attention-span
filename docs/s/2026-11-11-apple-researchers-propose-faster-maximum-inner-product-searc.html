Apple Researchers Propose Faster Maximum Inner Product Search for RAG

Apple researchers presented a paper at ICML 2026 detailing how small neural networks can amortize maximum inner product search to speed up RAG retrieval.

Maximum inner product search is central to retrieval-augmented generation. Apple researchers presented a paper at ICML 2026 proposing to train small neural networks offline for a fixed database of keys. This approach learns how to route queries toward their best match faster. Traditional methods group keys into clusters and compare queries against centroids, which can cause errors. By treating the mapping function as a support function from convex analysis, the researchers trained SupportNet to learn the support function and KeyNet to directly output the best-matching key vector.

Instead of searching through millions of items one by one every time you ask a question, you train a small helper beforehand that remembers where things usually are so it can point you directly to the right answer.

Why it matters

Precomputing query distributions with trained neural networks reduces the computational cost of vector retrieval in production systems.
