Signal restricts screenshots to block AI agents from reading chats

Signal is restricting screenshots on Android and iOS devices to stop AI agents from reading private chats.

Encrypted messaging service Signal is restricting screenshots on Android and iOS devices to prevent AI assistants like Siri and Gemini from reading user messages and on-screen data. Signal president Meredith Whittaker stated that operating system capabilities analyzing on-screen information undermine privacy guarantees. Signal noted that developers lack robust tools to prevent AI agents from accessing data without compromising user experience. Users can restore screenshot capabilities in settings, but Signal warns this exposes content to AI agents. Signal is calling on Apple, Google, and Microsoft to provide simple technical tools and documentation for app developers.
