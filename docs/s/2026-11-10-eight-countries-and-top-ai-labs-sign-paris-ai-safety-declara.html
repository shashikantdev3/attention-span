Eight countries and top AI labs sign Paris AI safety declaration

Eight countries and firms including OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft and Mistral AI signed the Paris Call for AI Safety and Security, which has no sanctions or deadlines.

Representatives of eight countries, tech companies and civil-society groups signed the "Paris Call for AI Safety and Security," adopted on Nov. 9, at Emmanuel Macron's initiative. Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, Brad Smith, Arthur Mensch, Yoshua Bengio and U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent joined. Germany did not sign. The companies pledge shutdown mechanisms for models under their control, independent audits, and disclosure of known risks. Non-sensitive test results are to be published in a standard format. The text is a political commitment. It sets neither deadlines nor sanctions, and shared standards still have to be worked out.
