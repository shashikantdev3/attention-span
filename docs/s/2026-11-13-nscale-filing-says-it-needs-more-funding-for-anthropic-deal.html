Nscale filing says it needs more funding for Anthropic deal, clouding $35B IPO

Nscale told investors it needs more financing for its 2027 commitments after ordering Nvidia chips for a $45B Anthropic deal, per the Financial Times.

The Financial Times reports that a filing from Nscale says the company needs more funding for its 2027 commitments. Nscale, an Nvidia-backed 'neocloud', ordered Nvidia chips for a $45B Anthropic deal. The report says this casts doubt on its planned $35B IPO. CNBC reports that Nscale released an updated IPO prospectus on Thursday for a U.S. offering. It shows Q3 revenue up 1,344% year over year to $307.5M, up from $140.6M in Q2. Net loss widened to $1.69B, up from $1.02B in Q2.
