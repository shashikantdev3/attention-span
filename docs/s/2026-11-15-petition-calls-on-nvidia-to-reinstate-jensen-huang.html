Petition calls on Nvidia to reinstate Jensen Huang

A petition asks Nvidia to bring back Jensen Huang after his resignation.

A petition calling on Nvidia to reinstate former CEO Jensen Huang attracted more than 115,000 signatures as of Saturday afternoon. The petition website stated that signers include current employees, former employees, and shareholders. Notable supporters include Elon Musk, Amjad Masad, Tim Sweeney, and Garry Tan. The petition states that signers are concerned that Huang was removed without meaningful due process. Huang resigned on Thursday, four days after confessing to having an affair with a chip executive suspected by U.S. intelligence to be a Chinese spy. Nvidia appointed Colette Kress as interim CEO.
