Ricursive's Ripple-65B Preview claims the best US open-weight score on agentic coding

Ricursive Intelligence released Ripple-65B Preview, a new open-weight model that achieves a U.S. state-of-the-art score on agentic coding.

Ricursive Intelligence introduced Ripple-65B Preview, a general-purpose variant of the model powering its AI chip design platform. Pretrained on 12 trillion tokens with support for up to 1M context length, the model achieved a U.S. open-weight state-of-the-art score of 80.52 on TerminalBench 2.1. It surpassed larger open-weight models including Reflection Beam 501B and Meta Muse Spark Open 816B. The dense Transformer model was developed for $32 million, which is one-tenth the cost of the previous open-weight state-of-the-art. It runs efficiently on local devices like the NVIDIA DGX Spark and is available on Hugging Face, Together AI, GMI Cloud, OpenRouter, and Fireworks.
