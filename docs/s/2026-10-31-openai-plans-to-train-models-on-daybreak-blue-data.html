OpenAI will train on Daybreak Blue security session data from 29 Nov, then promises an opt-out after backlash

OpenAI plans to use Daybreak Blue session data for training models, prompting a backlash and a promised opt-out.

Starting November 29, OpenAI will use Daybreak Blue session data to train its models. An email to Trusted Access program members confirmed the change. Previously, this data was excluded from training, even when users turned off the setting to improve the model for everyone. Following swift backlash, OpenAI sent a second email that promised a separate opt-out. Interactions flagged during safety reviews can still be used for safety purposes. This change has researchers worried about a repeat of the Buckmaster-Alpöge case, where mathematicians suspected OpenAI used their uploaded research drafts for training.
