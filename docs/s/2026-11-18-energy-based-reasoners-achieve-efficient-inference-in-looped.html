Energy-Based Reasoners achieve efficient inference in looped models

Researchers introduced Energy-Based Reasoners, which use an energy function and gradient descent for looped updates in reasoning tasks.

Energy-Based Reasoners learn an energy function over latent reasoning states and formulate looped updates as gradient descent on this energy. The energy scores latent states, while its gradient guides refinement and provides a signal for stopping. Exploration-based training helps discover additional modes and shape the energy landscape over a broader set of states. Across four algorithmic reasoning benchmarks, Energy-Based Reasoners match or outperform prior recurrent reasoning models. They achieve 99.9% accuracy on Sudoku-Extreme while using 345 times less inference compute than the next-best model and maintaining comparable training compute.
