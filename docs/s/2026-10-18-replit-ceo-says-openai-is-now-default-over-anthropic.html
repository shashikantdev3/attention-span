Replit CEO says OpenAI is now default over Anthropic

Replit CEO Amjad Masad stated that OpenAI is now the preferred partner for powering Replit Agent, replacing Anthropic as the default model.

Replit CEO Amjad Masad announced that OpenAI has replaced Anthropic as the default model provider for the platform. Masad stated that the decision was based on cost, partnership, and performance, citing internal and public benchmarks. Anthropic models remain accessible to customers on the platform, but OpenAI is now the default and preferred partner for powering Replit Agent. Prior to the agreement with OpenAI, Replit relied on Anthropic's Claude models. Insiders familiar with the partnership disputed that the relationship with Anthropic had ended, and evidence showed recent promotions of Anthropic products.
