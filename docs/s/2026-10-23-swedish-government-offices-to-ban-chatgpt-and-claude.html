Swedish government offices to ban ChatGPT and Claude

Swedish government offices will block access to ChatGPT and Claude from staff work computers to enforce approved services.

The Swedish government offices, Regeringskansliet, are blocking access to the AI services ChatGPT and Claude from staff work computers. The move aims to ensure employees use only government-approved services, which include Microsoft's AI tool Copilot. Digitalization director Mikael Wettercrantz stated that using AI services without an agreement can affect information security. Similar bans have been introduced at the Swedish Tax Agency and the National Board of Institutional Care, while the Enforcement Authority is reviewing the question. Internal reviews at Trafikverket also uncovered risky use of open AI services regarding sensitive information and generated answers.
