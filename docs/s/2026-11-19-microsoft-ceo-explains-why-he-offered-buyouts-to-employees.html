Microsoft CEO Explains Why He Offered Buyouts to Employees

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella explained that offering buyouts to 60,000 employees was meant to reorganize the company around AI work.

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella offered buyouts to 60,000 employees in October instead of using massive layoffs. He explained that the goal is to reorganize the 51-year-old company into an AI-first organization where everyone uses AI to do their work. Nadella stated that jobs will focus on managing AI agents rather than doing the work directly. He cited an example of a team building the Cowork AI agent in Microsoft 365 apps, which consists of just 11 developers building a software factory that creates the product. Nadella noted that the buyout gives employees agency to choose if they want to adapt.
