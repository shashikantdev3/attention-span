NVIDIA announces investment in Point2 and plans more in three Korean startups

NVIDIA announced an investment in Korean startup Point2 and planned investments in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage at a forum with Korea's science ministry.

Korea's Ministry of Science and ICT and NVIDIA hosted the MSIT-NVIDIA AI Startup Partnership Forum at COEX. Vice minister Ryu Je-myung joined NVIDIA leaders to meet over 20 Korean startups. NVIDIA announced an investment in Point2, which builds radio frequency-based interconnects for AI factories. It plans further investments in Motif Technologies, RLWRLD and Upstage. Motif released Motif-3, a large language model with open weights. RLWRLD builds robotics foundation models, and Upstage makes Solar language models and document AI tools. NVIDIA also noted more than 1,000 Korea-based members of NVIDIA Inception.
