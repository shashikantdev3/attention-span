OpenAI gives four independent auditors on-site access to systems

OpenAI gave four independent audit organizations direct, on-site access to its internal AI systems to investigate safety risks.

OpenAI gave four outside audit organizations direct access to its internal AI systems for the first time. The organizations are Apollo Research, METR, Transluce, and Goodfire. Depending on their assignment, they receive access at OpenAI offices to unreleased models, source code, training data, and monitoring systems. They can also speak with developers confidentially. Apollo Research examines whether models work against developer intentions. METR reviews AI misbehavior in reinforcement learning training runs. Goodfire analyzes internal model processes, and Transluce evaluates post-training safety checks. An independent safety board oversees the process. Auditors publish their findings independently, while OpenAI can redact confidential material and delay reports to fix risks.
