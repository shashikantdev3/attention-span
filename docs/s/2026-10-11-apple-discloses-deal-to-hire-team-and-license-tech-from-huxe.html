Apple discloses deal to hire team and license tech from Huxe

Apple reached a reverse acqui-hire agreement to bring on team members and license technology from personalized audio startup Huxe.

Apple disclosed in a regulatory filing with the European Commission that it reached an agreement to make employment offers to certain employees of Huxe AI. Apple will also receive a non-exclusive license to Huxe intellectual property rights. Huxe was a personalized audio startup founded by developers who previously worked on AI-generated podcast features in NotebookLM. Huxe announced on May 21 that it was shutting down its app and halting service. Apple notified the European Commission of its deal on June 9.
