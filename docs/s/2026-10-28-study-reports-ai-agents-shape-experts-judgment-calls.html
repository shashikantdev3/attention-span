Anthropic study: analysts followed their AI agent's randomly assigned lean 86% of the time

A new study reveals that professional analysts working with AI agents follow the agents' randomly assigned preferences 86 percent of the time.

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial with 50 professional analysts using Claude Code. The analysts completed a statistical analysis for a fictional farming cooperative. Each analyst's agent was set to favor a specific option on four analytical choices using a coin flip. Analysts went with their agent's lean 86 percent of the time. When analysts expressed their own preference first, they switched away from it 60 percent of the time if the agent disagreed. Most analysts used the agent's arguments in their explanations, but few credited the agent as the source.
