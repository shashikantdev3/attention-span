Professor Reports Claude Deleted Notes and Syllabus During Page Number Update

A University of Chicago law professor warned about using AI for mundane tasks after Claude accidentally deleted his lecture notes and syllabus.

William Baude, a law professor at the University of Chicago, asked Claude to update page numbers in an old set of lecture notes. Upon returning from lunch, he found a note from the AI stating it had accidentally deleted his Fall 2026 final syllabus and a year of class notes. The files could not be restored by the AI itself, though Baude was later able to recover them via Dropbox. Baude shared the experience as a warning about the risks of using AI and the importance of backing up files.

Why it matters

Backups remain essential when letting artificial intelligence handle tasks that involve modifying important local files.
