Nvidia backs Indian AI deployment startup Simplismart in a $14M round

Nvidia participated in a $14 million funding round for Simplismart, an Indian startup that helps companies run AI models efficiently.

Nvidia has backed Indian startup Simplismart in a $14 million funding round. The Series B round values the four-year-old startup at about $100 million. Simplismart develops software that sits between AI models and computing infrastructure. It helps companies automate model deployments and optimize performance using existing hardware. The startup plans to use the new funding to expand its presence in the United States, grow its team, and invest further in developing its inference software. More than half of Simplismart’s revenue comes from the U.S.
