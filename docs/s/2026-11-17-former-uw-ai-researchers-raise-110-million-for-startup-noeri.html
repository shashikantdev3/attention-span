Former UW AI Researchers Raise $110 Million for Startup Noeri

Former University of Washington AI researchers Yejin Choi and Carlos Guestrin have raised $110 million for their new startup, Noeri.

AI researchers Yejin Choi and Carlos Guestrin have raised $110 million for Noeri, a startup based in Palo Alto with an office in Zurich. Menlo Ventures led the funding round, with participation from Madrona, Kindred Ventures, Wing Venture Capital, NEA, Cisco Investments, Nvidia, and Snowflake. Noeri builds AI systems that study a company's data and workflows, then propose improvements to instructions, tools, and underlying models. Each proposed change requires human approval. The startup aims to help companies avoid choosing between expensive top-tier models and cheaper, weaker alternatives.
