Tencent releases Hy4 with vision and computer use; weights to follow

Tencent released its Hy4 language model featuring image understanding and software operation capabilities, scoring second among Chinese models on independent benchmarks.

Tencent has released Hy4, an AI model that features image understanding and the ability to operate software on its own. The model was trained on more data than its predecessor and was specifically optimized for coding, finance, and office work. It is available through Tencent Cloud, OpenRouter, and Tencent apps, with model weights to follow at a later date. On Artificial Analysis, Hy4 scores 35 points, ranking second among Chinese lab models. However, it still trails top models like Claude Opus 5.5 on hard math and coding tasks.
