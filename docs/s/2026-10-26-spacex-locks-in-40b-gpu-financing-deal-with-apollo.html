SpaceX Locks In $40B GPU Financing Deal With Apollo

SpaceX has reached a binding commitment for roughly $40 billion in financing from Apollo Global Management for AI chips and data center equipment.

SpaceX disclosed in a regulatory filing that it reached a binding commitment for about $40 billion in financing for AI chips and related data center equipment. An entity managed by Apollo Global Management will buy the hardware and lease it to SpaceX for about five to five-and-a-quarter years per tranche. The leases begin when equipment is delivered, with the deal expected to close by year-end and leases starting in the first half of 2027. SpaceX CFO Bret Johnsen stated the lease diversifies funding sources. Elon Musk argued on X that the deal highlights advantages over companies lacking revenue streams outside AI data centers.
