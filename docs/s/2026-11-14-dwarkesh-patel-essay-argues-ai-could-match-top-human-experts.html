Dwarkesh Patel essay argues AI could match top human experts by 2030

Dwarkesh Patel now expects AIs as good as the best humans at AI research within a couple of years, and superintelligence by 2030.

In a Nov 13, 2026 essay, Dwarkesh Patel says he has moved his timeline for superintelligence from at least the better part of a decade to by 2030, possibly next year. He points to rapid progress in math, where AI solved a Millennium Prize problem in 88 hours. He cites METR, which found Anthropic's progress on well-scoped tasks is 2x what it was a year ago. Anthropic says Claude leads more than 26% of its AI R&D work, up from under 1% in February. OpenAI says its heaviest 10% of researcher users went from around $1,000 a day to over $7,000 a day in coding agent use.
