OpenAI Launches API Credits Benefit for Eligible ChatGPT Subscriptions

OpenAI now gives $100, $200, and $500 monthly ChatGPT subscribers an equivalent quantity of expiring API credits.

OpenAI has launched a new deal for users with specific subscription plans. Subscribers on $100, $200, and $500 per month plans now receive the same quantity of expiring credits to apply to one of their API organizations. This follows a similar benefit announced earlier in the month by Anthropic for their Max and Team subscriptions, where subscribers receive matching credits for the Anthropic API. Simon Willison reported on the update, noting that the included credits may help him ship some public-facing demos since the costs are covered by his existing subscriptions.
