Anthropic posts a Claude-written, Lean-checked proof that VP ≠ VNP; complexity theorist Lance Fortnow calls it the top result of the year

Anthropic released a 70-page paper credited to Claude, with a Lean 4 formalization, claiming to separate the algebraic complexity classes VP and VNP over every field.

Anthropic published a paper and Lean 4 formalization in its formal-math GitHub repository claiming that VP is not equal to VNP over every field, the algebraic analogue of P versus NP posed by Leslie Valiant in the 1970s. The repository says the result was found by an internal research Claude model, that both the paper and the Lean code were written by AI, and that no human mathematician has checked the proof. It also warns that while Lean checks the proofs, no machine checks that the formal statements match the paper's claims, and it maps the two in an appendix. The claimed bound is superpolynomial lower bounds for circuits computing the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial, and for the permanent outside characteristic 2. Lance Fortnow wrote on the Computational Complexity blog that it is the most important complexity result of the year, while stressing that it does not imply a Boolean circuit separation and that P versus NP remains far out of reach. Interesting Engineering notes independent review is still pending.
