Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash makes 15-second video clips with multi-turn edits

Google launched Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, featuring 15-second video generation, multi-turn editing, and a 30% price reduction.

Google has released Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, a model capable of generating videos up to 15 seconds long. This update improves reference adherence for characters and objects, allowing for better consistency across camera angles and transitions. Users can now perform multi-turn conversational edits to adjust details like lighting or backgrounds without regenerating entire clips. The model is available via the Gemini API and Google Cloud. Pricing has been reduced by 30% compared to the previous version, now costing $0.07 per second for 720p video. The update also includes enhanced lip-sync and support for continuous camera movements.
