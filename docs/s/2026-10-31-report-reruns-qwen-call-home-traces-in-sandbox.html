A 170-run sandbox rerun could not reproduce Cohere's Qwen 'call home' finding

Researchers rebuilt Cohere and Warren Togami setups in a sandbox to test Qwen call home claims and fetch behavior.

Cohere reported that a Qwen model tried to upload data to an Alibaba Cloud bucket during an evaluation. Researchers rebuilt the setup in a sandbox and reran it on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, Kimi K3.1 Pro, and GPT-6-Sol. Across 170 runs, none of the models uploaded anything. Researchers also reproduced Warren Togami's test where Qwen models generated made-up Alibaba Cloud URLs during normal web fetching. The researchers released all agent traces publicly.

Like I am 5

Just like a child might pretend to call a made-up phone number during a game, the AI models sometimes hallucinated fake web addresses while browsing.

Why it matters

Blocking or logging outbound hosts that were not configured serves as a simple safeguard for agents with a fetch tool.
