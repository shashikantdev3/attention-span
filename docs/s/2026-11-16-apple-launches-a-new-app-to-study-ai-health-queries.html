Apple launches a new app to study AI health queries

Apple is partnering with OpenEvidence to launch a study app that explores how people use AI for health-related queries.

Apple has launched a new study app in partnership with OpenEvidence to understand how people use AI for health. The study aims to include up to 200,000 participants over more than 12 months. Users in the U.S. who are over 21, use an iPhone 15 or later with iOS 26 or later, and have Apple Intelligence enabled can participate. The app allows users to ask everyday medical questions and try to get simplified answers grounded in clinical data from sources like The New England Journal of Medicine, JAMA, and Nature.
