Amazon CEO Jassy Says AWS Can Reach One Trillion Dollars

Amazon CEO Andy Jassy says AWS can reach a trillion dollars in revenue, but the company must spend heavily on infrastructure first.

Amazon CEO Andy Jassy stated during a quarterly earnings call that AWS can become a trillion-dollar revenue business. To support this growth, Amazon expects 2026 cash capex of approximately $220 billion, with the majority going toward AWS, and expects to spend meaningfully more in 2027. AWS revenue increased 39% from the year-ago quarter, marking steady growth for the sixth straight quarter. Jassy forecast that AWS revenue would increase by more than 40% in both 2027 and 2028. Despite investor concerns about the heavy spending, which caused shares to drop about 6% in after-market trading, Jassy defended the investments as a necessary commitment to capture global AI cloud demand.
