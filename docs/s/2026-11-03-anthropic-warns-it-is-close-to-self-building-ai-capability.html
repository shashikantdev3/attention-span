Anthropic's filing warns it may soon reach its own threshold for AI that speeds up its own development

Anthropic warned in a regulatory filing that it is very close to developing recursive self-improvement capability.

Anthropic revealed in a revised S-1/A filing that it is on the cusp of creating a model capable of recursive self-improvement, also known as RSI. The company cautioned that using AI to build more capable AI could pose safety risks if it accelerates progress faster than security measures. Anthropic stated that none of its current models have crossed the threshold yet, and it plans to keep humans in control of frontier model development for the foreseeable future.
