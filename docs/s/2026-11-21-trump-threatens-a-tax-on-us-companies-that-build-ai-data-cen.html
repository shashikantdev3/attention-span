Trump threatens a tax on US companies that build AI data centers abroad

Trump says American tech firms will face a tremendous tax or penalties if they build AI data centers in other countries, but gave no details.

President Trump said US technology companies that build AI data centers outside the country will face a tremendous tax, the AP reports. Speaking in the White House briefing room on Friday, shortly after returning from Asia, he said the US would not allow its companies to build data centers abroad and that those who try would face tremendous penalties, NBC News reports. Trump gave no details on the size of the tax, which companies it would cover or when it would start, and said he had not yet told his own staff about the plan. NBC reports it is unclear how such a rule would work, and the White House did not immediately respond to a request for comment. Trump also urged communities to accept local data center projects and to demand payments from developers rather than block them, the AP reports.
