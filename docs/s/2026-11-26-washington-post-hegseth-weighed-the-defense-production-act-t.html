Washington Post: Hegseth weighed the Defense Production Act to press Nvidia's board into reinstating Huang; the Pentagon denies it

Three sources told the Post officials discussed using the 1950 law to make Nvidia divert capacity to military work unless Huang returned.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth considered invoking the Defense Production Act of 1950 to pressure Nvidia's board into restoring Jensen Huang as CEO, The Washington Post reported, according to Security Boulevard. Three anonymous sources told the Post that Hegseth and his deputy Steve Feinberg discussed the idea during weekend talks among senior administration officials. One source said officials explored using the law to force Nvidia to shift more manufacturing capacity to military projects unless the board brought Huang back. A Pentagon official denied the account, saying no such conversations took place and no such proposal was made. The report says President Trump and Vice President Vance were involved in the decision, though their exact roles are unclear, and that Trump asked Vance to help broker Huang's return. Nvidia's board reinstated Huang on Monday.
