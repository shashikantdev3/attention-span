Claude solves two open problems in black hole physics

An Anthropic researcher used Claude agents to solve two long-standing open problems in the physics of black holes.

In August, an Anthropic researcher tested Claude's capabilities by asking it to list interesting open problems in theoretical physics. Claude generated 22 candidates, and separate background agents worked on them for about six weeks. The agents successfully solved two problems. First, Claude showed that a phase transition turning hot gas into a black hole is abrupt at tiny scales. Second, Claude Fable 5.2 calculated the precise ceiling for the lightest possible black hole in a simplified theoretical universe and proved it could not be lowered.
