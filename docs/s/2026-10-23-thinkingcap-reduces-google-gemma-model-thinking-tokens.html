ThinkingCap Reduces Google Gemma Model Thinking Tokens

A new ThinkingCap model reduces Google's gemma-4-26B-A4B thinking tokens by an average of 38.9 percent while maintaining accuracy.

Developers released the fifth ThinkingCap model, applying token-efficient methods to Google's gemma-4-26B-A4B-it. Across twelve benchmarks, thinking tokens dropped by an average of 38.9 percent, with an accuracy change of minus 0.15 percentage points. Reductions reached up to 69.1 percent on LongBench v2. The model maintained identical sampling settings and serving stacks using vLLM. Additionally, response looping dropped from 3.6 percent to 0.8 percent. A PRO version of the model achieves up to an 87.6 percent reduction in thinking tokens.

An AI model usually pauses to think for a long time before answering, but this new version learns to give the same smart answers while thinking much less.

Why it matters

Lower inference costs and faster answers are possible at scale by reducing thinking tokens.
