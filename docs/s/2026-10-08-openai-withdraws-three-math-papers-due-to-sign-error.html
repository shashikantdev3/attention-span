OpenAI Withdraws Three Math Papers Due to Sign Error

OpenAI withdrew three mathematics papers and revised 14 other manuscripts after a sign error invalidated a stabilization-trace cancellation argument.

On October 7, 2026, OpenAI withdrew three math manuscripts because a sign error in the paper on Weil classes invalidated a stabilization-trace cancellation argument and the construction used by two dependent papers. The withdrawn papers are Algebraicity of Weil classes on split abelian eightfolds, Algebraicity of Kuga-Satake Correspondences for K3 Surfaces, and The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces. OpenAI also revised 14 other manuscripts with proof repairs, corrected statements, and clearer hypotheses. Additionally, the team added 6 formalizations and 5 other additions, bringing the total percentage of top-line results formalized to 300 out of 719, or about 42 percent.
