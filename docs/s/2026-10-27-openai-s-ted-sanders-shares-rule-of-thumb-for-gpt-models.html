OpenAI's Ted Sanders on picking a GPT model: Astra for quality, Sol, Terra or Luna to save money

OpenAI's Ted Sanders explains how model size, reasoning effort, and multi-agent systems guide model selection based on quality and price.

OpenAI's Ted Sanders has categorized the new GPT-6.1 Terra and explained how the company names its language models. Model performance is multidimensional. A model that is great at math will not automatically be good at jokes. Most performance gaps come down to model size. However, size does not explain everything, and newer smaller models can outperform older larger ones through better training data or improved architectures. Sanders shares a rule of thumb for picking models. Pick Astra for quality only, Sol for price sensitivity, and Terra or Luna for high price sensitivity.

Like I am 5

Choosing an AI model is like picking a toy for a specific job, where a larger toy is not always better at everything, and some smaller tools work better for certain tasks like technical communication.

Why it matters

Selecting the right language model requires balancing model size, reasoning effort, and multi-agent systems against specific quality and price requirements.
