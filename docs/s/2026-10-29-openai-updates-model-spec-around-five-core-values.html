OpenAI rewrites its Model Spec around five core values

OpenAI updated its public Model Spec, organizing its behavioral guidance around five core values.

On October 29, 2026, OpenAI updated its public Model Spec to describe how its models should behave as they take more consequential actions. The update reorganizes guidance around five underlying values: faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care. The revision adds guidance on context, oversight, and agency, adjusts behavioral defaults, and retires much of the detailed style guidance. OpenAI also explores new training methods to help models learn these core values, and plans to share results in future updates.
