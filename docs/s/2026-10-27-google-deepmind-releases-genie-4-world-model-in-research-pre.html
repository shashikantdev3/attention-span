Google DeepMind's Genie 4 world model adds audio, more realistic worlds and real-time steering

Google DeepMind released Genie 4, a new world model featuring real-time audio, realistic simulations, and text prompts.

Google DeepMind released Genie 4 in a research preview for trusted testers. Genie 4 is a new generation of world simulation. The model adds real-time audio, more realistic worlds, and real-time steering. Users can also use mid-simulation text prompts with the system.
