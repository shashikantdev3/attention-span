OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra for fast computer use, at $1 and $5 per million tokens

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Terra, a new model offering faster speeds, lower costs, and enhanced computer-use and visual reasoning capabilities.

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Terra, a fast and efficient model combining visual reasoning, precise computer use, and natural writing. Standard API prices are 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol, priced at $1 per million input tokens, $0.05 per million cached input tokens, and $5 per million output tokens. At medium reasoning effort, Terra scores 15 percentage points higher than GPT-5.6 Terra on Agents' Last Exam at about one-third the cost. The error rate at low reasoning effort fell from 53.6% to 16.7%. It is available now via the OpenAI API as gpt-6.1-terra and in ChatGPT Work and Codex.
