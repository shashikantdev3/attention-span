Reported $11.4M of Nvidia hardware disappears in transit from Oakland to Fort Worth

Six pallets of NVIDIA computer hardware, reportedly worth $11.4 million, vanished on a trip from Oakland, California to Fort Worth, Texas, and police are investigating.

Six pallets of NVIDIA computer hardware, reportedly worth $11.4 million, disappeared during a shipment from California to Texas. A truck picked up the load in Oakland on Aug. 29 for delivery to Fort Worth. Investigators reportedly tracked it through several long stops before losing it in San Bernardino County. Oakland police confirmed a grand theft investigation after a complaint filed Sept. 13, 2026. Police have not confirmed the $11.4 million value or the specific merchandise. They have not named the owner, shipper, carrier or driver, and report no arrests or recovery. NVIDIA has not responded.
