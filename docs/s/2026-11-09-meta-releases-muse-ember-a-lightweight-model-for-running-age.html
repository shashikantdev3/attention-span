Meta releases Muse Ember, a lightweight model for running agent tasks

Meta Superintelligence Lab released Muse Ember, its lightest and most cost-efficient model yet, priced at 10 cents per million input tokens.

Meta Superintelligence Lab released Muse Ember on Monday. Meta calls it the lab's lightest and most cost-efficient model yet. It is built for work agents repeat all day, such as tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long document parsing. At 10 cents per million input tokens, it is cheaper than Anthropic's Haiku and OpenAI's Luna, and cost-competitive with DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM 5.3 Flash. Meta designed it to pair with a larger model like Muse Wonder in an agentic harness. It also handles text, images and audio. Ember is the fourth Muse model.

Like I am 5

Think of a big helper who writes the rules for a game, and a small helper who plays the game over and over using those rules. Ember is the small helper, and it costs less to use.

Why it matters

Teams running agents at high volume get a low-priced model for repeated tasks, which can be paired with a larger model that plans the work.
