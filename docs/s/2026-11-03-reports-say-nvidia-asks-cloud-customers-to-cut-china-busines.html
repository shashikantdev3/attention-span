Nvidia reportedly asks cloud customers to cut back China business

Reports indicate Nvidia has verbally asked some cloud customers to reduce business with Chinese companies and target a diverse customer mix.

Nvidia has reportedly told some cloud compute clients to take on less business from Chinese companies. Sales reps and regional teams communicated the instruction verbally. Sources say clients were told to sell fifty one percent or more of their services to non-Chinese companies. The guidance is meant to encourage a diverse customer mix. The directive is not mandatory and applies to future contracts. Customers are also being asked to disclose the identity of end users accessing the chips. This targets a gap in United States export controls, which restrict chip shipments but not remote access through foreign data centers.
