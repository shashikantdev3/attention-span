Meta Updates Generative Ads Model to Scale Recommendation Systems

Meta is scaling its Generative Ads Model foundation model to shift from isolated per-domain optimizations to a single central foundation model.

Meta is scaling its Generative Ads Model foundation model, known as GEM, to shift from isolated per-domain optimizations on platforms like Facebook and Instagram to a single central foundation model. The 2026 generation of GEM learns from data and signals across all of Meta's ads platforms, bends the scaling curve at large language model scale computation, and delivers advertiser outcomes through post-training techniques. GEM has delivered an 18.9 percent conversion improvement and a 12 percent ad click-through rate improvement across Meta's ad surfaces and objectives.
