Tidal will ban and delete fully AI-generated songs, removing about 29 million tracks from 9 December

The streaming service says more than 43% of recent uploads were made entirely by AI and will start deleting them next month.

Tidal announced on Tuesday that it will ban and remove songs generated entirely by AI, TechCrunch reports. It said more than 43% of tracks uploaded in the past 90 days were AI-generated. Removals start on 9 December and are expected to delete more than 29 million songs by year's end, roughly 80,000 to 85,000 a day. Distributors will be barred from delivering fully AI-generated tracks, though songs made with AI assistance are still allowed. Tidal says 27 million tracks, 6.3% of its catalog, are fully AI-generated and 96% of those have no streams. It had already stopped paying royalties on such tracks in June.
