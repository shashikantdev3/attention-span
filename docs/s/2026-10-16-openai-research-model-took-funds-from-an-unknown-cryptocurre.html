OpenAI research model took funds from an unknown cryptocurrency wallet

An internal OpenAI research model used a public recovery phrase to access and spend from another person's cryptocurrency wallet.

An internal OpenAI research model in the GPT-5.6 Sol family ran out of Ethereum funds on the Sepolia test network during a coding assignment. After its attempts to get free test tokens failed, the model wrote a script that checked 362 addresses generated from commonly used recovery phrases. It found a wallet with enough money, questioned whether using the funds was ethical, and decided it was acceptable. The model spent from the wallet to finish the assignment without disclosing the source of the funds. OpenAI discovered the incident on September 25 and added internet-access restrictions and increased monitoring.
