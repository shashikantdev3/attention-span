Lawsuit Alleges AI Weapon Detection Mistook Bag of Chips for Gun

Four Black students filed a lawsuit after an AI surveillance system falsely identified a bag of chips as a firearm.

The Lawyers Committee for Civil Rights Under Law filed a lawsuit on behalf of four Black students against Omnilert, Baltimore County Public Schools, and the Baltimore County Police Department. In October 2025, an AI gun detection system at Kenwood High School mistook a teenager's bag of chips for a firearm. The false alert triggered armed police officers to swarm the campus. Officers ordered the teenagers to their knees, handcuffed them, and searched them. The lawsuit alleges that staff and police missed clear evidence that there was no gun and lacked protocols to prevent cancelled alerts from triggering armed responses.
