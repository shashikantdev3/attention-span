Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella Predicts AI Agents Will Reshuffle Web Economics

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella predicts that AI agents will reshape internet economics, impacting advertising business models and human web traffic.

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella spoke at The New York Times DealBook Summit on Wednesday. He predicted that the rise of AI agents will reshape the economics of the internet. This shift will impact advertising business models and human web traffic. Companies that currently monetize ads based on eyeballs may need to transition to cost-per-click or transactional models. Nadella also discussed Amazon and its decision to block AI agents from accessing its retail website. He suggested that companies trying to protect old business models could lose out to platforms like Shopify that embrace AI agents.
