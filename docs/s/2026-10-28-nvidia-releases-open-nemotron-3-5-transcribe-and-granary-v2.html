Nvidia Releases Open Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe and Granary v2

Nvidia has released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open speech recognition model, alongside the Granary v2 speech dataset.

Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open speech recognition model that handles transcription for 40 languages, speaker recognition, and translation. The model takes its instructions as text prompts and leads several benchmarks while using only a sixth of the active parameters of its strongest open rival. Nvidia is also shipping a dataset named Granary v2 with more than 800,000 hours of speech, along with tools for fine-tuning.

Like I am 5

Nvidia made a new computer ear that can write down spoken words, recognize who is talking, and translate languages when you tell it what to do. They also shared a huge library of spoken audio files to help train similar tools.

Why it matters

Speech recognition models can be built with higher efficiency and adapted using large open datasets.
