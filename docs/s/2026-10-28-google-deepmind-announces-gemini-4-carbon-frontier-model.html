Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, now #1 on Artificial Analysis, with top cybersecurity scores

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its strongest model featuring state-of-the-art cybersecurity and long horizon capabilities.

Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon as its strongest frontier model, ranking number one on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. The model delivers advanced cybersecurity capabilities across vulnerability detection, exploitation, and patching, scoring 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, 54.8% on ExploitGym, and 71.0% on CWE-bench. Designed for complex, multi-step engineering tasks, Carbon supports sustained agentic workflows. It launches at the same introductory price as Argon, costing $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens priced at 95% off. Google is rolling out the model starting with a subset of cyber defenders.
