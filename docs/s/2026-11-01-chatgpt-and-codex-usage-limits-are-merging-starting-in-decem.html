ChatGPT and Codex will share one usage limit from December

Plus, Pro, and Business plans will share a usage allowance across ChatGPT and Codex starting in December.

Starting in December, Plus, Pro, and Business plans will share a usage allowance across ChatGPT and Codex. This change happens as agentic capabilities of Work come into the main ChatGPT experience. Usage for ChatGPT and Codex will be measured in tokens. How much allowance you use generally depends on the chosen model and the task. Instant chat will not draw from usage. Users will see combined remaining usage and reset times in Settings, then Usage. If a limit is reached, users can wait for a reset, upgrade the plan, or buy credits.

Like I am 5

You will share one big bucket of allowance for two different toys instead of having two separate buckets. Just like sharing snacks, how fast you finish them depends on how big the snack is and what you do with it.

Why it matters

Combined token limits across tools change how developers budget and plan their workflows.
