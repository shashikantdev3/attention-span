NVIDIA Releases Nemotron 3.5 Super VL and Bolt Models

NVIDIA released the open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model and two new edge models for enterprise and local agentic workloads.

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, a 120-billion-parameter open model built in collaboration with the Nemotron Coalition. It delivers high accuracy on multimodal agentic tasks like instruction following, tool use, and visual understanding. NVIDIA also released two edge models: Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B 24GB for local workloads and Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B 4GB for conversational agents. Super VL and Bolt 74B-A9B are available now, and Bolt E4B launches on October 23. Dozens of organizations are customizing Super VL for finance, cybersecurity, and scientific discovery.
