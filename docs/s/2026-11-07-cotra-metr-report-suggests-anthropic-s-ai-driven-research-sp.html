Cotra: METR report suggests Anthropic's AI-driven research speedup has crossed its own threshold

Ajeya Cotra says a public METR report implies Anthropic has crossed its 'dramatic acceleration' threshold, with capabilities progress about twice as fast as the year before.

Ajeya Cotra discusses a public METR report on AI R&D acceleration at Anthropic. Anthropic stated in its S1 that it "may imminently meet" its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for dramatically accelerated progress. The report authors found that between October 2025 and October 2026, capabilities progress at Anthropic ran about twice as fast as over the previous twelve months, measured by an internal index called AECI. Much supporting evidence was redacted. Cotra reasons that researcher labor must be sped up by roughly 10x, and notes Anthropic staff self-report about a 12x uplift. These are her estimates, and she calls them very uncertain.
