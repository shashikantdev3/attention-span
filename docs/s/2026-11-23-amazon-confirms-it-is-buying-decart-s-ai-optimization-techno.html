Amazon confirms it is buying Decart's AI optimization technology and team; Bloomberg puts the price at about $7.5 billion

Amazon announced it will buy the technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack and hire Decart's founders, confirming last week's reports of a deal.

Amazon said it will purchase the technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack (DOS) and bring on Decart's three co-founders and their team of AI researchers and engineers. Amazon did not disclose a price, but TechCrunch and The Register cite Bloomberg as reporting the deal is worth about $7.5 billion. The Register says DOS sits between models and chips, tuning kernels, compilers and runtimes so hardware stays busy, and works on Nvidia GPUs, Google TPUs and AWS Trainium. Amazon says the technology will help optimize inference and its custom silicon so customers can train and run models faster and more cheaply. The Register reports this is an asset purchase rather than a full acquisition, and that Decart's Oasis world model and Lucy video model are not included. TechCrunch notes Amazon is preparing to sell Trainium chips to outside customers as an alternative to Nvidia. The deal still needs regulatory clearance; what is new since the FT and Calcalist reports last week is Amazon's own announcement.
