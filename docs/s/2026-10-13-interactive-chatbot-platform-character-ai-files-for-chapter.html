Interactive Chatbot Platform Character.AI Files for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy

Interactive chatbot platform Character.AI has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and agreed to sell assets to Disney.

Just five years after its founding, interactive chatbot platform Character.AI has filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. The startup reported assets and liabilities between $50 million and $100 million. Character.AI has agreed to sell some of its assets to Disney for $20 million along with a non-exclusive perpetual license for its technology. Disney will also provide transition services via former Character.AI employees. The bankruptcy follows legal, regulatory, and operational headwinds, including lawsuits regarding safety and child privacy investigations. If an actionable purchase proposal is not reached during the court-supervised sale process, the platform will be wound down.
