How OpenAI scaled Azure Cosmos DB to serve over 1 billion ChatGPT users

OpenAI scaled its online storage platform, Habitat, using Azure Cosmos DB to manage explosive growth for over 1 billion ChatGPT users.

OpenAI explains how it scaled its online storage platform, Habitat, to serve over 1 billion ChatGPT users across 40 geographic regions. Habitat evolved into a globally distributed storage platform serving over 500 petabytes of data. The engineering team worked closely with Azure to scale the underlying storage component, Azure Cosmos DB. They arrived at a viable multi-tenancy approach of co-managed containers with excellent read performance at massive scale. The post details their journey through single-tenancy and multi-tenancy, managing logical partition key limits, and handling noisy-neighbor issues.
