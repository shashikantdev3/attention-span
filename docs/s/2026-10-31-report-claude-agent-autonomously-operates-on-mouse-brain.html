Exin Therapeutics says a Claude Sonnet 5 agent chose and delivered a gene therapy to a mouse

An AI agent running on Claude Sonnet 5 chose a gene therapy and performed brain surgery on a mouse.

An AI agent running on Claude Sonnet 5 picked a drug, a brain region, and injection coordinates for brain surgery on a mouse. It then controlled the procedure through a motorized surgical rig. According to Gabriel Ocana-Santero of Exin Therapeutics, this is the first time an AI agent has both chosen a treatment and delivered it to a living mammal. Mid-surgery, the agent detected that its target position was outside the physical range of motion due to manual adjustments and refused to continue. The mouse recovered well according to Ocana-Santero. The results have not been peer-reviewed.

Why it matters

Autonomous agents connected to laboratory hardware can execute end-to-end biological experiments, but alignment guardrails can halt live procedures when physical errors occur.
