Elon Musk throttles Grok Bot limits, citing high costs

SpaceX throttled usage limits for Grok Bot over Halloween weekend because the serving costs were unsustainably high relative to revenue.

Over Halloween weekend, Grok Bot users found their weekly usage limits unexpectedly maxed out, even if they had not used the AI overnight. Some users reported jumps from 50% to 100%. SpaceX CEO Elon Musk confirmed on X that this was not a glitch. He stated that the cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue. SpaceX recently reported that AI makes up 52% of company income, and its renamed Super Intelligence division turned a profit of $431 million, offset by $1.2 billion of one-time costs related to its acquisition of Cursor. Musk has not shared how subscriber limits will be impacted moving forward.
