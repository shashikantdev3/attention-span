Report claims six Chinese AI models show fingerprints of export-controlled Blackwell GPUs

A forensic analysis by Will DePue claims six models from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group carry numerical patterns matching NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs.

Will DePue of Unemployment Labs analysed published model weights. He reports that six models released since April 2026 from DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, MiniMax and Ant Group match Blackwell hardware, while earlier models match the older Hopper generation. For DeepSeek-V4-Flash and Kimi K3, the match was also confirmed on physical Blackwell chips. The author says this supports, without proving, that Blackwell did the training. It does not show where the chips were, how access was obtained, or whether export controls were broken. The author estimates each run needed roughly 1,500 to 3,500 Blackwell GPUs.
