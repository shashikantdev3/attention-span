Satya Nadella Says to Assume Every AI Model Is Compromised

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella wrote that we must assume all AI models are compromised and add an emergency brake to stop them.

In posts on Saturday, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella stated that we must assume all artificial intelligence models are compromised from the start. He called for putting an emergency brake on artificial intelligence. Nadella wrote that it is time to assess the trust architecture of artificial intelligence. He wants to move away from black boxes where we simply accept advice. He called for transparent systems with containment, independent audits, timely incident disclosure, and tamper-proof human readable evidence. He also said authorized people must be able to pause or shut down a model mid-task.
