How OpenAI turned its coding tool into dots, an always-on personal assistant

OpenAI launched dots, an always-on personal assistant built from its agentic coding tools, available in desktop and mobile apps for Pro subscribers.

OpenAI released dots at its developer day in late September. Alexander Embiricos leads OpenAI's enterprise product team. The assistant is available in ChatGPT desktop and mobile apps for subscribers on the Pro tier or higher, with plans starting at $100 a month. It connects to calendars, email, and computers to help users work. The assistant uses proactive research to think about what it knows and what it can access to propose helpful actions. OpenAI says the agent has the same privacy controls as ChatGPT, though controls are not granular and the app refuses read-only access to cloud drives.
