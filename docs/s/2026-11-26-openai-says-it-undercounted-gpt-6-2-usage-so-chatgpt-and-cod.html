OpenAI says it undercounted GPT-6.2 usage, so ChatGPT and Codex limits now run out up to 40% sooner

Astra users hit limits 40% faster and Sol users 25% faster; OpenAI is giving paid accounts one banked reset and will move to a shared ChatGPT and Codex allowance in December.

OpenAI says it incorrectly discounted usage of GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol for most users when the models launched, Notebookcheck reports. Thibault Sottiaux of OpenAI announced the fix on November 26, saying the same work will now use more of a plan's allowance. Compared with the previous week, users reach their limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol, while other models are unaffected and OpenAI will not charge for past usage. As compensation, each Go, Plus, Pro and Business account gets one banked reset. A day earlier TechCrunch reported that OpenAI had begun putting a small group of Pro subscribers on a single meter shared by Chat and Work, with Instant conversations still unlimited. An OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch most users should not see more frequent resets but a small share of heavy users may hit limits sooner, and the shared allowance is due to reach more plans in December.
