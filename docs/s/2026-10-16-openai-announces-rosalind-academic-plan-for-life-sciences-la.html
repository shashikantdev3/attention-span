OpenAI Announces Rosalind Academic Plan for Life Sciences Labs

OpenAI introduced the Rosalind Academic Plan to offer eligible academic labs sponsored access to GPT-Rosalind and life sciences tools.

OpenAI introduced the Rosalind Academic Plan to help academic scientists access GPT-Rosalind. The model combines frontier reasoning with the Rosalind Workbench to enable specialized tool orchestration across medicinal chemistry, genomics, and wet-lab assistance. Eligible labs can receive up to 30 Business Standard seats at zero cost for up to 12 months. Access to GPT-Rosalind is subject to trusted-access approval. Research data shared through the plan is not used to train models by default. Approved researchers can use the model for drug and protein design, among other scientific applications.
