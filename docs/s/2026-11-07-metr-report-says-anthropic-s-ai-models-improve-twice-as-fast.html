METR report says Anthropic's AI models improve twice as fast, with heavy redactions

A METR report says Anthropic's models are improving roughly twice as quickly as a year ago, with AI driving most of the speedup.

AI safety group METR published a report on Thursday after an 11-week investigation inside Anthropic. It says Anthropic's models are improving roughly twice as quickly as a year ago. METR says it is confident the gain is not just more training compute, and that most of the acceleration as of October is due to AI. Employees self-reported being around 12 times as productive with the company's most capable internal model. Anthropic redacted a majority of the evidence METR found most informative from the public version. METR says the public has to rely on its judgment more than would be ideal.
