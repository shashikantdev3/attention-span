Four Gemini 4 Argon Agents Beat Minecraft Without Help

Four AI agents running on Google's Gemini 4 Argon have beaten Minecraft for the first time without human input.

Four AI agents running on Google's Gemini 4 Argon beat Minecraft without human input. They accomplished a fully autonomous run by slaying the Ender Dragon. The Google Deepmind team dropped them into a randomly generated world without assigning roles, strategies, or game-specific tools. The agents split up tasks, warned each other about threats, recovered items from dead teammates, and logged mistakes in a shared memory. Over 27 hours and 14 minutes, they wrote 2,783 JavaScript programs, exchanged 229 chat messages, and died 605 times. The session cost $1,620 at the standard price taking effect in 2027.
