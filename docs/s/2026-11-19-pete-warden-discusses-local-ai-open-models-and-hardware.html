Pete Warden Discusses Local AI, Open Models, and Hardware

Deep learning engineer Pete Warden joins a podcast to discuss local artificial intelligence, open-weight models, and voice interfaces.

Pete Warden, co-creator of Moonshine Voice and a deep learning engineer, appears on a podcast hosted by Ben Lorica. The conversation covers why artificial intelligence increasingly runs locally rather than in the cloud. They discuss the progress of open-weight models, running artificial intelligence on laptops and in browsers, and low-latency speech models. Additional topics include the economics of local artificial intelligence, specialized versus end-to-end models, and emerging hardware approaches that could challenge GPU-centric infrastructure. Warden compares the current state of local models to the early days of open-source projects and Linux.
