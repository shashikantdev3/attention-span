Karpathy says he built HackerTube in a weekend with Claude

Andrej Karpathy says he used Claude Opus 5.5 to turn 43 million Hacker News comments into 6,454 curated videos, beating YouTube's recommendations.

Andrej Karpathy built HackerTube over a weekend using Claude's Opus 5.5 model. Claude filtered 43 million comments from 20 years of Hacker News and distilled them into 6,454 videos that keep getting recommended there. Karpathy writes on X that the result is a "significantly better viewing experience" than YouTube's own recommendations. The site needs no login, shows a single mid-video frame instead of optimized thumbnails, and stores state locally in the browser. He then built TristanTube with Claude Code, with 47,000 videos drawn from a YouTube channel list by Tristan Hume. Karpathy says Claude Code did nearly all the work.
