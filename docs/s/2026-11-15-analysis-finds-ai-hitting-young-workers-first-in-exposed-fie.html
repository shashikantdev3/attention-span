Analysis Finds AI Hitting Young Workers First in Exposed Fields

An analysis for Australia's central bank and studies from Stanford and Anthropic indicate that AI is impacting young entry-level workers first.

An analysis by Mike Major for Australia's central bank indicates that young workers entering highly exposed fields like software development and customer service are facing weaker employment rates and higher unemployment since ChatGPT launched. Job postings dropped across groups, with the steepest declines in high-exposure areas. Similar patterns appeared in a Stanford study of US payroll records and an Anthropic study on job-finding rates. Researchers caution that factors like post-pandemic shifts and interest rate policies could also play a role, and direct causation is not established.
