Oracle Files to Sell $20 Billion in Stock and Bonds

Oracle filed plans to issue $10 billion in stock and $10 billion in bonds to finance its artificial intelligence buildout.

Oracle filed plans with the Securities and Exchange Commission to issue $10 billion in common stock and $10 billion in senior notes. The company plans to use the proceeds for general corporate purposes, which may include capital expenditures, debt repayments, and future investments. Oracle outlined these fundraising plans during its investor day, where it also issued a revenue forecast of at least $225 billion for fiscal 2030. Oracle shares fell 8.8% on Monday following the filing. Co-founded by Larry Ellison, Oracle holds $125.34 billion in debt and faces growing investor concern over the massive amount of debt used to fund AI infrastructure.
