The week in AI: 23 to 29 November 2026

The stories that mattered most, from 3 daily editions and 31 stories.

  1. Nvidia's board reverses course and reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO, ten days after his resignation

    Nvidia's board voted unanimously to bring Jensen Huang back as president and CEO, saying senior US officials gave input and it sees no national security risk.

  2. Anthropic posts a Claude-written, Lean-checked proof that VP ≠ VNP; complexity theorist Lance Fortnow calls it the top result of the year

    Anthropic released a 70-page paper credited to Claude, with a Lean 4 formalization, claiming to separate the algebraic complexity classes VP and VNP over every field.

  3. OpenAI posts a model-written preprint claiming the matrix multiplication exponent is exactly 9/4

    A new manuscript in OpenAI's public math repository claims to settle how fast matrices can be multiplied, with part of the result checked in Lean.

  4. AI stocks slide again: Nvidia gives back part of its Huang rally and Anthropic falls below its $83 IPO price

    A day after Nvidia jumped more than 30% on Jensen Huang's return, chip and AI stocks fell sharply and Anthropic traded below its IPO price for the first time.

  5. Amazon confirms it is buying Decart's AI optimization technology and team; Bloomberg puts the price at about $7.5 billion

    Amazon announced it will buy the technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack and hire Decart's founders, confirming last week's reports of a deal.

  6. Virginia and Oregon sue SpaceX, X and xAI over sexualized Grok images, the first state lawsuits over the chatbot

    The two attorneys general filed coordinated consumer protection suits alleging the companies let Grok create non-consensual and child sexual images while marketing it as safe.

  7. Anthropic launches Claude Ship, agents that review, fix and can approve GitHub pull requests

    Claude Ship bundles Code Review with two beta agents, PR Steward and Claude Approvals, for Team and Enterprise customers.
